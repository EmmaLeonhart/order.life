Ancestor Days

The births and deaths of the founder's ancestors, on the days of the Gaian year.

{{ t.nav.home }} › Ancestors

A Gaian date falls on the same weekday every year, so an ancestor's day comes round unchanged. Each day of the year has a page here, the seven days of Horus included, and each day's entry in the calendar feed links to it. Only dates known to the day are placed; the living are left out.
