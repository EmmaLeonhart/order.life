How to subscribe

Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar on the web.
  2. In the left sidebar, next to Other calendars, click + → From URL.
  3. Paste the feed URL above and click Add calendar.

Apple Calendar (macOS / iOS)

  1. Click the Subscribe button above — Apple Calendar recognises the webcal:// link directly.
  2. Or, in Calendar: File → New Calendar Subscription (macOS) and paste the feed URL.
  3. On iOS: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste the URL.

Outlook

  1. Open Outlook on the web.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the feed URL, give it a name, and click Import.

Subscribed calendars refresh on their app's own schedule (typically every few hours to once a day) — exact timing is controlled by your calendar app, not by this site.
