# Chapter 344: Instead of Trust

No holiday is named for this day.
The leftover note on an older expansion of the mind
Is not this page.

In March a house in {{p|New York}} was agreed,
With the reserve in the deal.
The closing is not that March.
The agreement is not this September.
The price is not taken from one sentence.

On Sunday, the seventh of September, 2008,
The two mortgage houses were taken in.
One chronology says a guarantee
Of a hundred billion for each.
The chapter does not audit the guarantee.

On Monday, the fifteenth,
In the early morning,
Another house filed.
It is called the largest filing to that day.
The size is not one number.
The same weekend a third house was sold to a bank.
One chronology prices that sale at fifty billion.
The chapter does not audit the price.
Over the weekend the secretary said
There would be no public money for a buyer.
A paper of December says the reserve lent,
On the Monday and again on the Tuesday,
To a piece of the house that had filed.
The refusal and the loan are not one policy.

On Tuesday the insurer was not allowed to fail.
The first credit is given as eighty-five billion.
Later credits are not that Tuesday.

On Monday, the twenty-ninth,
{{x|The House}} refused the plan,
Two hundred twenty-eight to two hundred five.
The fall of the index that day
Is not one number in the papers.
None is chosen.
On Wednesday, the first of October,
{{x|The Senate}} passed a changed bill,
Seventy-four to twenty-five.
On Friday, the third,
{{x|The House}} passed that bill,
Two hundred sixty-three to one hundred seventy-one.
{{c|George W. Bush}} signed it the same day.
The law authorizes up to seven hundred billion.
The signed line lets the available sum begin
At two hundred fifty,
With room, on further steps, to seven hundred.
The first sum is not the whole.

On Friday, the thirty-first of October,
A note went to a list of people who worked on codes.
The name on the note is {{c|Satoshi Nakamoto}}.
Who held the name is not known.
The note says a system of cash,
Peer to peer, with no trusted third party.
The paper is called
{{x|Bitcoin}}: {{x|A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System}}.
The paper says a proof in the place of trust.
The note and the paper are not one sentence.
The way of the proof is not set down.
This Friday is after the law.
It is not the law.

On Saturday, the third of January, 2009,
The first block was made.
In it is a line from the paper of that morning,
In {{p|London}}:
{{x|The Times}}, the third of January, 2009,
Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks.
The chancellor is not the American secretary.
The bailout in the line is not the law of October.
The line shows the block was not made before that morning.
What else the line was for is not agreed.
The first reward cannot be spent.
The accounts do not agree whether that was meant.
The next block is six days later.
Why the gap is that long is not agreed.
The offer of the program to other people
Is not this Saturday.
A payment to a second person is not this Saturday.
A price in a market is not this January.
A price paid for a pizza is not this year.

The absurdity is a Monday when a house is allowed to fail,
And a Tuesday when an insurer is not,
And a Friday paper that says the payment
Needs no trusted party,
And a Saturday block that carries a headline
About a second bailout.
The striving is to keep the protocol
From being taken for the repair,
And the failure of the houses
From being taken for a proof
That no debt can be trusted to anyone,
And to hold the people who lost the houses
Apart from the law and apart from the block.
The counts are not chosen.
The way is not set down.
