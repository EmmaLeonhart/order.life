# Chapter 330: The Source Book

A holy day: the reading of the book

Clock from October first, the 'ninety day
The fiscal year began; fifteen years' clock;
The plan, a worldwide effort, and the way
Of other countries organizing; stock

The five years ask no whole book: first, a chart
Of human inheritance, marks along
The chromosomes, some two to five apart,
Centimorgans; each mark a short run, strong;

A centimorgan counts how readily
Two marks are split from parent unto child;
A physical map: marks spaced evenly,
A hundred thousand letters; then, compiled,

A method: fifty cents a letter; ten
Million letters of human DNA,
In stretches long; the mouse's map; and then
Some twenty million more, the others' way.

The plan said fifty, hundred thousand genes;
An estimate, not count; and it would aid
To understand, and treat, by later means,
Four thousand genetic diseases made;

It calls the information the source book
Of biomedicine, the century
To come; this book will keep that day, and look
On reading genes with hope, not fear; set free.

Two hundred million dollars, near, a year,
For fifteen years, the council's figure, named;
From 'eighty-eight to 'ninety: getting clear,
Its organizing; energy's work, framed

To weigh what radiation does to health,
And energy's chemicals; institutes,
The people's health, and their genetic wealth
Already funded. {{p|Santa Cruz}}, the roots:

In 'eighty-five, the twenty-fourth of May,
{{c|4470|Robert Sinsheimer}}, the chancellor, had planned
A workshop, Friday into Sunday; they
Had a gift, thirty-six million, in hand,

Meant for a telescope, to be returned;
He thought the money might found, for the race,
An institute to read; unfunded, spurned;
The question changed: could, then should; in its place

The council, 'eighty-eight, advised the deed,
With new money, not taken from the old;

Not yet a safeguard. But, this book's own side:

A reading of the genes is life's own gift;
It lets us heal, and better what we hand
Our children; and the source book, set adrift
No longer, is the thread on which we stand;

The marks are how a child's piece can be traced,
The copy, once called possible, the road
The letters travel; and this feast is placed
On the first of October: holy, owed.

Ten million letters, five years' aim; the whole
The book, a term, that morning did not reach;
Now it is read: the body's sacred scroll,
The thread of all descent, and taught to each.
