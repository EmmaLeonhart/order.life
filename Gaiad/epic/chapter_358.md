# Chapter 358: The Longest Night

No holiday is named for this day.

In 2022 {{c|Emma Leonhart}} turned twenty-six, in her second year at the college in {{p|Kelowna}}.
It was the year the war of the chapter of 2008 became the war of the continent,
The year a machine that writes was opened to everyone,
And the year the young man of the movement, who had meant to earn to give,
Was found to have given away what was not his.
She had written with a machine before most people had.
Before the chat came, she had written with an earlier tool for writers, called {{x|Sudowrite}},
Stories set in the world of this book.
It was her first writing with a machine.
A first writing with a machine is not a book written by one.
She chose what stayed. Choosing what stays is the writing.
Within a month of the chat's opening she was one of the million,
Asking it to make plain the pages of her faith.
Being one of the million is not understanding it.
She said so later herself.

## The special operation

Before dawn on Thursday the twenty-fourth of February
{{c|Vladimir Putin}} announced what he called a special military operation.
A name is not a thing.
The columns crossed into {{p|Ukraine}} from the north, the east, and the south,
And the missiles reached {{p|Kyiv}}.
The capital was expected to fall in days. Expected is not done.
{{c|Volodymyr Zelenskyy}} stayed in the city and filmed himself in the street at night, saying, we are here.
He was offered a way out, and said he needed ammunition, not a ride.
At the end of March the Russian forces left the north.
In {{p|Bucha}} the dead were found in the streets, some with their hands tied,
And a town's name became a word for what was done there.
In {{p|Mariupol}}, on the sixteenth of March, the theater where hundreds sheltered,
With the word children written on the ground outside in letters seen from the sky,
Was bombed.
The defenders held the steelworks of {{x|Azovstal}} until May.
In September {{p|Ukraine}} took back most of the province of {{p|Kharkiv}} in days,
And in November the city of {{p|Kherson}}.
{{p|Russia}} called up three hundred thousand men, and many fled to {{p|Georgia}} and {{p|Kazakhstan}},
And it declared four provinces of {{p|Ukraine}} its own, in votes held under guns, as in {{p|Crimea}} in the chapter of 2014.
On the twenty-sixth of September the pipelines under the {{x|Baltic}} that carried its gas to {{p|Germany}}
Were blown apart, and who did it is argued still.
Millions of Ukrainians fled west, and were taken in.
War was in {{p|Europe}} again, of the size the continent had sworn after 1945 not to have again.
A vow is not a wall.

## The machine that writes

In April the lab named open showed a machine that drew any picture from a sentence,
And in the summer others, {{x|Midjourney}} and {{x|Stable Diffusion}}, did the same,
Trained on the pictures of artists who had not been asked,
And the artists found their own styles coming back to them from the machines, unsigned.
On Wednesday the thirtieth of November, in {{p|San Francisco}},
The lab released a chat with a machine that writes.
In five days a million people had tried it.
A million trying is not a million understanding.
It wrote a letter, a poem, a lie, an answer, in the same even voice.
The even voice is not the truth. It is the likely next word, again and again.
Within two months it had a hundred million users, faster than anything before.
The machine built of attention of the chapter of 2017 had become the thing everyone attended to.

## The villain

In the movement of the chapter of 2015, the young man's exchange had grown to be worth thirty-two billion dollars,
And he had become its most famous giver,
And in February it founded a fund for the far future, with the philosopher of that chapter among its advisers,
And gave away more than a hundred million dollars,
And put five hundred million into the company of safety of the chapter before.
In August the philosopher published a book called {{x|What We Owe the Future}},
And it was on the covers of the magazines.
On the second of November a paper printed a page of the accounts of the young man's trading firm,
Showing that it held mostly the exchange's own coin.
On the sixth the head of a rival exchange said he would sell his,
And the customers of the young man's exchange ran to take their money out,
And the money was not there.
About eight billion dollars of the customers' money had been lent to his trading firm,
And spent, on bets, and buildings, and politicians, and the causes of the movement.
On the eleventh of November the exchange filed for bankruptcy.
On the twelfth of December he was arrested in the {{p|Bahamas}}.
The next year a jury found him guilty of fraud,
And he was sentenced to twenty-five years.
The movement's leaders said they had been deceived, and many had been.
Some had been warned, and had not looked, because the money was good.
This book calls him a villain, because he is one.
It does not call the movement his.
The arithmetic of good of the chapter of 2015 was not the fraud.
A movement that counts everything must also count who it takes money from.
It did not, and it paid for it, and so did the customers, who had not chosen to give anything.
The worry about the machines that the rationalists had raised before almost anyone
Was not made false by the man who funded part of it.

## The court and the rights

On the twenty-fourth of June the {{x|Supreme Court}} of the {{p|United States}}, in {{x|Dobbs}},
Overturned the right to end a pregnancy that it had found in 1973,
And within weeks the states of half the country banned it, or nearly.
In the same year the states passed laws against people like the woman on the magazine of the chapter of 2015
By the dozen: against their care, their sports, their names in schools, their bathrooms.
On the nineteenth of November a man walked into {{x|Club Q}}, a gay bar in {{p|Colorado Springs}},
And killed five people before a soldier and a drag performer beat him down.
On the sixteenth of September, in {{p|Tehran}}, a young Kurdish woman, {{c|Mahsa Amini}},
Arrested for showing her hair, died in the custody of the morality police,
And the women of {{p|Iran}} took off their scarves in the streets and burned them,
Under the words woman, life, freedom,
And hundreds were killed, and some were hanged.

## The killings in May

On the fourteenth of May a young man who believed the great replacement of the chapter of 2017
Drove to a supermarket in a black neighborhood of {{p|Buffalo}} and killed ten people,
Streaming it.
On the twenty-fourth of May a young man walked into an elementary school in {{p|Uvalde}}, in {{p|Texas}},
And killed nineteen children and two teachers,
While nearly four hundred police waited in the hallway for more than an hour.

## The deaths

On the eighth of July {{c|Shinzo Abe}}, who had led {{p|Japan}} longer than anyone,
Was shot in the street in {{p|Nara}} while giving a speech,
By a man whose mother had given all the family's money to a church that Abe had praised.

And millions queued for miles along the {{x|Thames}} to pass her coffin.

Thirty-three years after a ruler had called for his death, and lived, and lost an eye.

## The longest night

On Sunday the twenty-fifth of December many in {{p|Ukraine}} kept Christmas on that day,
And not only in January by the older reckoning,
And the next year the state moved the holiday.
Moving a date is not moving a faith. It is moving away from a neighbor's calendar.
A calendar is a side taken.
On the longest night the families of {{p|China}} eat dumplings, or rice balls by the south's custom.
The light returns. The returning light is not peace. It is only the turning of the year.
