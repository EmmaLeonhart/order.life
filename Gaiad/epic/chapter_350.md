# Chapter 350: The Borrowed Robe

This is the Ides of {{x|Ophiuchus}}.
The ides are the middle of the month.
The middle is not a turning.
But this year is.

In 2014 {{c|Emma Leonhart}} turned eighteen,
And moved house for the third time.
In her words, she moved houses in 2002, in 2008, and in 2014.
In September she began at the university, {{x|UBC Okanagan}}, in {{p|Kelowna}},
And stayed five years.
And she went online.
In her words, she was pretty offline until she started university.
So the net she entered at eighteen was the net of this year,
And this year, she has said, is the year of fascism.
In her words, 2014 is a really big year of fascism and stuff like that,
And that is essentially what 2014 is defined by.
This chapter takes her at her word.
It was the year the old robes were put back on:
A caliph's robe in {{p|Mosul}}, an empire's map in {{p|Crimea}},
A nation's blood-and-soil in the elections of {{p|Europe}},
And on the net a mob that learned it could choose a woman and destroy her,
And called itself a movement about ethics.
She arrived on the net in the year the net learned that.

## The borrowed robe

On the third of March, 1924, in {{p|Ankara}}, the assembly of {{p|Turkey}} had abolished the caliphate.
Ninety years is not long for a word.
On Tuesday the tenth of June, 2014, {{p|Mosul}} fell
To a group that called itself a state.
The army that held the city left, and left its weapons.
On Sunday the twenty-ninth of June, the first day of the fast,
Its spokesman declared a caliphate.
The scholars of most of the world's Muslims did not accept it.
Not accepting is not stopping.
On Friday the fourth of July, in the {{x|Great Mosque of al-Nuri}},
{{c|Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi}} gave the sermon in a black robe and turban
That recalled the {{x|Abbasid}}s.
Recalling is not descending.
A pulpit is not a lineage.
The group struck coins, and wrote rules of dress and punishment,
And said they were the first century's.
Most of what it called old was made in the printing of that year.
An invented tradition is not made less dangerous by being invented.

On Sunday the third of August it came to {{p|Sinjar}}, where the {{x|Yazidi}}s live.
The men were killed.
The women and girls were taken and sold.
Tens of thousands fled up the mountain and were trapped there without water.
A people with no book, the group said, has no protection.
A people with no book is not a people with no tradition.
The {{x|Yazidi}}s' tradition is older than the group's caliph.
On the nineteenth of August it published a video of the killing of {{c|James Foley}}, an American reporter,
And then of others, in orange, kneeling in the desert,
Made for the screens of young men in the countries that would bomb them.
In the next summer it killed {{c|Khaled al-Asaad}}, who had kept the ruins of {{p|Palmyra}} for decades,
And blew up the temple of {{x|Bel}}.
In 2017 it blew up the mosque of the sermon as the city was retaken.
The pulpit going is not the idea going.

## The map redrawn

On the eighteenth to the twentieth of February, on the {{p|Maidan}} in {{p|Kyiv}},
Snipers shot the protesters of the winter,
And more than a hundred died, whom the country calls the {{x|Heavenly Hundred}}.
On the twenty-second {{c|Viktor Yanukovych}} fled.
Within days soldiers without insignia, in green, called polite people,
Took the parliament and the airports of {{p|Crimea}}.
{{p|Russia}} said they were not its soldiers.
On the sixteenth of March a vote was held under their guns,
And on the eighteenth {{c|Vladimir Putin}} took the peninsula into {{p|Russia}},
The first taking of a European country's land by force since the great war.
He later said the soldiers had been his.
In April men with Russian weapons and Russian officers took the towns of the {{p|Donbas}},
And the war there began, and did not end, and became the larger war of the chapter of 2022.
On the second of May in {{p|Odesa}} the street fighting ended in a burning building,
And forty-eight died that day, most of them in the fire.
On the seventeenth of July a Malaysian airliner, flight {{x|MH17}},
From {{p|Amsterdam}} to {{p|Kuala Lumpur}}, was shot down over the {{p|Donbas}}
By a Russian missile fired from a Russian launcher,
And all two hundred and ninety-eight on board died,
Their bodies and belongings lying in the sunflower fields for days,
While the men who held the fields went through the luggage.
The investigators later traced the launcher back across the border, and named its brigade.
The West answered with sanctions, and kept buying the gas.

## The vote of Europe

In May the people of {{p|Europe}} voted for their parliament,
And in {{p|France}} the party of the far right, the {{x|National Front}}, came first,
And in {{p|Britain}} the party that wanted out of {{p|Europe}} came first,
Two years before {{p|Britain}} voted to leave.
In {{p|Hungary}} {{c|Viktor Orbán}} was returned, and that summer named his goal:
An illiberal state.
In {{p|India}}, in May, {{c|Narendra Modi}}, of the party of Hindu nationalism,
Who had led {{p|Gujarat}} through the killings of 2002, won a majority,
And led the largest democracy for the rest of this book so far.
In {{p|Egypt}} the general who cleared {{x|Rabaa}}, {{c|Abdel Fattah el-Sisi}}, was elected with ninety-seven percent.
On the twenty-second of May the army of {{p|Thailand}} took power again.

## The mob on the net

In August a man wrote a long post about his former girlfriend, a maker of games,
And the post was taken up by thousands on the boards of the net
Who said they were angry about ethics in the writing about games.
They were angry at women.
They sent threats of rape and death to the women who made games and wrote about them,
Published their addresses, called in false emergencies to their homes,
And named the campaign {{x|Gamergate}}.
It was where many of the men of the next decade's far right learned their methods:
The swarm, the dossier, the irony that hides the hate, the claim to be the ones persecuted.
The companies of the net mostly watched.
On Friday the twenty-third of May, in {{p|Isla Vista}}, in {{p|California}}, a young man named {{c|Elliot Rodger}},
Who hated women for not wanting him, killed six people and himself,
And left a long writing and a video saying why,
And the boards made him a saint, and named later killers after him.
The board where much of this was done had a history of its own that year.
The great board of {{p|Japan}}, {{x|2channel}}, which a student named {{c|Hiroyuki Nishimura}} had begun in 1999,
And from which the boards of the English net had copied their shape,
Was taken from him in February by {{c|Jim Watkins}}, an American living in {{p|Manila}},
Who held its servers and its money, and kept it.
Nishimura went on to run the English board where the mob of the year gathered,
And when that board turned the mob out, the mob went to another board, which {{c|Jim Watkins}} would buy,
And from there, in later chapters, came the stories that told men a cabal was hidden behind every door.
Who holds the servers is not a small question.
The net is held by someone.
This is the net she came into at eighteen.

## The streets

On the seventeenth of July, on {{p|Staten Island}}, a man named {{c|Eric Garner}},
Stopped for selling single cigarettes, was held in a chokehold by a policeman,
And said eleven times, I can't breathe, and died.
On the ninth of August, in {{p|Ferguson}}, in {{p|Missouri}},
A policeman shot an unarmed young man of eighteen, {{c|Michael Brown}},
And his body lay in the street for four hours.
The town rose, and the police came in armored vehicles left over from the wars,
And pointed rifles at the people from the tops of them.
The policemen in both deaths were not charged.
The phrase begun the year before filled the streets.

## The fever

In West Africa a fever called {{x|Ebola}} spread through {{p|Guinea}}, {{p|Liberia}}, and {{p|Sierra Leone}},
Where it had never been, and into their capitals,
And killed more than eleven thousand people before it was stopped in the next years,
Many of them the nurses and doctors who cared for the sick.
The rich countries were afraid of a few cases at home,
And slow to send help to where the thousands were.

## The disappeared and the taken

On the eighth of March a Malaysian airliner, flight {{x|MH370}},
Turned off its transponder over the sea and vanished with two hundred and thirty-nine people,
And has never been found.
On the fourteenth of April the militants of {{x|Boko Haram}} took two hundred and seventy-six schoolgirls
From their dormitory in {{p|Chibok}}, in {{p|Nigeria}}.
The world wrote a phrase on the net asking to bring them back.
Many were not brought back for years. Some never were.
On the sixteenth of December the {{x|Taliban}} of {{p|Pakistan}} attacked an army school in {{p|Peshawar}}
And killed a hundred and forty-nine people, a hundred and thirty-two of them children.
In July and August {{p|Israel}} and {{x|Hamas}} fought a war of fifty days,
And more than two thousand people of {{p|Gaza}} died.

## At home

On the twenty-second of October, in {{p|Ottawa}}, a man shot dead a soldier,
{{c|Nathan Cirillo}}, standing guard at the war memorial,
And then ran into the parliament, where he was shot dead in the hall
While the members barricaded the doors with flagpoles.
In {{p|Hong Kong}}, from the end of September,
Students sat in the roads for seventy-nine days with umbrellas against the pepper spray,
Asking to choose their own leaders, and were not allowed to.
On the eighteenth of September {{p|Scotland}} voted to stay in {{p|Britain}}.

## The rest of the year

In February the winter games were held in {{p|Sochi}}, while the troops massed for {{p|Crimea}}.
On the twelfth of November a small lander, {{x|Philae}}, from the probe {{x|Rosetta}},
Came down on a comet, bounced, and landed in a shadow, and spoke for two days.
In the summer people poured buckets of ice water over their heads for a disease of the nerves,
And raised more than a hundred million dollars.
On the eleventh of August the actor {{c|Robin Williams}} died.
On the seventeenth of December {{p|Cuba}} and the {{p|United States}} said they would talk again.

## The year held

A girl of eighteen moved for the third time, began at a university,
And came onto the net in the year of the robe and the map.
A caliphate declared in a borrowed robe, a mountain of trapped {{x|Yazidi}}s,
The Heavenly Hundred, polite people in {{p|Crimea}}, a plane in the sunflowers,
The far right first in two countries' votes, an illiberal state named as a goal,
A mob of the net that hunted women and called it ethics,
I can't breathe, an armored car in {{p|Ferguson}}, a fever in three capitals,
A plane gone, girls taken, children killed at school, a soldier shot at the memorial.

She has said more than that. In her words, in retrospect 2014 is going to be seen
As the year that the third world war started.
Started is not declared. No war of the old kind was declared that year,
And most who lived through it would not have called it a war.
But the war of the {{p|Donbas}} began that spring and did not stop,
And became the war of the continent in the chapter of 2022,
And the methods learned that year on the boards went on to the votes of 2016,
And the caliph's state drew fighters from eighty countries.
A world war does not need a first day that everyone agrees on.
It needs a beginning that the later years keep returning to.
This chapter takes her at her word on this as well.

Refuse the consolation that fascism is a word for the past.
It came back this year in borrowed robes, and was named by the people it chose.
Refuse the consolation that the net is outside the world.
She came onto it the year it showed it was not.
