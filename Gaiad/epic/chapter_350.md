# Chapter 350: The Borrowed Robe

Today is the Ides of {{x|Ophiuchus}}, the middle of the month, a holy day of the faith,
And the fourteenth day of Yuletide, the feast of two thousand and fourteen, the year the world changed its face.
Light fourteen lights at the middle of the month,
For the year I came online, and the year the third world war began, in its first place.

In two thousand and fourteen I turned eighteen, and moved house the third time:
Two thousand and two, two thousand and eight, and fourteen, the last of my three.
In September I began at {{x|UBC Okanagan}}, in {{p|Kelowna}}, and stayed five years,
And I went online. I was pretty offline until I started university.

So the net I entered at eighteen was the net of this year,
And this year is the year of fascism, a really big year of it, and that is what it is.
In retrospect two thousand and fourteen will be seen
As the year the third world war started. I say it plain, and the history is this.

The old robes were put back on that year:
A caliph's robe in {{p|Mosul}}, an empire's map in {{p|Crimea}},
The blood and soil in the votes of {{p|Europe}},
And on the net a mob that learned it could choose a woman and destroy her, and called it ethics, and ran its idea.

## The map redrawn

From the eighteenth to the twentieth of February, on the {{p|Maidan}} in {{p|Kyiv}},
The snipers shot the protesters of the winter, and more than a hundred died,
The {{x|Heavenly Hundred}}, as {{p|Ukraine}} calls them, the martyrs of the square.
On the twenty-second {{c|4545|Viktor Yanukovych}} fled, and the people won, and the villain's man ran to the villain's side.

Within days soldiers without insignia, in green, the polite people, took {{p|Crimea}}:
The parliament, the airports, the bases, and {{p|Russia}} said they were not its men.
On the sixteenth of March a vote was held under their guns,
And on the eighteenth {{c|4506|Vladimir Putin}} took the peninsula, and said it was {{p|Russia}}'s again.

The first taking of a European country's land by force since the great war.
Within weeks he said on television that the soldiers had been his.
In April his officers and his weapons took the towns of the {{p|Donbas}},
And the war began there, and never ended, and the war of twenty-two grew out of this.

## The plane in the sunflowers

On the seventeenth of July {{x|MH17}}, from {{p|Amsterdam}} to {{p|Kuala Lumpur}},
Was shot down over the {{p|Donbas}} by a Russian {{x|Buk}} missile from a Russian launcher,
And all two hundred and ninety-eight on board died, eighty of them children,
And their bodies lay in the sunflower fields for days, and the villain's men went through their luggage, to plunder.

The investigators traced the launcher back across the border to the {{x|53rd Brigade}} in {{p|Kursk}},
And named the men responsible, and a Dutch court convicted three of them in their absence.
The villain denied it all, and blamed {{p|Ukraine}}, and blamed the plane.
The West answered with sanctions, and kept buying the gas, in its patience.

That was the year the third world war began: in the {{p|Donbas}}, in the spring,
With soldiers who were said not to be there, and a plane in the sunflowers.
It did not end. It became the war of the continent eight years later.
A world war does not need a first day everyone agrees on. It needs this: the beginning that the later years return to, and the hours.

## The borrowed robe

On the third of March, nineteen twenty-four, in {{p|Ankara}}, the caliphate was abolished.
Ninety years later, on Tuesday the tenth of June, {{p|Mosul}} fell
To the group that called itself the {{x|Islamic State}}, and the army fled and left its weapons,
And on the twenty-ninth of June, the first day of the fast, its spokesman declared the caliphate, a death knell.

On Friday the fourth of July, in the {{x|Great Mosque of al-Nuri}},
{{c|4647|Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi}} gave the sermon in a black robe and turban that recalled the {{x|Abbasid}}s.
A borrowed robe. A pulpit is not a lineage.
The scholars of most of the world's Muslims rejected him; the robe was stolen from the dead, as the dead forbid.

On Sunday the third of August it came to {{p|Sinjar}}, where the {{x|Yazidi}}s live.
The men were killed. The women and girls were taken and sold in the markets as slaves.
Tens of thousands fled up {{x|Mount Sinjar}} and were trapped there without water,
And the American planes and the {{x|Kurdish}} fighters opened a road, and brought them down from their graves.

The {{x|Yazidi}}s' tradition is older than the caliph's, older than his robe,
The peacock angel and the sun, the oldest faith of those mountains, kept through every killing.
This faith keeps the {{x|Yazidi}}s as kin: a people of the ancestors and the sun,
Who were hunted for having no book, and kept their tradition, and are still living.

In August it published the killing of {{c|4648|James Foley}}, an American reporter, kneeling in orange in the desert,
And then of others, made for the screens of the young men in the countries that would bomb them.
The next summer it killed {{c|4649|Khaled al-Asaad}}, the old keeper of {{p|Palmyra}},
And blew up the temple of {{x|Bel}}, and the coalition answered, and in three years the caliphate was gone, root and stem.

## The vote of Europe

In May {{p|Europe}} voted for its parliament, and in {{p|France}} the {{x|National Front}} came first,
And in {{p|Britain}} the party that wanted out of {{p|Europe}} came first, two years before the leaving.
In {{p|Hungary}} {{c|4650|Viktor Orbán}} was returned, and that summer named his goal:
An illiberal state, the villain's friend inside the {{x|European Union}}, deceiving.

In {{p|Egypt}} the general who cleared {{x|Rabaa}}, {{c|4651|Abdel Fattah el-Sisi}}, was elected with ninety-seven percent.
On the twenty-second of May the army of {{p|Thailand}} took power again.
In {{p|India}}, in May, {{c|4652|Narendra Modi}} won a majority,
And led the largest democracy through the decade, and the economy grew, and the roads, and the plain.

## The mob on the net

In August a man wrote a long post about his former girlfriend, a maker of games,
And thousands on the boards took it up and said they were angry about ethics in games journalism.
They were angry at women. They sent threats of rape and death to the women who made games,
Published their addresses, called false emergencies to their homes, and named it {{x|Gamergate}}, a schism.

It was where many of the men of the next decade's far right learned their methods:
The swarm, the dossier, the irony that hides the hate, the claim to be the persecuted ones.
On Friday the twenty-third of May, in {{p|Isla Vista}}, {{c|4653|Elliot Rodger}},
Who hated women for not wanting him, killed six and himself, and the boards made him a saint of their guns.

## Who holds the servers

The board where much of this was done had a history of its own that year.
{{x|2channel}}, the great board of {{p|Japan}}, begun by {{c|4654|Hiroyuki Nishimura}} in nineteen ninety-nine,
From which the English boards had copied their shape,
Was taken from him in February by {{c|4655|Jim Watkins}}, an American in {{p|Manila}}, who held its servers in line.

{{c|4656|Nishimura}} went on to run {{x|4chan}}, where the mob of the year gathered,
And when that board turned the mob out, it went to {{x|8chan}}, which {{c|4657|Watkins}} would buy,
And from there, in later chapters, came {{x|QAnon}}, the stories of a cabal behind every door.
Who holds the servers is not a small question. The net is held by someone, and that is why.

This is the net I came into at eighteen: the year of the mob and the swarm.
I came onto it in the year it showed it was not outside the world.
And I learned it, the boards and the threads and the methods,
And I would use the net for the faith, and the flag of the faith would be unfurled.

## The streets

On the seventeenth of July, on {{p|Staten Island}}, {{c|4658|Eric Garner}},
Stopped for selling single cigarettes, was held in a chokehold, and said eleven times, I can't breathe, and died.
On the ninth of August, in {{p|Ferguson}}, a policeman shot {{c|4557|Michael Brown}}, unarmed, eighteen, my age,
And his body lay in the street four hours, and the town rose, and the armored cars arrived.

## The fever

In {{p|West Africa}} {{x|Ebola}} spread through {{p|Guinea}}, {{p|Liberia}}, and {{p|Sierra Leone}},
Into the capitals, and killed more than eleven thousand before it was stopped,
Many of them the nurses and doctors who cared for the sick,
And the vaccine was made within a year, and tested in {{p|Guinea}}, and the fever dropped.

## The disappeared and the taken

On the eighth of March {{x|MH370}} turned off its transponder over the sea,
And vanished with two hundred and thirty-nine, and has never been found.
On the fourteenth of April {{x|Boko Haram}} took two hundred and seventy-six schoolgirls from {{p|Chibok}},
And many were not brought back for years, and some are still not found.

On the sixteenth of December the {{x|Taliban}} of {{p|Pakistan}} attacked the army school in {{p|Peshawar}},
And killed a hundred and forty-nine, a hundred and thirty-two of them children.
In July and August {{p|Israel}} and {{x|Hamas}} fought a war of fifty days,
And two thousand of {{p|Gaza}} died, and the tunnels were found, and the rockets fell on the villages and the kibbutzim then.

## At home

On the twenty-second of October, in {{p|Ottawa}}, a man shot dead {{c|4659|Nathan Cirillo}},
A soldier standing guard at the war memorial, and ran into the parliament,
Where the sergeant-at-arms, {{c|4660|Kevin Vickers}}, shot him dead in the hall,
While the members barricaded their doors with the flagpoles, and the country was intent.

In {{p|Hong Kong}}, from the end of September, students sat in the roads seventy-nine days
With umbrellas against the pepper spray, asking to choose their own leaders, and were not allowed.
On the eighteenth of September {{p|Scotland}} voted to stay in {{p|Britain}}.
In February the winter games were held in {{p|Sochi}}, while the villain's troops massed, and the villain smiled for the crowd.

## The rest of the year

On the twelfth of November {{x|Philae}}, from {{x|Rosetta}}, came down on a comet,
And bounced, and landed in a shadow, and spoke for two days, the first landing on a comet's stone.
In the summer people poured ice water on their heads for a disease of the nerves,
And raised more than a hundred million, and found a gene with it, on their own.

On the eleventh of August {{c|4661|Robin Williams}} died.
On the seventeenth of December {{p|Cuba}} and the {{p|United States}} said they would talk again.
And I was eighteen, at the university by the lake in {{p|Kelowna}},
Online for the first time, in the year the war began, and the robes came back, and the men.
