# Chapter 305: By Physical Processes

In October, eighteen seventy-four,
A colonel went up-country for the dead:
{{c|4254|Henry Steel Olcott}}, lawyer, who before
Had worn the {{x|Union}} blue, wrote what they said,

There at the {{c|4255|Eddy}} farm, {{p|Chittenden}}, then;
He met {{c|4256|Helena Blavatsky}} there, new
In the country a year, come, as the men
And women all came, to the séances too.

Neither held office yet; the season there
Was {{x|America}}'s time of talking dead;
And what they carried out was not a prayer,
But a meeting, a question left unsaid.

September, 'seventy-five; to her rooms
In {{p|New York}}, on the seventh, seventeen
To hear {{c|4257|George Henry Felt}}: the stones, the tombs
Of {{p|Egypt}}, {{p|Greece}}, {{p|Rome}}: one canon, between,

In stone and frame, they knew. The lecture done,
{{c|4258|Olcott}} moved a society be made;
The next night {{c|4259|Felt}} spoke once again; and one,
{{c|4258|Olcott}}, took the chair; the minutes were laid

Down by {{c|4260|William Quan Judge}}, a lawyer, young;
And sixteen handed in their names; they said,
On the thirteenth, they took, from an old tongue,
Theosophy, wisdom, as letterhead

Of gods; the wisdom old, the club was new,
Its aims still being written; bylaws passed
October's thirtieth; the founders' view,
The preamble, a method, built to last:

To know the {{c|4261|Supreme Power}}, and the high
Spirits by physical processes' aid,
Instrument, body, law; and by and by
So went the preamble the founders made.

It stood November seventeenth, the day{{fn|The printed sheet dated the organization to 30 October, the day of the bylaws; Olcott later corrected the founding to his inaugural address of 17 November.}}
At sixty-four, {{p|Madison Avenue}},
{{p|Mott Memorial Hall}}, {{p|New York}}; the way
Its head gave his inaugural; and who

Held office: he the president, the chair;
She kept the letters; {{c|4262|Judge}} was counsel, for
Vice-presidents were {{c|4259|Felt}}, and, sitting there,
{{c|4263|Seth Pancoast}}, doctor; treasurer; and more:

Librarian, recorder: a machine
Small and complete, pointed at the occult,
The cabala, the laws behind the seen
Wonders; no pilgrimage; no land; result:

A club in hired rooms; and yet the heat
Was larger than its officers: they sought
One study: old faiths side by side, to meet,
The {{p|East}}'s and {{p|West}}'s texts, read as they were taught,

Not a mission to clear them from the field.
That longing's real, kin to this faith; its own:
Not forerunner, but guest; a psalm, unsealed,
A sutra, by one reader both are known;

The ancestors don't perish when a book
More opens. In 'seventy-seven, she
Brought out *{{x|Isis Unveiled}}*, two volumes; took
Tradition by tradition's side; then, see:

In May, 'seventy-eight, a printed call
Carried the longing; and a harder charge:
Teach the {{p|West}} the {{x|Eastern}}; oppose, in all,
The dogmatic theology at large,

The {{x|Christian}} most of all, pernicious, said
The circular; and brotherhood the same
Paragraphs. In 'seventy-nine, they sped{{fn|The three objects quoted today, a nucleus of brotherhood, the comparative study of religions, and the investigation of the powers latent in man, were drafted and redrafted after 1875; they are not the bylaws of that November.}}
To {{p|Mumbai}}'s shore; and on to {{p|Chennai}} came;

At {{p|Adyar}} they made the headquarters' stand,
Where still it stands. She said, as time went by,
The teaching was in Masters' keeping, and
The members were to take it from on high.

And this book takes the masters at their word,
And wonders seriously; letters came,
The hands unseen, the voice that she had heard;
A reaching, and the ground answered the claim;

What was heard, heard: processes, wonders, all
Study; the ground between is where they meet,
The minutes and the miracles. Recall
{{c|4259|Felt}}'s lost canon: one proportion, complete,

That would make temples of three peoples' lands
Agree in one measure: hope human, old,
The scope of every faith that understands
The world as one. This, too, would see, enrolled,

The temples in one measure; in one tree
The faiths: not rough drafts from a {{p|New York}} press,
But branches, prayed for centuries, each free
With its own people, its own word, no less;

Norito stays norito, sutra stays
A sutra; and the reader's work, to keep
Each one, and all, and keep them heard, their ways,
From seventy-five, one hall, rooted deep.

Wisdom by physical means: both halves stand;
The wisdom has the worth; the means, for all;
The masters, wonders, measure reach one land,
One reaching, and the ground answers the call.
