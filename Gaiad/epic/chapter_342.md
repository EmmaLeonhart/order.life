# Chapter 342: One Device

No holiday is named for this day.
The leftover note on an older medicine is not this page.

On Tuesday, the ninth of January, 2007,
In {{p|San Francisco}},
{{c|Steve Jobs}} showed a phone that was not yet for sale.
He said he had looked forward to the day
For two and a half years.
He set it beside the computer of nineteen eighty-four
And the music player of two thousand one.
Those machines are not this Tuesday.

He said he was introducing three products.
A wide music player, the glass as its control.
A phone.
A machine for the net.
One account has him say these are not three devices.
This is one device, and the name is iPhone.
Another account shortens that sentence.
The two accounts are not made one.
One account has him say the company
Is going to reinvent the phone.
Another has him say they want to.
The verb is not agreed.

The same day the computer left the company's name.
Apple Computer became Apple.
The company did not stop making the computers.

There is no separate pen.
He said the pointer is the hand,
What a person is born with.
The way of the glass is not set down.

He set the phones already in the world
At nine hundred fifty-seven million.
The chapter does not audit the figure.
The phone on the stage was not in those hands.

The price that day, with a contract of two years,
Is four hundred ninety-nine dollars
For the smaller memory,
And five hundred ninety-nine for the larger.
One report rounds each price to the hundred.
The round is not the price.
The partner in the January papers is Cingular.
He said the phone would not be sold until June,
And at first only in the {{p|United States}}.
He said it was shown early,
So the licensing would not disclose it first.
He said Europe in the last quarter of this year,
And Asia in the next.
The day a hand in Europe held one
Is not this January.
He said the faster network was coming.
The faster phone is not this year.

On Monday, the eleventh of June,
Still before any sale,
He offered other people's programs
As pages on the web.
The release of that day says such a page
Can look like a program built into the phone,
And that this keeps the phone secure.
Accounts of the room have him call it a sweet solution.
A program that lives on the phone
Is not what that Monday offered.
The way of making the page is not set down.

On Friday, the twenty-ninth of June,
The sale began.
The release of the day before,
Dated from {{p|Cupertino}},
Says six in the evening, local time,
At a hundred sixty-four of its own stores,
Open until midnight,
And as many as two phones to a person.
The online store took orders at six,
Pacific time.
The two sixes are not one clock.
The release names the partner's stores AT&T.
The January name and the June name
Are not printed as one word.
The prices in the release are the January prices.
The company said two hundred seventy thousand
In the first two days.
That count is not a million.

On Wednesday, the fifth of September,
The larger phone was cut by two hundred dollars,
To three hundred ninety-nine.
The smaller phone was withdrawn.
One paper rounds the prices
From six hundred to four hundred.
The round is not the company's figure.
He said the cut was for the holiday to come,
And that a million by the end of the month
Would have been reached without the cut.
Another reading says the sales were short.
The two reasons are not made one.

On Thursday he wrote to the people
Who had bought at the old price.
The letter says they trusted the company,
And that the company must live up to the trust.
It offers a hundred dollars in the store
To a buyer who is not already due a rebate.
The hundred is not the two hundred.
The way of collecting the credit is not set down.

The day the count reached a million is not agreed.
One paper says the weekend, and seventy-four days.
Another says the Monday.
He had said the end of September.
None is chosen.
A million hands are not the figure he gave in January.
A hope of ten million by the end of the next year
Is not a count of this summer.

In October he wrote that he wanted
Other people's programs on the phone itself,
And that a kit would reach their hands in February.
February is the next year.
The shop of the summer after is not this year.

The absurdity is a machine announced as three things
And as one thing,
Set against a count of other phones,
And sold, that first night, two to a person,
On one service, at a price that did not hold the summer.
The striving is to keep the device in a hand
From being the claim that every hand has one,
And to hold the letter's sentence about trust
Beside the cut the letter does not repay in full.
The page on the web is not the later shop.
The counts are not chosen.

Stand.
