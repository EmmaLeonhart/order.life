# Chapter 342: The Typed Name

The sixth day of Yuletide: two thousand six,
The feast of indexers, who typed the dead
Names, letter by letter, in all their clicks;
Light six; and type a name that none has read,

Offer them what the living have: the light.
I turned ten that year; now I spend my nights
In records strangers typed; I trace the flight
Mothers and fathers, {{p|Sweden}}'s parish rites,

The farms of {{p|Germany}}, where they begin
This book's own genealogy; at ten
I didn't know; none types for whom, within
The child who'll need it; they type it, and then

The child comes, and the name is there; and so
Is the indexers' faith, and mine: the work
Done for the dead is for the heir; I know
Each name, in time, will find its heir; none shirk.

## The typed names

August the first, a Tuesday, {{p|Provo}}: one,
{{c|4566|Marlin K. Jensen}}, its history man,
Of the {{x|Latter-day Saints}}: the rolls, each one,
In {{p|Utah}}'s mountain vault, were now to scan

And typed before they could be searched; and so
The typing opened: twice, each record, typed
By strangers, and a third to judge; a flow
Fifty thousand, next summer; pages swiped:

{{p|Indiana}}'s marriages; census, too,
Of nineteen hundred; parish books of lands
None of them would visit; the church's view:
The dead should have what living have, in hands

Of offering; this book shares it, ours too:
The ancestors are numberless; each name
Recited, offering; and, free, to you,
{{x|FamilySearch}} gave them back, to me, the same.

## Indexing and the encyclopedia

I hate encyclopedias' unpaid
Labor, and love the indexing; the line
Is purpose: indexers type names, and aid
So the dead are found; typing is the sign;

The volunteer not broken by it; none
Tells her she is a scholar while it grinds;
Encyclopedias take labor, spun
From would-be academics; and it binds

Them to a lesser academe, for free,
And calls it the dream; the indexer, though,
Wanted the dead to have their names; and she
Got what she wanted; the difference, so.

## The small messages

March twenty-first, {{c|4567|Jack Dorsey}} sent the first
On {{x|Twitter}}: just setting up my twttr; less
Than one-forty letters; and, so immersed,
The age argued there; now {{c|4552|Musk}}'s {{x|X}}, no less.

September fifth, the {{x|News Feed}}; users' cry,
Hundreds of thousands, on the feed; they stayed;
Then, twenty-sixth, to all thirteen and by;
October ninth, {{x|Google}} and {{x|YouTube}}, paid;

{{x|Amazon}}'s storage, rented, March; then hours
Of its machines in August,{{fn|Amazon Web Services.}} and the years
Of firms to come, built on unseen powers,
Mine too; {{x|Time}}: You, mirror; user appears.

## The planet

On August twenty-fourth, in {{p|Prague}}, a vote
Put {{p|Pluto}} out: {{x|Eris}}, beyond, was more,
Was larger; this book does not take the note:
{{p|Pluto}} is a planet, ninth, as before:

At nine I learned it; the rock did not change
With any vote. July twelfth: in the north,
{{x|Hezbollah}} crossed; soldiers killed; and the range
Of two seized; thirty-four days' bombs, sent forth

On {{p|Lebanon}}: the airport, bridges, south
Of {{p|Beirut}}; and thousands of rockets came
To {{p|Israel}}'s north; and, August, from the mouth
A ceasefire; the border held; guns tame.

## The civil war

February's twenty-second: the gold
Dome of the {{x|al-Askari Shrine}} fell, bombed;
Of two imams' tomb; and then, uncontrolled,
{{x|Shia}} and {{x|Sunni}} fought, their dead embalmed,

Whose dead, whose rite; a civil war; and laid
In {{p|Baghdad}}, the hands of each morning's dead
Were found. November: {{c|4568|Rumsfeld}}'s exit made;
December's thirtieth: {{c|4528|Saddam}}, and dead,

Hanged at dawn, who gassed {{p|Halabja}}, who filled
The graves; a phone filmed it; and everywhere
The film went; and the tyrant, gone and stilled;
October seventh, {{p|Moscow}}: in the stair,

There, {{c|4569|Anna Politkovskaya}}, who wrote
The truth of {{p|Chechnya}}, was shot at home,
Elevator, {{c|4506|Putin}}'s birthday: the note
His land's gift. In {{p|London}}: one, fled from home,

{{c|4570|Alexander Litvinenko}} drank tea
With two from {{c|4518|Putin}}'s service; it was spun
{{x|polonium}}, burning inside; and he
Named {{c|4518|Putin}}, dying; and, in good time, one

Inquiry said he probably approved;
The rarest metal, so the world would know
Whose hand: deniable, not hidden; moved
Traitors to understand. October, though,

The ninth, {{p|North Korea}} tested its first bomb,
Underground, small, and real; the world condemned;
The regime went on, with the camps, and calm;
The {{c|4571|Kim}}s would keep it, and famines unstemmed.

## The votes

January twenty-fifth, {{x|Hamas}} won
In {{p|Palestine}}; the money, cut off, for
Who won, by those who'd asked them; and as one
In February, {{c|4572|Stephen Harper}}; more

Nine years, {{p|Canada}}'s prime minister; mine,
My land. {{c|4573|Ellen Johnson Sirleaf}}, the first
{{p|Liberia}}: {{p|Africa}}'s first, a sign;
{{c|4574|Evo Morales}}, {{p|Bolivia}}, nursed

From altiplano farmers; first; and so
June third, {{p|Montenegro}}; then {{p|Thailand}}'s coup;
July eleventh: {{p|Mumbai}}'s trains, a blow;
Two hundred nine killed; back to work they knew.

## The rest of the year

{{c|4575|Al Gore}}'s slides, in May, a film was made plain:
{{x|An Inconvenient Truth}}; a lecture's heat
Cinemas; then {{x|Nintendo}}'s {{x|Wii}}; again,
Grandparents bowled in rooms, upon their feet.

## The year held

A girl of ten; strangers typing the dead
Twice, for the dead and for the heir; the feed,
One-forty letters, cloud by hour; and said,
{{p|Pluto}}, planet, whatever votes decreed;

The villain shot a writer, poisoned one
With burning metal; the tyrant hanged; hands
Of strangers typed my forebears' names, as done
For the dead, asking nothing of the lands.
