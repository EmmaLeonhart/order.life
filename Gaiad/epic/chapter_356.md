# Chapter 356: The Weekly Verses

No holiday is named for this day.

In 2020 {{c|Emma Leonhart}} turned twenty-four.
She has said this year is COVID.
In her words, 2020 is COVID.
The whole world held still at once, or tried to,
And the days lost their names, and a Tuesday was a Saturday.
That year she was writing a book of the world's ages in verse,
And putting out its verses week by week.
The verses ran through much of that year, to about the sixtieth chapter.
About the sixtieth is not the end.
A weekly verse is not a calendar yet. It was the beginning of one.
When the days lost their names, she was giving them verses.
This book is that book.
The year was everyone's.
It notes only that one of the things begun in the year the world stayed home
Was a way of giving each day a name again.

## The closed city

On the last day of 2019 the office of the health body of the nations in {{p|China}}
Heard of a pneumonia of unknown cause in {{p|Wuhan}}.
On the twenty-third of January the city of eleven million was closed,
The trains stopped, the roads blocked, the people sealed in their towers.
A closed city is not a closed virus.
On the thirtieth the body declared an emergency of international concern.
In February the doctor who had warned, {{c|Li Wenliang}}, died of it,
And the people of {{p|China}} filled the net with grief until the censors came.
In {{p|Lombardy}}, in March, the hospitals filled, and the army trucks carried the coffins out of {{p|Bergamo}} at night,
Because the crematoria could not keep up.
On the eleventh of March, in {{p|Geneva}}, {{c|Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus}} said it could be called a pandemic.
A word late is not a word wrong.
On the twenty-seventh of March, in the rain, in an empty {{p|St. Peter's Square}},
{{c|Pope Francis}}, of the chapter of 2013, prayed alone before the crowd that was not there.
Alone is not unheard. The crowd was on screens.

## Into the house

The work went into the house. The school went into the house. The church went into the house.
A meeting on a screen is not a meeting in a room. It is a meeting.
Those who kept the shops and the wards and the warehouses did not go into the house,
And were called essential, and were paid the same.
The markets fell faster than in any month since 1929, and then rose higher than before,
Because the banks of the chapter of 2008 had learned to print.
The rich got richer that year. The poor got sick.
Masks became a flag, and a mask on a face in some towns was a fight.
By the end of the year nearly two million had died that were counted,
And the counts were argued over, by cause, by place, by excess.
An argued count is not a false count.

## The book of risks

In March, the month the world closed,
A philosopher of the movement of the chapter of 2015, {{c|Toby Ord}},
Published a book on the risks that could end humanity, {{x|The Precipice}},
And ranked a pandemic high among them,
And a machine that did not share our aims higher still.
The movement had turned from the malaria nets toward the far future,
And called it longtermism,
And the young man of the exchange of the chapter before gave to it, and was praised for it.
A risk named the month it arrives is not a risk foreseen in time.
It was foreseen. It was not prepared for.

## The knee

On the twenty-fifth of May, in {{p|Minneapolis}},
A policeman knelt on the neck of {{c|George Floyd}}, a black man accused of passing a false twenty-dollar bill,
For nine minutes and twenty-nine seconds, while he said he could not breathe,
And called for his mother, and died,
And a girl of seventeen filmed it on her phone.
The country rose, in the middle of the sickness, in every city,
The largest protests in its history, under the words of the chapter of 2013,
And a police station in {{p|Minneapolis}} burned.
In March the police had killed {{c|Breonna Taylor}} in her bed in {{p|Louisville}}.
In February men in a truck had chased and shot {{c|Ahmaud Arbery}} while he ran in {{p|Georgia}}.
The policeman of {{p|Minneapolis}} was convicted the next year.

## The plane and the farm town

On the eighth of January, five days after the {{p|United States}} killed the general {{c|Qasem Soleimani}} in {{p|Baghdad}},
And {{p|Iran}} fired missiles back,
{{p|Iran}} shot down a Ukrainian airliner leaving {{p|Tehran}}, taking it for a missile,
And all one hundred and seventy-six aboard died,
Most of them bound for {{p|Canada}}, students and families, fifty-five of them Canadian.
On the eighteenth and nineteenth of April a man dressed as a policeman, in a car painted like a police car,
Drove across {{p|Nova Scotia}} for thirteen hours and killed twenty-two people,
The worst such killing in the country's history.
In February the people of the {{x|Wet'suwet'en}} and those with them
Blocked the railways of {{p|Canada}} against a pipeline through their land.

## The blast

On the fourth of August, in the port of {{p|Beirut}},
Two thousand seven hundred tons of ammonium nitrate, seized from a ship and left in a warehouse for six years,
Caught fire and exploded,
One of the largest blasts ever not made by a bomb,
And killed two hundred and eighteen people and broke the windows of half the city.
The officials who had left it there had been warned, in writing, many times.

## The courts and the laws

On the fifteenth of June, in {{x|Bostock v. Clayton County}},
The {{x|Supreme Court}} of the {{p|United States}} said that to fire a person for being gay or transgender
Is to fire them because of sex, which the law forbids.
On the eighteenth of September {{c|Ruth Bader Ginsburg}}, the justice who had fought for women's equality for fifty years,
Died, and the president put a judge of the other side in her seat
Eight days before the election.
On the thirtieth of June {{p|China}} wrote a law of security for {{p|Hong Kong}},
And the protests of the chapter before were over, and their leaders jailed or fled.

## The vote

On the third of November {{c|Joe Biden}} was elected president, with seven million more votes than the man of the chapter of 2016,
Who said he had won, and that it had been stolen,
And sent his lawyers to sixty courts, and lost in all but one,
And kept saying it.
The next chapter opens with what that saying did.

## The wars and the poison

On the twenty-seventh of September {{p|Azerbaijan}} attacked the {{p|Armenian}}-held land of {{p|Nagorno-Karabakh}},
With drones bought from {{p|Turkey}} and {{p|Israel}},
And in forty-four days took much of it.
On the fourth of November the prime minister of {{p|Ethiopia}}, prized for peace in the chapter of 2018,
Sent his army into {{p|Tigray}}, and a war began that killed hundreds of thousands, many by hunger.
On the ninth of August {{p|Belarus}} held a vote the people said was stolen, and filled the streets in white and red,
And were beaten into silence by the winter.
On the twentieth of August {{c|Alexei Navalny}}, who had opposed {{c|Putin}} for years,
Was poisoned on a plane with the nerve agent of the chapter of 2018, and lived, flown to {{p|Berlin}}.

## The machines

In June the lab named open showed its third model, of a hundred and seventy-five billion weights,
That could write an essay, a poem, a program, from a few words of asking,
And people began to say that the machines could write.
On the thirtieth of November a program of {{c|DeepMind}}, {{x|AlphaFold}},
Predicted the shapes of proteins from their letters nearly as well as the laboratories,
Solving a problem of fifty years, and it would win a {{x|Nobel Prize}} in the chapter of 2024.
On the thirtieth of May a rocket of a private company carried astronauts to the station for the first time.

## The needle

On the eighth of December, in {{p|Coventry}}, {{c|Margaret Keenan}}, ninety years old,
Was given the first dose of a vaccine made of a message of the cell, outside the trials,
Less than a year after the letters of the virus were published.
The vaccine was built on the work of {{c|Katalin Karikó}} and {{c|Drew Weissman}},
Who had worked for decades on such messages while the grants were refused,
And of {{c|Uğur Şahin}} and {{c|Özlem Türeci}}, children of migrants to {{p|Germany}}.

## The rest of the year

On the thirty-first of January {{p|Britain}} left the {{x|European Union}}.
On the twenty-sixth of January {{c|Kobe Bryant}} died in a helicopter with his daughter.
In the summer of {{p|Australia}} the fires of the chapter before finally went out in the rain.
In November the farmers of {{p|India}} camped on the roads around {{p|Delhi}} against the laws on their crops,
And stayed for a year, and won.

## The year held

A woman of twenty-four giving the days back their names, a verse a week.
A city closed, coffins in {{p|Bergamo}}, a pope alone in the rain,
The work, the school, the church in the house, the essential paid the same,
A book of risks the month the risk came, a knee for nine minutes and twenty-nine seconds,
A plane shot down with the students bound for {{p|Canada}}, twenty-two killed in {{p|Nova Scotia}},
A port that exploded, a law that said fired for being who you are is fired because of sex,
A justice replaced in eight days, a vote said to be stolen that was not,
Drones over {{p|Karabakh}}, a war in {{p|Tigray}}, the white and red of {{p|Minsk}}, poison on a plane,
A machine that writes, a machine that folds, and a needle in {{p|Coventry}}.

Refuse the consolation that an empty street is an empty city,
And that an argued count is a false one.
Refuse the consolation that the days without names had to stay nameless.
