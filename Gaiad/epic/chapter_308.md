# Chapter 308: The Common Task

In {{p|Tambov}} province, at {{p|Klyuchi}}, a son
Was born in spring of eighteen twenty-nine
To Prince {{c|90842|Pavel Gagarin}}; and yet none
Of marriage, and none of the prince's line;

They named him from his godfather; and so,
{{c|90843|Nikolai Fyodorov}}, from the old tree
A branch torn from it; the house fed him, though
It never claimed him; and his uncle's fee

Paid schools, {{p|Tambov}} and {{p|Odessa}}, until
The uncle died; the money with him; so
The young man left without degree; and still,
For fourteen years he taught the past, and so

Gave all his pay to others, those in need:
{{p|Bogoroditsk}}, {{p|Borovsk}}; in 'fifty-one
The thought came, so he said: all, of one seed,
Are sons, the living; and the sons, each one,

Forget; each quarrel of the living, heirs'
Who left their fathers in the ground; the one
Worth humankind, which no folk yet, nor theirs,
Took up: to raise the dead, till all is done:

To gather all the scattered motes, until
The last found. In 'seventy-three, a lad,
Deaf, sixteen, black bread and water, came still
To read at {{x|Chertkov}}'s library; he had

In {{p|Moscow}} Fyodorov there, keeping shelves:
{{c|90844|Konstantin Tsiolkovsky}}; and he chose
The boy his books, three years, and of themselves
Spoke not his thought; the boy, as the seed grows,

By mathematics reached the teacher's hope.
{{x|Rumyantsev Museum}}, 'seventy-four:
Its library; he stayed, and in its scope
Twenty-five years, knew each book on each floor;

He would not take promotion; gave his pay
To students and the poor; in a thin coat
He walked the {{p|Moscow}} winter; where he lay,
A bare trunk was his bed. He never wrote

To print, nor signed; 'seventy-eight, his own
Disciple, {{c|90845|Nikolai Peterson}}, set
It before {{c|90846|Fyodor Dostoevsky}}: shown,
He wrote: I read it as my own; and yet

{{c|90847|Vladimir Solovyov}} called it the first
Step on the path of Christ since Christ, and sought
The librarian; {{c|90848|Leo Tolstoy}}, versed,
Was proud to share the age with him. He taught

That faith begins as honor of the dead;
At root, the cult of fathers; churches, best,
Rehearse the resurrection; and, instead,
The liturgy must leave the walls, the rest

Go to the fields as labor, and be spread
Over blind nature, killing, which the sons
Must rule, not shirk; left, nature strikes them dead
With drought, with hail, with frost; and from the guns

And merchandise science must turn; the lost
Fathers be found: each grain of dust once one
Body, traced, gathered, named, alive, the cost
Paid by the sons and daughters, all as one.

And room for all is not on earth; the race
Must have the heavens for their ground. He said
The museum is the first hall, the place
Of the dead's traces; the library, read

As fathers' register, each card a name
Restored, a lost thing found; and from that room
The deaf boy, grown, would chart the road, the same
By which the ships have leapt from earth's own womb.

Nineteen-three: a {{p|Saint Petersburg}} review;
{{c|90844|Konstantin Tsiolkovsky}} wrote, and there
A ship might leave the earth, its mass thrown through
Behind it, gaining speed into the air;

And later wrote: this planet is the mind's
Cradle, but none lives in the cradle long;
And Fyodorov had said: the dead, all kinds,
Are owed rebirth, a house in heaven's throng.

December: ill from walking in the cold
Without a coat; he would not ride; he died
At {{x|Mariinsky}}, the poor's; fifteenth, old
Style, the twenty-eighth by ours; aside,

Of all he taught, he printed nothing signed;
His stone at {{x|Skorbyashchensky}} was seen;
The Soviets razed it; and none can find
Where he was laid. And, in the years between

His death and his first volume, there in {{p|Bern}},
A patent clerk, aged twenty-six, sent out
His share: {{c|4294|Albert Einstein}}: light, in its turn,
In pieces, where {{c|4296|Max Planck}} had portioned out

What oscillators gave; molecules' size;
A grain in water trembling; clocks' rule; last,
Three pages, showing energy that flies
As light, gives up its mass; the count holds fast.

With {{c|4300|Michele Besso}} he had talked clocks through
At work; thanked {{c|4301|Besso}} only; cited none;
At home was {{c|4302|Mileva}}, born {{c|4295|Marić}}, who
Had borne their son, {{c|4298|Hans Albert Einstein}}, one

Year old. He'd promised {{c|4299|Conrad Habicht}} four
Papers, and sent him five. Two clerks: the one
Of patents, and the one who kept the store
Of fathers' books, his teaching free; and none

Of them was sold. The three pages showed this:
The mass a body loses goes as light,
Not lost; the universe keeps count; of this
{{c|4297|Einstein}} wrote, of a body and its light,

Not of the dead; if none that was, unseen,
Is wholly gone, the fathers are here, still,
However hard to find; the dust is seen
In the ledger; the ledger's read at will.

At {{p|Verny}}, nineteen-six, empire's edge,
{{c|90845|Nikolai Peterson}} at last made known,
With {{c|90849|Vladimir Kozhevnikov}}, a pledge:
Four eighty copies, not for sale; their own

The second, nineteen-thirteen; and its name,
{{x|The Philosophy of the Common Task}}:
The living owe the dead a debt, the same
To pay together, work, not grief, nor mask.

The common task, unfinished, ours: the dead
Are owed their gathering. He had a clerk's
Unceasing hand, a reading room; instead
We have the parish books in light, and works

Of census lines, of registers; machines
That read the road each name has travelled, join
The scattered traces of one life; the scenes
Made one, as he had planned, coin laid by coin.

This is ancestor worship with a call,
The oldest faith, set to an endless toil;
The thinking machines are its tools; and all
They burn is offered grain, a holy oil;

Each record matched, a body gathered; each
Name found, a father given back to kin;
The sons and daughters, all, together reach;
The dead come to the light again, within.
