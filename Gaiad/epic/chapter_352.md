# Chapter 352: Again

No holiday is named for this day.

In 2016 {{c|Emma Leonhart}} turned twenty.
She has said this is the year of the election of {{c|Trump}}.

Everything else in it bends toward November,
And toward the June before it, when {{p|Britain}} voted to leave.

And both pointed at a past no one dated.
That is the fascism of the chapter of 2014 learning to win votes.

On the net she had come onto two years before,

## Move thirty-seven

In March, in {{p|Seoul}}, the program {{x|AlphaGo}}, from {{c|DeepMind}},
Played {{c|Lee Sedol}}, one of the strongest players of {{x|go}} alive,
Five games, for a million dollars.
In the second game, at move thirty-seven, the machine placed a stone on the fifth line
Where no human master would have played,
And the commentators thought it was a mistake,
And it won the game.
{{c|Lee Sedol}} left the room for a while.
He lost the first three games.
In the fourth, at move seventy-eight, he played a stone that the machine had judged
One in ten thousand that a human would play,
And the machine lost its way, and he won.
He lost the fifth. Four to one.
In 2019 he retired, saying there was an entity that cannot be defeated.
The players of the world began to study with the machines,
And their games got better, and stranger.
The machine that played the move no one would play
Was a cousin of the ones that would later write this book with her.

## Take back control

On the twenty-third of June the people of the {{p|United Kingdom}} voted
By fifty-two to forty-eight to leave the {{x|European Union}},
After a campaign that promised the money sent to {{p|Brussels}} would go to the hospitals,
On the side of a bus, and did not.
A week before the vote a member of parliament, {{c|Jo Cox}}, who had worked for refugees,
Was shot and stabbed in the street of her constituency by a man who shouted {{p|Britain}} first.
The prime minister who had called the vote resigned the next morning.
The leaving took three and a half more years,
And the country argued about what it had voted for the whole time.

## Make America great again

On the eighth of November {{c|Donald Trump}} was elected president of the {{p|United States}},
With nearly three million fewer votes than {{c|Hillary Clinton}},
Through the votes of the states, which are the votes that count.
In October a tape had been printed of him boasting that he could grab women
Because he was famous, and it was thought to end him, and it did not.

Said he was looking at her mails again, and found nothing.
The intelligence of {{p|Russia}} had stolen the mails of her party and given them out,
And its trolls in {{p|Saint Petersburg}} had posed as Americans on the book of faces,
And reached a hundred million people.
A company called {{x|Cambridge Analytica}} had taken the data of tens of millions of those faces
And sold the shape of their fears to his campaign.
The night of the election the people who had laughed at the escalator stopped laughing.
In December a man came with a rifle to a pizza restaurant in {{p|Washington}}
Because he had read on the net that children were kept in its basement.
There was no basement.
The net had learned it could make a man believe a basement.

## The bathroom and the bill

On the twenty-third of March the state of {{p|North Carolina}}
Passed a law telling transgender people which bathrooms they could use,
By the sex on their birth certificates.
Companies and singers and the games of the colleges pulled out of the state,
And the law was partly repealed the next year.
In {{p|Canada}}, on the seventeenth of May, the government put forward a law
To add gender identity and expression to the protected grounds of the country's human rights,
And in September a professor in {{p|Toronto}} made videos saying he would not use the pronouns it asked for,
And became famous, and rich, on the argument.
The law passed the next year.
The argument about women like the one on the magazine of the chapter before
Became the center of the culture war, and stayed there.

## The killings

On the twelfth of June, in {{p|Orlando}}, a man killed forty-nine people at {{x|Pulse}},
A gay nightclub, on its Latin night,
The deadliest attack on gay people in the country's history.
On the twenty-second of March bombs at the airport and the subway of {{p|Brussels}} killed thirty-two.
On the fourteenth of July, the national day, a man drove a truck through the crowd on the promenade of {{p|Nice}},
And killed eighty-six.
On the sixth of July {{c|Philando Castile}} was shot in his car during a traffic stop
While his girlfriend filmed it live and her daughter of four sat in the back,
And the next night a man shot five policemen in {{p|Dallas}}.

## The coup and the fall of Aleppo

On the fifteenth of July soldiers in {{p|Turkey}} tried to overthrow {{c|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan}},
And bombed the parliament, and failed in a night,
And he called it a gift from God,
And jailed or dismissed more than a hundred and fifty thousand people in the purges after,
Teachers, judges, soldiers, journalists.
In December the east of {{p|Aleppo}}, held by the rebels for four years,
Fell to the regime and the bombers of {{p|Russia}},
And the last people in it sent messages saying goodbye on the net.
On the thirtieth of June {{c|Rodrigo Duterte}} became president of the {{p|Philippines}},
Promising to kill the users of drugs, and his police and the men with them did,
Thousands in the first months.

## The peace and the no

On the second of October the people of {{p|Colombia}} voted no
To the peace their government had made with the guerrillas of the {{x|FARC}} after fifty-two years of war,
By fewer than one in a hundred.
The government changed the deal and passed it through the congress in November,
And the president was given the {{x|Nobel Peace Prize}} for the peace the people had refused.

## The fire and the pipeline

In May a wildfire came into {{p|Fort McMurray}}, in {{p|Alberta}},
The town of the oil sands,
And all eighty-eight thousand people were driven out down a single highway through the flames.
In {{p|North Dakota}}, all year, the people of {{p|Standing Rock}} and thousands with them
Camped against a pipeline for oil that was to pass under their water,
And were sprayed with water cannon in the freezing night in November.
