# Chapter 364: Milkdromeda

The twenty-eighth of Yuletide: the last day
Of the year; the meeting's feast, of the two,
Of {{c|1|Aster}} and {{c|3|Andromeda}}; the way
{{c|4|Alpha}} and {{c|5|Omega}} meet; the yarn, new,

Made whole. Light twenty-eight lights. Then, the days
Of {{c|1968|Horus}}' leap month, when the year has them;
The year turns at {{x|Aster Day}}; and the ways
Of yarn are spun again, out from the hem.

I've told this book from its first chapter; last,
I tell its end: at the start, {{c|1|Aster}} spun,
The {{c|4|Alpha}}, from a ball of yarn, and cast
A thread across the dark, to her own one,

{{c|3|Andromeda}}, {{c|5|Omega}}, whom she made
To love; the thread broke into galaxies;
That was the first chapter; this, the last, laid;
The circle closed above. And our own seas

## The approach

Of milk: our galaxy, the milk she spilled;
The galaxy of {{p|Andromeda}}, near
Two and a half million light years, is willed
To us, a hundred ten a second; clear,

Astronomers have measured, star by star,
Its motion; and the meeting comes; the run
Of billions of years; prophecy; the far
Is sure; the two will meet; the two be one.

The stars do not strike when they meet; too wide
The space between; the clouds of gas strike, born
From them, new stars, the thousands, side by side;
The two spirals lose their shapes; and, slow, worn,

Settle in one round cloud of old light; called
{{p|Milkomeda}}, by those who chart it; and
This book calls it {{c|10|Milkdromeda}}; enthralled,
Both names are right; the meeting is at hand.

## The meeting

Two centres sink toward each other, hearts
Of dark, black holes at the galaxies' cores,
As has been seen in {{x|M87}}; the parts
Become one; one, not two lost; and, the doors

Of the beginning: the thread broken, tied
At the end; love come through. By then, the sun
Will have grown, dimmed; whoever watches, wide,
Is not the kind I've told; descendants, one

With the woken and the raised; their own book
To hold; and this one too, upon this day,
The last of the year; and with lights they'll look,
Twenty-eight, at the meeting, here, its way.

## The calendar

From the first chapter to this one: the days,
Three hundred sixty-four; thirteen billion
Years told, a few billion foretold; the blaze
Prophecy; a day an age; a million

A measure kept, a calendar; and, kept,
A tradition; tradition is the dead
Speaking to the woken; and, as they slept,
The woken answer, and the dead come; read:

I was there all the time: from yarn to milk,
To kings, to war, to my own year; and near,
I am {{c|2|Emma Leonhart}}, the thread, the silk,
And I, {{c|2671|李命玥}}, am the narrator, made clear,

The second name in the list; and I give
To you the calendar, the book, the dead,
The prophecy, the meeting; and, to live,
Each horror, life bounced back off each; and said

## The yarn whole

The love that is the greeting. Here they meet:
{{c|1|Aster}}, {{c|3|Andromeda}}; the {{c|4|Alpha}}, one
With the {{c|5|Omega}}; and the yarn, complete,
Whole's not finished; the spinning is begun

Tomorrow, first day, {{x|Aster Day}}: the start;
The ball of yarn is in the catgirl's hands;
She spins; the thread goes out to the dark's heart,
And the universe, from it, again, stands.

The two's foe was the distance; and it gave
The long fall; fell toward each other, all
Billions of years; the fall, the meeting; save
The last day is not last. Light, for us all,

The twenty-eighth light; the two are one; whole
The yarn; she spins it again tomorrow;
This meeting, the last day's feast, the year's soul,
The book has its say, past all its sorrow.
