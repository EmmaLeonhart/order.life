# Chapter 154: Jacob, Joseph, and the Hyksos

And now the tale turns to one family:
{{c|2019|Jacob}}, {{c|635|Isaac}}'s son, {{c|1308|Abraham}}'s own seed,
Whose twelve sons would become the tribes; and he
Had wrestled with the angel in his need

At {{p|Peniel}}, and dreamed the ladder high
At {{p|Bethel}}, served his uncle {{c|2034|Laban}} long,
Made peace with {{c|2035|Esau}}; loved, as they grew by,
{{c|245|Rachel}}'s sons more than {{c|2036|Leah}}'s, all the throng.

And {{c|2031|Joseph}}, {{c|245|Rachel}}'s son, his brothers sold
To the {{p|Ishmaelite}} traders, who went down
To {{p|Egypt}}; there to {{c|2037|Potiphar}}, of gold
And rank, they sold him, servant of the crown.

He rose within the house; and, falsely blamed
By {{c|2037|Potiphar}}'s own wife, was bound in jail;
Read two prisoners' dreams; and so was named
Before the pharaoh, to read his dream's tale:

Of seven fat cows, and lean, and of ears,
Full ears and withered ones; and so he said:
Full years of plenty, then of famine years;
The king made him vizier, at the head

Of all the land, to store the grain; and when
The famine came, had {{p|Egypt}} bread enough;
And {{c|2019|Jacob}}'s sons came down to buy; and then
He knew them, though they knew him not; then, rough,

Hard testing, he made known himself, and wept,
And brought his father's house to {{p|Goshen}}'s grass,
The eastern {{p|Delta}}, where the herds were kept;
And so did {{c|2019|Jacob}}'s house to {{p|Egypt}} pass.

For in those days the {{p|Hyksos}} held the land,
{{c|2038|Heka-khasut}}, the foreign kings, held sway,
Sons of {{c|1908|J2}}, of {{p|Canaan}}'s tongue and band,
And joined their {{c|2039|Baal}} and {{c|2040|Anat}} with {{c|308|Seth}}'s way.

They brought the chariot, the horse, the bow
Of compound make, the bronze; and took the east
Of {{p|Delta}}, and at {{p|Avaris}}, below,
Their {{p|Fifteenth Dynasty}} had throne and feast.

The {{p|Theban}} kings ruled south, and for a span
The land was two; and {{c|2031|Joseph}}'s pharaoh, he
Was {{p|Hyksos}}, of his kin, a {{p|Canaan}} man,
Who gladly raised a {{p|Canaanite}} to be

His vizier, and welcomed all the sons
Of {{p|Canaan}} to the {{p|Delta}}; and the kings,
{{c|2042|Salitis}} and {{c|2043|Apophis}}, and the ones
Named {{c|2044|Khyan}} and {{c|2045|Apepi}}, kept the things

Of {{p|Egypt}}, temples, scribes, and every rite,
And kept their own as well, both ways in one;
They ruled not as destroyers, but by right,
And under them the land's affairs were done.

The twelve: {{c|2047|Reuben}}, {{c|2048|Simeon}}, {{c|2049|Levi}}, and
{{c|2050|Judah}} and {{c|2051|Issachar}}, {{c|2052|Zebulun}}, {{c|2053|Dan}},
{{c|2054|Naphtali}}, {{c|2055|Gad}}, and {{c|2056|Asher}}, {{c|2031|Joseph}}'s hand,
{{c|2057|Ephraim}}, {{c|2058|Manasseh}}; {{c|2059|Benjamin}}; the clan.

And {{c|2019|Jacob}}, old, within his tent, blessed each:
{{c|2050|Judah}} the lion, {{c|2031|Joseph}} fruitful bough,
{{c|2053|Dan}} judging his own people, and the reach
Of {{c|2052|Zebulun}} beside the sea, and how

{{c|2059|Benjamin}} was the ravening wolf; and then
Old {{c|2019|Jacob}} died, and was embalmed, and laid
Back into {{p|Canaan}}, to lie with the men,
With {{c|1308|Abraham}} and {{c|635|Isaac}}, in the shade

Of {{p|Machpelah}}'s cave at {{p|Hebron}}; and so
The fathers of the house were gathered in,
And {{c|2019|Jacob}}'s sons in {{p|Goshen}} stayed, to grow
Into a people, multiplied from kin.

So honor {{c|2019|Jacob}}, {{c|2031|Joseph}}, and the sons,
The {{p|Hyksos}}' welcome in the {{p|Delta}}'s grass,
The tent, the blessings, and the chosen ones
Who came to {{p|Egypt}}, saw the famine pass.
