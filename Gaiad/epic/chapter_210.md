# Chapter 210: The Kenmu Restoration

In thirteen-thirty-three, {{c|3131|Go-Daigo}} rose,
Emperor; and the {{c|2928|Shogunate}} he fought;
With samurai who turned: among all those,
{{c|3141|Ashikaga Takauji}}, who brought

And {{c|3142|Kusunoki Masashige}} came too;
The {{c|3121|Hōjō}} fell; {{x|Kamakura}} was won;
A hundred fifty years of warriors, through;
{{c|3131|Go-Daigo}} entered {{p|Kyoto}}; it was done.

Then there came the {{c|3132|Kenmu Restoration}}:
His own first minister, he gave the lands
And posts; but he favored court and station,
Above the samurai, who'd won; their hands

Had hoped for {{c|3121|Hōjō}} lands, and they were spurned;
{{c|3146|Takauji}}, who'd the {{c|3121|Hōjō}} betrayed,
The {{c|3121|Hōjō}}, now {{c|3131|Go-Daigo}}; and he turned,
In thirteen-thirty-six, took {{p|Kyoto}}; made

A rival of the junior line his own;
And {{c|3131|Go-Daigo}} fled to {{p|Yoshino}}, south,
Up in the mountains, and he claimed the throne;
Two courts, for fifty-six years, mouth to mouth:

The {{c|3143|Ashikaga}}'s North, in {{p|Kyoto}}'s halls;
The South at {{p|Yoshino}}; {{c|3144|Nanboku-chō}},
{{x|Northern and Southern Courts}}; and each one calls
Itself the line; the samurai chose, so.

{{c|3142|Kusunoki Masashige}}, the most true
Of all {{c|3131|Go-Daigo}}'s generals, who'd won
By ambush against the many, and knew
At {{x|Minatogawa}} he would be undone,

And fought; and after it, with his brother,
He took his life, the warrior's way; his son
Had sworn to fight; he, above all other,
Became the emblem of the loyal one.

So {{c|3146|Takauji}} made his {{c|3147|Shogunate}}
At {{x|Muromachi}}, in {{p|Kyoto}}; but weak
Than {{x|Kamakura}}'s; its writ, its state,
Reached central {{p|Japan}}; the {{c|3148|shugo}} would seek

Their own; the shogun's word they would ignore.
And under them the {{c|3149|Zen}} grew; and the art
Of {{c|3150|tea}}, the {{c|3151|sadō}}, was refined; and more,
The {{c|3153|Noh}}, by {{c|3154|Zeami}} made, and set apart;

And {{c|3155|ink painting}}, the {{c|3156|sumi-e}}, there came
From {{x|Song}} {{p|China}}; thirteen-ninety-seven,
The {{c|3157|Kinkaku-ji}}, in gold leaf's bright flame,
Built by {{c|3158|Yoshimitsu}}, rose to heaven;

And {{c|3160|Yoshimasa}}, later, built with art
The {{c|3159|Ginkaku-ji}}, subtler, and less bright,
The {{p|Silver Pavilion}}, the quiet heart;
And all of it beneath a failing might.

The {{c|3132|Kenmu Restoration}} failed; and so
{{c|3131|Go-Daigo}} is remembered as the brave
Who tried what could not be, so long ago:
Restorer, failed, heroic, to the grave.
