# Chapter 16: Arka's Line and the First Temples

From {{c|206|Luca}}'s lineage, two daughters sprang,
{{c|208|Arka}} and {{c|209|Bacta}}, shaped to change the world;
In marshy cradles, where their voices rang,
Their stories intertwined, their fates unfurled.

{{c|208|Arka}}, the fearless, ventured far and wide,
Exploring {{c|202|Terra}}'s depths and mysteries;
Her courage blazed, a beacon and a guide,
Upon her path, the sisters placed their pleas.

And {{c|209|Bacta}}, strong, adaptable, and keen,
Faced trials and change with steady resilience;
In her, the sisters found a fortress sheen,
A bastion of strength and firm compliance.

United, these descendants of {{c|24|Bios}}' grace,
{{c|210|Embarked}} upon their journey through the strife;
With {{c|208|Arka}}'s questing soul and {{c|209|Bacta}}'s pace,
They forged ahead to carve a future life.

One moonlit eve, as {{c|201|Luna}} graced the sky,
Her voice rang clear to {{c|206|Luca}}, filled with light;
A prophecy she whispered from on high,
To share with both her daughters in the night.

"My child, your daughters shall divide the {{c|96|Earth}};
Two realms will rise beneath their guiding hands.
From your own lineage, life shall find rebirth,
A testament to your creation's plans.

{{c|208|Arka}}'s domain shall spread with wandering feet,
Exploring lands unseen by mortal eyes;
Her children brave and bold shall fears defeat,
Discovering where hidden beauty lies.

And {{c|209|Bacta}}'s kin, resilient they'll remain,
{{c|211|Adapting}} to the ever-changing world;
They'll hold their ground through hardship and through pain,
Their legacy's banner never furled."

With {{c|201|Luna}}'s words, a promise made to keep,
{{c|206|Luca}}, both hopeful and yet filled with care,
Gazed at her daughters, pondering paths deep,
Of two great realms their destinies would bear.

From north to south, these sacred temples stood,
In distant lands, their purpose to fulfill;
With fervent prayers and rites, they understood
The bond between their people and {{c|96|Earth}}'s will.

In {{c|212|Isua}}, where {{c|213|Greenland}}'s cold winds blow,
And {{c|214|Acasta}}'s {{c|215|Northwest}} {{c|216|Territories}},
The Great {{c|217|Temple}} thrived in ice and snow,
A testament to ancient legacies.

While far away, the {{c|218|Zircon}} {{c|217|Temple}} gleamed
In {{c|204|Jack}} {{c|205|Hills}} under {{c|219|Australian}} sun;
And {{c|220|Barberton}}, where life first stirred and dreamed,
The temples' influence had just begun.

From {{c|208|Arka}} sprang a lineage of might:
{{c|221|Dipana}}, {{c|222|Yuri}}, {{c|223|Proteus}} arose;
Their daughters ventured forth into the light,
To every corner where the wind now blows.

{{c|221|Dipana}}'s kin, a multitude so grand,
{{c|224|Inhabited}} {{c|96|Earth}}'s edges far and wide;
{{c|222|Yuri}} {{c|225|Archaea}}, leading her band—
{{c|226|Methana}}, {{c|227|Halo}}, {{c|228|Pyra}} at her side.

{{c|226|Methana}}, consort to {{c|229|Methanius}} fair,
In methane's arts her mastery was shown;
{{c|227|Halo}} the {{c|230|Purple}}, with seductive flair,
Won {{c|231|Salta}}, {{c|61|Sol}}, and {{c|232|Carotenus}}' throne.

Deep purple hues she harnessed from the sun,
With {{c|232|Carotenus}}' colors intertwined;
In salty realms, her daughters dwelt as one,
While {{c|223|Proteus}}' offspring spread unconfined.

Among them, {{c|233|Asgarda}} held the throne,
And from her sprang a million daughters strong:
{{c|234|Heimdalla}}, {{c|235|Odina}}, {{c|236|Thora}} known,
With {{c|237|Lokia}} and {{c|238|Hela}} in their throng.

{{c|234|Heimdalla}}, watchful guardian of the realm;
{{c|235|Odina}}, keeper of all wisdom's light;
And {{c|236|Thora}}, whose great strength could overwhelm;
While {{c|237|Lokia}} stirred up chaos and delight.

Then {{c|238|Hela}}, dwelling in the shadow's veil,
Her secrets buried deep within the night;
An epic lineage woven without fail,
Their tales of strength and cunning brought to sight.

An epic lineage, these daughters bold,
Their stories echo through the halls of time;
From {{c|206|Luca}}'s line, their destinies unfold,
A tapestry of life, profound, sublime.

Through trials faced and victories hard-won,
They forged ahead to shape their universe;
The legacy of {{c|24|Bios}} carried on,
In every deed, in every whispered verse.

With {{c|201|Luna}}'s elixir waiting in the skies,
They journeyed forth, their faith forever strong;
Awaiting when the stars would align their ties,
To drink immortal life and join the song.

Across the cosmos, endless paths they roam,
The daughters of the {{c|96|Earth}} and {{c|198|Moon}} combined;
Forever seeking, ever moving home,
Their fates with threads of destiny entwined.
