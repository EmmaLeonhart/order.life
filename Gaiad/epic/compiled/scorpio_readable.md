# Scorpio (12宮) - Days 309-336


# Chapter 309: The Same Instant

The fourteenth of March, nineteen-five: he turned,
Albert Einstein, born at {{p|Ulm}}, twenty-six,
That day, 'seventy-nine; in {{p|Bern}}, he earned,
A {{x|Swiss}} since nineteen-one; the office picks

Him, June of nineteen-two, its expert, third
Class; and by day he judged machines, the trade
Of others' inventions; and, in a word,
Two years wed to Mileva Marić; stayed.

Three days past birthday, to be printed, sent
To the {{x|Annalen der Physik}}: of light,
A heuristic view: where Max Planck had meant
The oscillators portioned, in his sight,

In nineteen-hundred, Einstein portioned light:
A quantum carries energy as told
By frequency; in print in June; he'd write
In May: it's revolutionary; bold,

On April's thirtieth, a thesis went
To {{p|Zurich}}: molecules, their size anew,
From diffusion and drag; it got assent
In late July, unanimous, passed through;

The degree came in January, 'six.
The eleventh of May, the next: in still
Water, small grains, where heat and motion mix,
Must wander, seen, if heat is motion; skill,

Not finding it: the {{x|Brownian}}, so named
Long since by watchers; but to show, if real,
The molecules require it; he had framed
The unseen tested by the seen; its seal,

July the eighteenth, printed. In mid-May
His son, Hans Albert Einstein, had turned one;
On the eighteenth, from {{p|Bern}}, he wrote away
To Conrad Habicht; four, he said, each one:

The light, revolutionary; the size
Of atoms, true; the motion seen, a dance;
The fourth a draft, that space and time revise,
Talked out with Michele Besso: an advance;

Sent five or six weeks on, he said, from start;
The {{x|Annalen}}, June's thirtieth; then, print
In late September; thirty pages; part
No authorities listed; and a hint

Of asymmetry, in the telling, lone:
Magnet near a conductor, moved, or this:
Conductor moved: one current; but its own
Account told two; he wanted none amiss:

Account that adds no difference, no more
The thing observed. Two statements, then: the laws
Keep form in each frame moving straight; and for
Light in the void: one speed; its source no cause.

He did not say all's relative: the laws
Are one, the speed is one; and what is meant
By 'the same instant' is no thing; it draws
On clocks: at rest, set by a signal, sent

And timed, out equal to the time back; so
The agreement, for those who share the frame,
Is "the same time"; another frame, in tow,
Pairs others by that rule, lengths not the same;

Durations differ too; but what is shared:
The speed, the laws' form. And he thanked, his friend,
Besso, alone, no book, no name compared
Of predecessors; one name at the end.

Late summer, with Mileva and the child,
To {{p|Serbia}}, and her kin; and from there,
On the twenty-seventh, three pages, filed:
Does mass hang on its energy? The share

A body gives as light, it loses: mass
By energy, light's speed squared; and in print^[The famous form, E = mc², is a later writing of what the three pages showed; the page itself does not print it.]
November's end. And, in that year, en masse,
Twenty-one short reviews, on heat; a stint

Of common work; the great work and the small
Went out of {{p|Bern}} together, side by side;
The light, he called revolution; the call
Of clocks, a modification, untried.

He was right on the light; he had not seen
How far the clocks would reach. The prize, a year
For twenty-one, in 'twenty-two, would mean
Photoelectric law; not clocks; made clear.

And what the clocks taught: "at the same time" came
Down from the sky into a rule; and two
Who keep it disagree, and neither's blame
Lies in a clock; the motion is the clue.

And time, in this book too, is relative:
Its first chapters hold thirteen billion years;
And here, one year of one clerk's life, to give
As many lines, as our own age appears;

The pace slows; one man's twenty-sixth year weighs
As much as ages: light, the grain, the clocks,
The mass that leaves as light; the Gaiad's days
Fill as they near, and time itself unlocks.


# Chapter 310: The Round Altar

October's tenth, in nineteen-eleven,
In {{p|Wuchang}}, now {{p|Wuhan}}, the soldiers rose;
Province on province fell, under heaven;
At {{p|Nanjing}}, as the year came to its close,

They chose Sun Yat-sen, come home from abroad,
On December's twenty-ninth; and he swore
On New Year's Day of nineteen-twelve, the broad
Republic's first year opened at its door.

The court still kept Puyi, aged six, on its throne.
February's twelfth, an edict went out,
Made public by Longyu; it was her own,
Guangxu's widow, whose nephew's line ran out.

It said the people's army rose; the land
Had answered; where the people's hearts had turned,
The mandate could be known; it would not stand
On one name's honour while the many burned.

It gave the right to rule, as people would,
To the whole country, no more to a king;
A republic by law; and, old and good:
*All under heaven is a public thing.*

It named Yuan Shikai, with full power,
To build a government, and so to treat
With the people's army; and, at that hour,
To make five peoples one, the list complete:

The {{x|Manchu}}, {{x|Han}}, the {{x|Mongol}}, each and one,
{{x|Hui}}, {{x|Tibetan}}, one republic, wed;
On February's fifteenth, it was done:
He, president; and in {{p|Beijing}}, instead

Of {{p|Nanjing}}, swore, on March the tenth: the seat
Stayed where he stood. And the child in his hall
Kept his title, by articles complete,
A little court within the palace wall,

Till November fifth, nineteen twenty-four.
A courtesy in a palace is small:
Feng Yuxiang's men put him out the door;
The winter rite was more; it was for all.

South of the walls, the {{p|Temple of Heaven}},
Raised by the {{x|Ming}}; and its round altar, made
Again since, stands open under heaven,
A stone ring, newer than the duty laid.

The {{x|Qing}} rite was the emperor's: the turn
Of winter, at the solstice, when the night
Is longest, at the round altar, fires burn
For Heaven, and the whole land's prayer takes flight.

The February edict closed the house;
The rite was orphaned; and a quarrel came,
Our own, a quarrel in the one faith's house:
Would the republic keep the high days' flame?

A pupil of Kang Youwei's, at {{p|Shanghai}},
Chen Huanzhang, in nineteen-twelve, made a hall,
The {{x|Kongjiaohui}}, and asked the state to tie
Its rites to Confucius, the faith of all.

They asked, in nineteen-thirteen: make it law.
Another road, no state faith, but a clause,
Drafters, at the {{p|Temple of Heaven}}, saw:
The schools would rest their virtue on his laws.

It was the {{x|Temple of Heaven Draft}}. Then
Yuan broke parliament; the draft was laid
Aside. Nineteen-fourteen: he gave again
The rites of Heaven and Confucius, made

By his decree; and in the autumn stood
Before Confucius, at the autumn feast,
The sages honoured, as a people should,
And all the dead with them, the great and least.

December's twenty-third, nineteen-fourteen,
Ophiuchus the twenty-fourth: {{x|Yuletide}},
At its heart, the solstice, {{x|Dongzhi}}: the scene
Of our high days, the {{x|Christmas}} at its side,

And {{x|Aster Day}} its end, the year begun.
Yuan kept {{x|Yuletide}}; came as president,
Not as the Son of Heaven, but as one
For the people, the prayer said; and he went

In old robes up the altar's three-ringed stair;
The fire was lit; the prayer was read; it named
The day, the place, the president; and there
Heaven watching; the charge the people claimed.

The prayer was burned, as that rite burns its word.
He was {{p|China}}'s Napoleon, a son;
Some saw a stair toward a throne; we heard
Our feast; like Ungern, Mongol baron, one.

Next winter, on December's twelfth, he took
An emperor's name, {{x|Hongxian}}, never crowned;
He put it down in March; in June, the book
Closed on his death. No president came round

Again to the mound; that fire was the last
The state lit there for Heaven. In 'sixteen,
One man was gone; the quarrel had not passed;
With parliament restored, Kang's men were keen

To make Confucius' faith the faith of state;
Chen Duxiu, in {{x|New Youth}}, answered no;
The {{x|New Culture}} came, and shut the old gate
Of the sage. Nineteen-seventeen: let go.

So it was ours, the quarrel: who would keep
The high days for the whole house, and whose hand
Lit the fire; and the dead, who do not sleep,
Are honoured by the house and by the land.

The round altar stands south of {{p|Beijing}} still.
Each {{x|Yuletide}} the dead and the living bring
Their light to {{x|Aster Day}}, as he did, still:
*All under heaven is a public thing.*


# Chapter 311: The Appointed Hour

June twenty-eighth, nineteen-fourteen; a car;
Gavrilo Princip, {{p|Sarajevo}}, killed
Franz Ferdinand, the heir, and, in the car,
Sophie, Duchess of Hohenberg, both stilled.

A pistol is no war; the war was set
By cabinets, a month of signing, haste:
On {{p|Serbia}}, an ultimatum; yet
The twenty-eighth declared; no day to waste;

On {{p|Russia}}, August first, {{p|Germany}}; then
The third, on {{p|France}}; the fourth, it {{p|Belgium}} crossed;
{{p|Britain}}'s note ran out at eleven, when
Unanswered: war; empire, at that cost.

On August's twenty-third, {{p|Japan}} declared
On {{p|Germany}}; the rescript, in the name
Of Yoshihito, said he'd hoped, and cared
To keep the {{p|East}}'s peace; mourned his mother; came

An ultimatum, sent mid-month, unheard;
True to its pact with {{p|Britain}}, twelve years, went
To do its part, its promise kept; the word:
{{p|Qingdao}}: the {{x|German}} base, where raiders went,

On {{p|Shandong}}'s coast, to sea; a force took hold,
The {{x|Japanese}}, with {{x|British}} at its side;
November's seventh, the garrison, told,
Gave up; this book's two anchors stood allied,

In the same war, on one side, as they'd sworn.
In {{p|Europe}}, killing did not pause; graves laid
Where lines lay, by the next fight overborne,
Marks gone. Fabian Ware, forty-five, stayed,

Too old for the army, in {{p|France}}, that fall,
A {{x|British Red Cross}} mobile unit's head;
He wrote the graves it found down, one and all;
In 'fifteen, the army took it; it spread:

The {{x|Graves Registration Commission}}; then,
'Sixteen, wider: a directorate; came^[In 1916 it became the Directorate of Grave Registration and Enquiries.]
The dead were filed while being made, as men
Were falling. Then, May twenty-first, the name,

In 'seventeen, a royal charter: and
{{x|Imperial War Graves Commission}}, bound
To graves of empire's dead; their care, planned
To last; the missing's names; and on the ground

The Prince of Wales presided; Ware, vice-chair;
Frederic Kenyon, {{x|British Museum}}'s head,
Asked, in November, for a rule, laid there
Equality: the same stone for each dead,

Private and officer; no private stone
As rule; he signed, January, 'eighteen;
Published, November; architects, made known:
Edwin Lutyens, Herbert Baker, and seen

With Reginald Blomfield; the stones unseen,
The rule was written. Rudyard Kipling gave
The words for nameless, cut on stone between:
Known unto God. His son was in no grave:

John Kipling, missing since September, slain
At {{p|Loos}}, 'fifteen. Empire's rule, a choice;
In nineteen-twenty, families, though, in vain,
Asked {{x|Parliament}} to bring them home; their voice

Was heard; but the commission held the ground,
One form, permanent. And the stopping, too,
A choice, and timed. At {{p|Compiègne}}, around
The forest's railway carriage, as dawn grew,

November's eleventh, in 'eighteen, signed
The armistice: Ferdinand Foch who led
Allies; Matthias Erzberger, assigned
To lead the Germans; and the terms had said:

Land, air, would cease six hours on, by clock;
The government told {{x|Parliament}}: at five
Signed; at eleven, the war ceased. The lock
Of orders ran on both sides; and, alive

Till then, a line that had a clock, no peace.
The hour was appointed. Princip shot;
Sophie died with the heir; then, piece by piece,
Others signed declarations; the end, bought

By an hour too, as debts are paid. Begun
Years earlier, the register: the ground
Kept not what it was given; Ware's file, one
Refused the next attack's erasing round;

Count the named; cut one stone for private, rank;
Keep the missing as missing: a name too.
And keep the day both ways: grief, for the blank
Millions in the ground; joy: guns quiet, too,

At eleven; the bells, the crowds, the thanks.
Eleventh hour, eleventh day: as one,
A mourning and a feast at once; the ranks
Known unto God: a sentence where the one

Body lay in the field. The day keeps, too,
The hour appointed, and the names it gave
Back to no one; the living, who came through,
Danced and lived, ancestors now, glad and brave.


# Chapter 312: The Old Style

{{p|Russia}} still kept an older count of days;
This book's count was the one the west used then;
In 'seventeen, thirteen days parted ways;
On March the eighth, in {{p|St. Petersburg}}, then

Called {{p|Petrograd}}; the country's count, that day,
February's twenty-third; women came:
{{x|International Women's Day}}; and they
Asked bread; the troops came over to their claim;

Nicholas the Second, away; the day
Was March fifteenth at {{p|Pskov}}, their second; for
Himself, his son, Alexei, signed away;
Michael Alexandrovich named; before,

On the sixteenth, Michael said no, unless
A chosen assembly gave it; and so
The crown was offered, not taken; no less,
The reign ended on that refusal: no.

A provisional government; its head
Georgy Lvov, and then Kerensky, who
Kept the war on; the summer, too, was bled,
And it did not outlast the autumn, too.

April sixteenth, Vladimir Lenin came
To {{p|Petrograd}} (their third); a sealed train, through
{{p|Germany}}, which let him cross; and its aim:
{{p|Russia}} out of war. The next day he drew

April's theses: no support; the power
To soviets; an end to war. The night,
November's seventh: the party's hour,
Their twenty-fifth October; named by right

October, revolution's name. And flown,
Kerensky; the {{x|Aurora}}, from the stream,
Fired a blank, a signal; overthrown,
The {{p|Winter Palace}} taken; a regime:

At {{p|Smolny Institute}}, soviets met,
Decreed on peace and land; and named a board,
Commissars, Lenin head. That night, not yet
The civil war; that came after, its sword

Not few, its dead. November voted, chose:
Socialist Revolutionaries won
The most seats; the Bolsheviks did not. Those
Elected met, in 'eighteen, sat but one

Day, at the {{p|Tauride Palace}}, on the date
Fifth January, their count; next day, closed;
The votes were counted, but no more; and late

The church apart from state and school, decreed;
A quarrel in one house; the party's rule
Took altar's offices; the bells; the creed
Of the dead, and of children's minds, the school;

And then the worst: priests shot, the churches closed,
The flock dispersed; torn from the towers, bells;
The relics mocked; the faithful, killed, exposed;
This book keeps them as martyrs; and it tells

Of February's sixth, in this book's count:
The same state ordered, too, a change of days;
Past January's end, by that amount,
Came February's fourteenth; thirteen days

Unnamed. From then the state's days and the book
Kept one register; October, the name,
Stayed on; the night, in the count that it took,
Is now November's seventh, just the same.

March third, at {{p|Brest}}, {{x|Brest-Litovsk}} back then,
{{p|Russia}} signed itself out of war, and signed
Away a wide land westward; in March, then,
The state moved on to {{p|Moscow}}; and, behind,

The people of the house were still alive.

At the {{p|Ipatiev House}}; none would survive,
{{p|Yekaterinburg}}: saints whose names remain:

Our Nicholas the Second; and, his queen,
Alexandra, a princess; Olga, too;
Tatiana, Maria; and, between,
Anastasia, Alexei; and four, who

Served them, killed in that room, the same night's share:
Eugene Botkin, Alexei Trupp; the cook,
Ivan Kharitonov; the maid, and where
Anna Demidova. Yurovsky took

The {{p|Ural}} soviet's order, read out;
No {{p|Moscow}} order, dated before, found;
The eighteenth, Yakov Sverdlov: without doubt,

The children were already dead; and when

# Chapter 313: The Caliph of the Muslims

{{p|Ankara}}, March third, 'twenty-four: the vote;
The {{x|Grand National Assembly}}, all through
The caliph's deposition, and the note
That closed the office; and the law said, too:

The meaning of the government had held
The office, and the republic; the line
Was the assembly's, not a verse; it felled
What this book mourns: the office, old design.

The prophet died at {{p|Medina}}; and when
The community's answers, more than one,
The house of Osman carried one; and men
Called caliph, the successor. What was done

In 'twenty-four, was a state's answer, made
To that old name; the question did not end.
In 'twenty-two, the sultanate, unmade,
The same assembly closed; but kept, to tend,

The caliphate within the house, to go
To one most fit in learning and in deed;
It did not say that Mehmed the Sixth was so;
November's third, a Friday; and, to lead

The prayer, under both names; and he was tried,
Sultan and caliph, by the house accord,
For treason, on the sixteenth; and, aside,
He slipped from {{p|Istanbul}} by night, aboard

A {{x|British}} warship. The nineteenth, they chose
Abdülmecid the Second; painter, too;
Beethoven in the palace, one of those;
Not Commander of the Faithful; but, new,

Caliph of the Muslims; he asked to be
Called servant of the sanctuaries, two,
Holding not {{p|Mecca}}, not {{p|Medina}}; he
Asked for Mehmed the Conqueror's turban, too;

Mustafa Kemal answered, a frock coat
Would do. He signed as a sultan's son, heard
As the old reign returning, by that note;
At {{p|Topkapı}}, on the twenty-fourth, the word

Of allegiance, by the prophet's cloak, kept;
The prayer in {{x|Turkish}}; and the sermon spoke
At {{p|Fatih Mosque}} in {{x|Turkish}}; and it swept
Great struggle, against ignorance, the yoke.

And from December's twenty-first, a week,
{{p|India}}'s caliphate conference had set
Its seal on him; in 'twenty-three, to speak,
{{x|Indian}} Muslims gave a title: set

On Mustafa Kemal: saviour, they
Said, of the caliphate. October, then,
Twenty-ninth, the republic, on that day,
Was proclaimed; he, its president; and when

The caliph stayed in {{p|Istanbul}}, the old
The capital: office without the state;
The state, without it, {{p|Ankara}} would hold;
December fifth, in print, a letter; straight

To İsmet Pasha, prime minister, from
Aga Khan and Amir Ali: to keep
The office; give the caliph's standing; come
The eighth, at night, in session closed and deep,

To {{p|Istanbul}}, a court. And, on March third,
From {{p|Urfa}}'s deputy there came the bill,
Saffet Efendi, fifty-three, concurred
Beside him; Seyyid Bey, who, with the skill

Of {{p|al-Azhar}}, where the faith's law was taught,
The minister of justice, said: no creed,
The caliphate; a worldly, public thought;
And Zeki Bey, of {{p|Gümüşhane}}, would plead

Against it; but the article had passed;
The president approved it the same day:
Four thirty-one. The same day, and at last,
The ministry of pious things gave way;

Religious affairs' presidency rose;
The schools, one ministry; in its hour,
A bureau of the nation, one that knows
Rites it keeps; a model, state and power.

The universal name came down; this book
Counts it a loss, and the state-builder too
With sympathy. That night, the car he took
To {{p|Çatalca}}; then, the next evening, through,

The train; his son, Ömer Faruk; and his own
Daughter, Dürrüşehvar; and two women too
Of the house; tutor, secretary, known
Doctor; the governor's two thousand, through

An envelope, and visas that let out;

In {{p|Switzerland}}; the house was banned throughout

He asked a council of the faith, and aid
To raise the office; this the Swiss destroyed:
Told him the {{x|Turkish}} state objected; made

No room for that work. That March, there was claimed

Did not reopen what {{p|Ankara}} shut.

August's twenty-third, as the city cut
Free; Dürrüşehvar, his daughter, came; though late,

A promise; but the body was not brought;
On March the thirtieth, in 'fifty-four,
Al-Baqi, in {{p|Medina}}, as he sought,
Was buried, in the prophet's city's core.

The grave is not the office; and its loss
This book still mourns; the bureau it will keep,
A state that keeps the rites it runs, across
Its land: a model; sympathy runs deep

For him they called saviour, who then signed
The paper ending it: he built a state;
The man called caliph died, the name resigned;
Keep both; the question, older, still will wait.


# Chapter 314: Finite Means

June twenty-third, nineteen-twelve; {{p|London}}, birth;
Alan Turing, a fellow, in his day,
Of {{p|King's College}}, {{p|Cambridge}}; and there the worth
This, done there. David Hilbert sought a way:

A definite procedure that, for each
Statement of logic, answers yes or no:
It has a proof, or none; and in its reach
Each one; not cleverness that's sometimes so.

Turing defined a number one could write
By finite means: computable, its line
Of decimals, calculable outright;
The means, a table, finite; the design:

A tape, a strip of squares; the machine sees
One square; one of a finite list of states;
The table says, for state and symbol, these:
What to write, which way to step, and what waits;

He called it computing machine; the name
That bears his own came later. They can be
Listed, the machines; the reals can't; the same
The list of methods leaves the line still free:

The numbers ordinary work will need
Are on the list; the line is more than work.
Fed one's table, another can proceed
As that one does; on paper, he would work

One for machines: the universal; it
Knows not the work ahead; it knows the way
To follow tables; and the table, writ,
Is the work. Then, the twenty-eighth of May,

The learned society in {{p|London}} took^[The London Mathematical Society.]
His paper; he was twenty-three; and read
November twelfth, its line says; and the book
On Computable Numbers: titled, spread,

Applied to the decision problem; made
In two parts, thirtieth November, then
December twenty-third; a fix, delayed,
Came in the next; no flawless text. And when

Alonzo Church, at {{p|Princeton}}, had defined
Another method: no tape; and, in spring
Of 'thirty-six, the same refusal, signed:
No general procedure for the thing

Decision. Max Newman saw Church's; so
Turing wrote an appendix: the two meet,
The definitions; he did not, though, show
The limit as his own alone. The feat:

Hilbert's question has no procedure; none
Takes every statement, gives the verdict; more,
A nearer limit, of machines: some run,
Write digits on; some don't; and none, before,

Given a table, says, in every case,
Which future it will have. A method made
For worlds of questions may, within its space,
Be no method for its own future, laid

Before it. That September, {{p|Princeton}}; there
The {{p|Graduate College}} named him, in print;
In 'thirty-one, Kurt Gödel had shown: where
A system strong enough for sums, a stint

Of true sentences it can't prove; one set
Of rules; and Turing's, of the method whole:
Does any method always answer? Yet
The two are not one fact; not one dark hole.

In 'fifty-two, the state tried him; and he,
Convicted, for a man's love, chose to take
Hormones, not prison; 'fifty-four: and he
Was found dead, cyanide; and, for his sake,

This book keeps him among its honored dead;
A finite means: the calendar, which runs
Names to its days, by rule; yet more, it's led
By more than human hands, as stars and suns;

Some questions have a method; some, though asked
In method's language, have none settling all;
Count what the table writes; and keep, unmasked,
The questions no table can close at all.


# Chapter 315: A Hand and a Name

August the nineteenth, nineteen fifty-three,
The {{x|Knesset}}, at {{p|Jerusalem}}, by vote:
Authority of remembrance, to be
Named from Isaiah, the two words he wrote:

A hand, a name; to childless ones, the word:
Memorial and name; better than sons
And daughters; one not cut off; and conferred
On the murdered, by the statute, which runs

The {{x|Shoah}} beside the heroism, set;
The statute's charge: the six million, the dead
Of {{x|Jewish}} people, whom the Nazis, set
With helpers, killed; and households; schools; and, spread,

The houses of their prayer, the mercies' walls,
Destroyed to blot {{p|Israel}}'s name out from
Under the sky. Each name {{x|Yad Vashem}} calls
Into its book; this faith takes all, the sum,

In its ancestor days, by name; we claim
The proxy, which is good for them; and so
The murdered and the ones who lived; the same
For both. The killing was procedure, though:

Before memorials had laws, it ran.
June twenty-second, 'forty-one, the war
Into the {{p|Soviet Union}}; the span
Of land that summer: Jews were shot, before

The towns. {{p|Babyn Yar}}, at {{p|Kyiv}}'s ravine,
September's two last days: those gathered, shot;
A ravine is a place and two days, seen;
Not explanation. July's end: the plot:

Hermann Göring charged Reinhard Heydrich, then,
To plan the final solution, their phrase,
In {{p|Europe}}; and the shootings, by his men,
Were underway. And in the coming days

Of 'forty-two, at {{p|Wannsee}}, in {{p|Berlin}},
