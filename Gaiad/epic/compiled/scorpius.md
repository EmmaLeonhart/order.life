# Chapter 309: The Same Instant

On the fourteenth of March, 1905, {{c|Albert Einstein}} turned twenty-six.
He had been born in {{p|Ulm}} on that day in 1879.
He was in {{p|Bern}}.
He had been a Swiss citizen since the twenty-first of February, 1901.
On the nineteenth of June, 1902, the federal patent office there
Had chosen him technical expert of the third class.
By day he judged other people's machines.
The office was a register of inventions.

On the sixth of January it had been two years
Since he married {{c|Mileva Marić}}.

Three days after the birthday he sent a paper to the Annalen der Physik.
The journal received it on the eighteenth of March,
And printed it on the ninth of June.
On a heuristic point of view
Concerning the production and transformation of light.
{{c|Max Planck}}, in 1900, had portioned the energy of the oscillators
That absorb and emit.
Einstein portioned the light itself.
A quantum of it carries an energy proportional to the frequency of the light.
He did not, in March, call the paper revolutionary.
That word comes in May, in a letter, and it is for this paper.

On the thirtieth of April he submitted a dissertation
To the University of {{p|Zurich}}.
A new determination of molecular dimensions.
The size of a molecule, taken from diffusion
And from the drag inside a dilute solution.
On the twenty-seventh of July the Philosophy II faculty approved it,
Unanimously.
The degree was dated the fifteenth of January, 1906.
Through the months of the papers, he was not yet formally the doctor.

On the eleventh of May the Annalen received the next.
Particles on the order of a thousandth of a millimeter,
Suspended in a liquid that is otherwise still,
Must already wander so that the wander can be seen,
If heat is the motion of molecules.
Physiologists had watched that wander and named it Brownian.
He had not discovered the motion.
He showed that molecules, if they are real, require it,
And he set down the measure of the wander,
So that a thing too small to see could be tested by a thing that is seen.
The journal printed that paper on the eighteenth of July.

On the fourteenth of May his son, {{c|Hans Albert Einstein}}, turned one.

On the eighteenth of May he wrote from Bern to {{c|Conrad Habicht}},
Who was no longer in the city.
He promised four papers.
The first, on radiation and the energy of light, he called very revolutionary.
The second, the true size of atoms.
The third, the visible random motion.
The fourth, he said, lay only in draft:
An electrodynamics of moving bodies,
By a modification of the teaching of space and time.

He worked that fourth paper out in talk with {{c|Michele Besso}},
At the same office.
He later said he sent it five or six weeks after the idea came.
The Annalen received it on the thirtieth of June.
On the electrodynamics of moving bodies.
Printed the twenty-sixth of September.
About thirty pages. No list of authorities.

It opens on an asymmetry that sits in the description only.
A magnet moving near a conductor,
And a conductor moving near a magnet,
Produce the same current.
The usual account tells the two as if they were different kinds of happening.
He wanted an account that does not add a difference
The observable thing does not have.

Two statements carry the rest.
The laws take the same form in every frame
That moves at a steady speed in a straight line.
Light in empty space has one speed,
And the speed does not depend on the motion of the source.
He did not say that everything is relative.
He said the laws are the same, and the speed is one speed,
And therefore the same instant is not a property of the world
Apart from a procedure.

The procedure is clocks.
Clocks at rest with respect to one another are set by a light signal,
The time out taken to equal the time back.
That agreement is "at the same time" for the people who share the frame.
Another frame, in uniform motion, using the same rule,
Does not pair the same events.
The length of a duration is not shared either.
What is shared is the speed, and the form of the laws.

The paper names no book and no predecessor.
It ends by thanking Besso, friend and colleague,
For his help, and for suggestions.
One living name. No bibliography.

In the late summer Einstein went with Mileva and the child to {{p|Serbia}},
To friends, and to her family.
On the twenty-seventh of September the Annalen received three pages.
Does the inertia of a body depend upon its energy content?
Printed the twenty-first of November.
If a body gives off energy as light, its mass lessens
By that energy divided by the square of the speed of light.
The page does not contain the line the world later printed.
The line is a later writing of what the three pages showed.

In the same year the Annalen's supplement carried twenty-one short reviews
He had written, of papers on heat.
The work he called revolutionary, and the ordinary notice of other work,
Went out of the same months in Bern.

Keep the distinction he made on the eighteenth of May.
The countable light, he called revolutionary.
The clocks, he called a modification, and they were still a draft.
A person can misjudge which of his own papers will be the one that is kept.
He was not wrong about the light.
He had not yet seen how far the clocks would reach.
The prize for 1921, announced in 1922,
Cited the law of the photoelectric effect.
That is the light paper. It is not the clocks.

Hold what the clocks did.
They took "at the same time" down from the sky
And put it into a method anyone can state.
Two observers who follow the method can disagree,
And the disagreement is not a fault in either clock.
It is the motion between the frames.
A hidden absolute is a worse instrument than a rule.

This calendar does not put the absolute back.
The days are one sequence, kept, so the names have an order a person can say.
A register is a frame that has been chosen and then not dropped.
He showed why two frames need not share an instant.
He did not show that a kept list is empty.
The dead are not simultaneous with the living.
They are earlier, in the order the register keeps.
That order is a procedure, as his clocks were a procedure.
Count the light. It comes in pieces, and the pieces can be numbered.
Keep the days. They are not the world's one now.
The same instant is a thing you make,
With a signal, and a rule that the way there equals the way back.
Make it. Say which frame you made it in.
Do not pretend the sky held it before you did.

Stand.

# Chapter 329: The Covenant of Peace

Proclaim a dawn that crowns the world with light,
The chains of hate lie shattered in the dust.
The crowds arise, and labor claims its right,
From forge and field their hopeful banners thrust.

Yet scarlet suns twice scorched the mourning earth,
Their silent fire wrote horror on the sky;
But hope endured where ash replaced the sand,
And swore that war’s last ember here would die.

A crackling voice on August’s fervent air
Confessed defeat across the empire’s seas;
In Times Square strangers, dazzled, found a dare,
And sealed a vow with one unbridled squeeze.

On Labor Day the treaty met its fate;
Steel decks became the altar of release.
The working world inhaled and sealed its fate,
Proclaiming labor’s covenant of peace.

From Pittsburgh mills to London’s river docks,
The union drums beat loud in molten halls;
Red banners rolled like quick-forged iron shocks,
And bosses felt the tremor in their walls.

Kartik lamps glowed through India’s dark night,
A widow smiled, her children at her side;
As sparks like comets chased the ghosts to flight,
And whispered freedom swelling in the tide.

In ruined fields the Shintō harvest bowed,
White grains were heaped before a broken shrine;
The farmer wept yet offered thanks aloud,
For seeds of hope still quickened in the brine.

In Nuremberg the gavel cracked like doom,
The plague of hate was named before the earth;
Twelve shadows waited judgment in the room,
And heard mankind demand their curse’s birth.

December’s pilgrims gathered on the sand,
Indian scholar singing promised land;
Trader from Malay knelt upon the sand,
Their voices mingled, binding sand to land.

Eight fragile lights renewed a Warsaw room,
The youngest played where once the shadows lay;
Their mother sang against remembered gloom,
And turned the night to promise with her pray.

In ruined towns the bells returned to life,
Their silver peals stitched silence into song;
Young soldiers danced beside a wooden fife,
And swore no tyrant’s reign would last as long.

In Seoul the crowds lit torches in the street,
Their flags proclaimed the empire’s yoke was done;
Yet lines were drawn where foreign soldiers meet,
And dawn arose upon a severed sun.

Across the plains of China drums of peace
Replaced the clash of empire’s hungry blade;
Yet rival banners gathered, never ceased,
And thunder warned of storms that had not fled.

In Tokyo the courtroom lights burned slow,
Two years of witness weighed each mortal crime;
Yet on the throne a sacred ember glowed,
And judgment blurred along the edge of time.

New Year’s decree spoke softly through the gray;
No myth shall bind my people’s hearts at all.
Not akitsumikami, far away,
And trust shall rise, a beacon shared by all.

So ended war and dawned the workers’ dream,
With lamps and bells and grains beneath the sky;
The plague of hate dissolved like distant steam,
And human hands began to build on high.
