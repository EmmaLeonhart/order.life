# Chapter 337: Add a Little Article

No holiday is named for this day.

The book that asks a degree before a page
Is not this page.
A page anyone may change is not a finished truth.
It is also not nothing.

The night of the two digits is the previous page.
{{c|Larry Sanger}} had been writing a digest
Of the reports about those clocks.
In January 2000 he sent a letter
About a successor to that digest.
{{c|Jimmy Wales}} answered with an encyclopedia,
And asked him to lead it.
He has written that the open encyclopedia was Wales's idea,
And the money the company's.
The company was Bomis. He names {{c|Jimmy Wales}},
{{c|Tim Shell}}, and {{c|Michael Davis}}.
He arrived in {{p|San Diego}} early in February,
And finished in June the degree the work had asked.

The book was called Nupedia.
Through the spring and the fall the work went by mail.
A board of volunteers, most of them with degrees,
Agreed to seven steps before a page was posted.
He later wrote that he proposed much of the plan,
And that the board shaped it.
The first page to pass was on atonality,
By {{c|Christoph Hust}}, in the summer, the month not pinned.
By the next winter he remembers about twenty-five
Approved, and many more still in the steps.
One later interview gives twelve after eighteen months.
His own page, that July, gives just over twenty.
This chapter does not make the counts one.

They had already agreed, he writes,
That a disputed thing be set down
As someone's view, and not as the fact.
That rule is older than the open book.

On the second of January, a Tuesday,
He ate with {{c|Ben Kovitz}}
At a Mexican place in {{p|Pacific Beach}}.
{{c|Ben Kovitz}} told him of {{c|Ward Cunningham}}'s pages,
Which anyone could change.
He had not seen such a page.
He later writes that the encyclopedia's use of it
Came to him that evening.
{{c|Ben Kovitz}} has written that he set the encyclopedia
In front of him.
The two memories are not one sentence.
The tool was already called by a word for quick.

He asked that the tool be set up.
It asked for no new programmer.
On the tenth, a Wednesday, the pages opened
Under Nupedia's own address, and articles were written.
He has written that a clear majority of the board
Wanted nothing to do with a page of that kind.
So the pages took their own name, and their own address.
He made the name from the tool and from encyclopedia.
A silly name, he has said, for a silly project.
The address opened on the fifteenth, a Monday,
At wikipedia.com.
It was a .com because ads were still in mind,
To pay the editor, the programmers, and the machines.
The later assurance against ads is not this month.
The foundation that later held the book is not this January.

He wrote to the list: humor me.
Go there and add a little article.
It will take all of five or ten minutes.
One dating of that letter puts it on the fifteenth.
Another puts it on the seventeenth.
This chapter does not join the two datings.

The software did not keep a sure history.
A later recovery gives a line on the home page
That day, from the company's office machine, unsigned.
It reads, This is the new WikiPedia!
This chapter does not call those words a hello,
And does not give the line to {{c|Jimmy Wales}}
Or to {{c|Larry Sanger}}.

On the twenty-fourth he wrote that the book
Had taken a life of its own.
He later gives the sizes as approximate.
About six hundred by the end of January.
Thirteen hundred in March, twenty-three hundred in April,
Thirty-nine hundred in May.
On the twentieth of September, a Thursday,
{{c|Peter Meyers}}, in the New York Times,
Wrote of some ten thousand entries, from Abba to zygote,
And about a hundred volunteers since January.
His later count for the ninth of that month is higher.
A magazine later has {{c|Jimmy Wales}} say
Eighteen thousand in a year.
He remembers twenty thousand announced
Just before the anniversary.
None of these counts is made into the others.

The paper calls him {{c|Larry Sanger}} of {{p|Las Vegas}},
Who founded the book with {{c|Jimmy Wales}},
And editor in chief of the book with the steps.
In March he had called himself that,
And, with {{c|Jimmy Wales}}, instigator of this one.
He refused, he writes, the title of editor of the open book.
Others used it later.
This chapter does not give him the title he refused.

A rule he proposed said that if the rules
Make you nervous and depressed,
And not desirous of participating,
Then ignore them and go about your business.
He meant it temporary, and a joke,
So the writing would come before the argument of form.
He later set the joke aside.
The book did not.

{{c|Jimmy Wales}} opened a page
Called Neutral Point of View.
The older rule had been to avoid a bias.
{{c|Larry Sanger}} has written that a neutrality
Is not a point of view.
The name stayed.

He later writes that {{c|Ward Cunningham}}, asked
Whether the tool could make an encyclopedia,
Said that in the end it would be a wiki.
{{c|Larry Sanger}} answered that it was already of use as one.
The pair is his memory.
This chapter does not settle it as a transcript.

The open book was meant to feed the book of steps.
The board would not have them as one book.
By the winter the open book was the one that grew,
And his time had gone to it.
The slow steps were not the only thinning. The money thinned too.
His leaving is the next year, and is not this page.

Hold the unsigned line, the five or ten minutes,
The seven steps, and the two memories of the dinner.
Refuse the consolation that a page anyone can change
Has already been judged true,
And the consolation that a degree, on the older book,
Had already judged the open one empty.

Stand.


# Chapter 338: The Middle Hour

No holiday is named for this day.
The leftover note on an older march is not this page.

On Tuesday, the eleventh of September, 2001,
Four planes were turned.
The first struck the north tower, in {{p|New York}}.
The second struck the south.
The third struck the Pentagon, in {{p|Arlington}}.
The fourth came down in a field near {{p|Shanksville}}.
The order is that order.
The minute of the fourth is not agreed.
The way of the turning is not set down.

The people on the fourth plane fought.
The way of the fight is not set down.
The plane did not reach a city.
What building it had been turned toward
Is not given in one sentence.

The south tower fell first.
The north tower fell after.
A part of the Pentagon fell later that morning.
The minute of that fall is not agreed.
An earlier bomb, in 1993, under the same towers,
Is not this morning.

The counts of the dead do not agree.
One later report leaves out the men on the planes.
Another adds them in.
The first reports said more than either.
None is chosen.
Deaths of later years, from the dust,
Are not this morning.
A report of 2004 counts nineteen men on the four planes.
The report is not this morning.

{{c|George W. Bush}} was with children
In a school in {{p|Sarasota}}
When he was told of the second plane.
How long he remained is not agreed.
The children are not the plot.

That night he spoke from the house.
The speech of that night is not the Friday.
On Friday the fourteenth, at the cathedral
In {{p|Washington}},
He said, we are here in the middle hour of our grief.
They came to pray for the missing and the dead,
And for the ones who loved them.
The grief is not a trick.
The use of the grief is not the grief.

On Sunday the sixteenth he said the word crusade,
For the war on terrorism.
His spokesman said he would regret the word
If it upset a partner, and that he had meant a broad cause.
The papers said he regretted it.
The two sayings are not one.
The word had been said.
A regret he spoke in 2004 is not this autumn.
The chapter does not take the word up.

On Thursday the twentieth he said
Every nation was with them, or with the terrorists.
He set demands on the men who held {{p|Afghanistan}}:
Give up the leaders they were sheltering.
{{c|Osama bin Laden}} was named.
What he himself said, and on which tape,
The tapes do not make one sentence.
This chapter does not choose the tape,
And does not set a creed down for him,
Or for the country that answered.

On Sunday the seventh of October
The strikes began, on his order,
Against the camps and against the regime
That held the camps.
{{p|Britain}} was in it with them.
Others pledged forces.
A pledge is not that Sunday's strikes.
The way of the strikes is not set down.
Some in the government named {{p|Iraq}} in those days.
The war there is not this year.

In October a law was signed,
On Friday the twenty-sixth.
A law is not a creed.

The absurdity is a morning pressed into a holy war,
By the ones who sent the planes,
And by the word the answer reached for.
The striving is to keep the dead from being a permission,
And to tell the prayer and the order apart.
Hold the Tuesday of the four planes,
The Friday of the middle hour,
The Sunday the word was said,
And the Sunday the strikes began.
Refuse the consolation that the dead had proved a creed,
And the consolation that the prayer was only politics.
The counts are not chosen.
The way is not set down.

Stand.


# Chapter 339: Not Completely Complete

No holiday is named for this day.

The plan of 1990 is an earlier page.
Its range of genes was not a count.
A draft announced in June 2000 is not this April.
The letters of one life are not this reference.
A reference is also not nothing.

On Monday, the fourteenth of April, 2003,
In {{p|Bethesda}},
An international consortium announced
That the project had met its goals,
More than two years before the schedule.
In the {{p|United States}} the work was led
By two agencies.
The release calls the sequence finished.
Finished, in that release, means highly accurate,
Fewer than one error in ten thousand letters,
And highly contiguous,
The remaining gaps being the regions
The method could not reliably resolve.
The same release says the finish covers
About ninety-nine in a hundred
Of the gene-containing part,
At an accuracy of ninety-nine point ninety-nine
In a hundred.
That accuracy is the same standard
As one error in ten thousand.
It is not a second standard.

The same week, another report says
The finish was ten times better than that standard.
{{c|Robert Waterston}} says the error
Was one in a hundred thousand.
This chapter does not make the rates one.

The release sets the draft against the finish.
The draft covered ninety in a hundred
Of the gene-containing part,
And held about a hundred and fifty thousand gaps.
The finish, the release says,
Holds the missing part in fewer than four hundred gaps.
{{c|Francis Collins}} said, of the sequence
That opened that day in {{p|Santa Cruz}},
That it is not completely complete,
And perhaps never will be.
The gaps he names are about four hundred,
About one in a hundred
Of the gene-containing part.
Another report says ninety-nine in a hundred
Of the genome.
The gene-containing part is not the whole.
This chapter does not make the two sentences one.

The paper of Tuesday gives a sentence
{{c|Robert Waterston}} said at the announcement.
We have before us the instruction set
That carries each of us from the one-cell egg
Through adulthood to the grave.
The sentence is his.
A reference sequence is not each life.
The donors are not named on this page.
The way the letters were read is not set down.

This April did not give a count of the genes.
The paper says the analysts had not yet computed one.
An earlier estimate, about thirty thousand,
Is not a count this chapter chooses.
A count published the next year is not this page.
The plan's range remains on its own page.

The draft of June is not this Monday.
The promise of a later paper is not this Monday.
A gap the method could not close
Is part of the definition they announced,
And it is still a gap.
Collins said almost everybody looking
For answers from the genome
Would find it in the most final form
They could ever have dreamed of.
A form that can be searched
Is not an answer already judged.

Hold the Monday in {{p|Bethesda}},
The sequence that opened in {{p|Santa Cruz}},
The fewer than four hundred gaps,
And the two rates of error.
Refuse the consolation that finished means every letter,
And the consolation that a reference
Has already read a life.

Stand.


# Chapter 340: A Friend of a Friend

No holiday is named for this day.
The leftover note on a suburban dream is not this page.

The houses at {{p|Cambridge}} already kept books of faces.
A book gave a house its names and its pictures.
A book for the whole college was being talked about,
And was not finished.
{{c|Mark Zuckerberg}} said everyone had been talking about one.
He said it was silly that the college would take years.
He said he could do it better, and in a week.

On Wednesday, the fourth of February, 2004,
In the afternoon, after about a week of coding,
He opened thefacebook.com.
The first paper does not name the room.
A later account puts it in Kirkland House.

A Harvard address could put up a picture,
And personal and academic information.
The way of the joining is not set down.
He said Friendster was a model.
A person could search by interests,
And make a network of friends.
He said a search could be limited
To a friend, or a friend of a friend,
Or to a year, a house, or a class.

An earlier site, from the fall, is not this Wednesday.
He said that one had been a joke,
And that it had problems.
He hoped the limits would help his name after it.
The way of that site is not set down.

The paper of Monday, the ninth of February,
Gives more than six hundred and fifty
As of Sunday afternoon.
He thought nine hundred by the morning of the paper.
A later telling puts six hundred and fifty
Inside the first day.
The counts are not made one.

He said the nature of the site
Is that each person's experience improves
If their friends join.
He said the new thing was a search of a class,
So a person could start a friendship, or a study.
A student said the names were how to ask for help
When a person knew no one there.
On the Wednesday two weeks on,
He said he had downplayed the courses,
Because people would use the social part.
The two uses are not made one.

That Wednesday, the eighteenth,
He said he had no idea why it was so popular.
The paper gives more than four thousand three hundred
As of the day before,
Students, alumni, and faculty.
He said the count was likely larger,
Because a person could keep out of the tally.
The Sunday count and this count are not one week.

He said he was not going to sell
Anybody's e-mail address.
He had thought of a fee,
So companies could search resumes,
And he did not want to touch it.
He said it would make the thing
More serious, and less fun.
What was sold in later years is not this February.

{{c|Kevin Davis}}, who directed residential computing,
Said a facebook was already a high priority,
And was meant to be done by the end of the spring.
The complication, he said,
Was how to keep a directory
From being compiled without authorization.
He said there was nothing inherently wrong
With a site where students chose
To make a personal network.
Whether the college finished its book that spring
Is not this page.

The day the other schools opened is not agreed.
One paper says Columbia, in {{p|New York}},
On Wednesday the twenty-fifth,
And Stanford on the Thursday,
And Yale, in {{p|New Haven}}, on the Monday.
Another paper puts Columbia on the Thursday.
None is chosen.
A person at one school could not open
A person at another.
The Monday paper gives just under
Seven thousand five hundred in the whole,
And five thousand fifty-five students at Harvard
As of the last day of February.
He wrote that eighty-five in a hundred at Harvard
Had registered.
That fraction is his letter, not an audit.

A paper in {{p|New York}} says a member
Could post one picture, not an album.
An earlier network there is not this site.

He said the server was about eighty-five dollars a month.
The paper says he paid it.
A later telling puts a thousand dollars
With {{c|Eduardo Saverin}},
To keep the site up for at least two months.
The sums are not made one.
The lists of who began it do not agree.
{{c|Chris Hughes}} is named that March
As the one who spoke for the site.
{{c|Dustin Moskovitz}} is named that March, writing the programs.
This chapter does not close the list.

In the summer he and Moskovitz went to the valley
South of {{p|San Francisco}}.
A later account names {{p|Palo Alto}}.
The two namings are not made one.
A later account says Saverin did not go.
That year {{c|Peter Thiel}} put in five hundred thousand.
The next year Zuckerberg called it seed money,
The first large sum.
Another account calls it a loan
Later made into a share.
The form is not agreed.
The later price of the share is not this year.

The shorter name is not this year.
The opening past the schools is not this year.
A feed of what the friends did is not this year.

The absurdity is a house book of faces
Becoming a graph of who named whom.
The reason for going slowly
Was keeping a directory from being compiled.
The use of the week
Was that a friend of a friend can be looked up.
The striving is to keep a named friend
From being taken for a parent, or for a life,
And to hold the sentence about the addresses
Beside the search.
Neither sentence is a trick.
His week and the college's spring are not one clock.
The counts are not chosen.

Stand.
