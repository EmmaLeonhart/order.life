# Chapter 341: The Page Shown

No holiday is named for this day.
The leftover note on an older awakening is not this page.
The letters of a life are an earlier page.
This page is the books.

The publishers' program had been in a test
For nearly a year.
The day it opened is not agreed.
One trade paper says it went live
On Tuesday, the fifth of October, 2004.
Another dates an announcement
To Wednesday, the sixth.
The paper of Friday, the eighth,
Is dated from the fair in {{p|Frankfurt}},
On Thursday, the seventh.
{{c|Larry Page}} and {{c|Sergey Brin}} were there.

The lists of the houses do not agree.
Random House, that week, had not signed.
A talk at the fair is not a signature.
They would not say how many books
Had been taken in.
A user could search a word
Inside a book a publisher had given.
A few pages could be seen,
The phrase marked, and a link to a shop.
The publishers did not pay.
The company sold advertisements on the page,
And shared that money.
{{c|Susan Wojcicki}} said there were no plans
To sell the books themselves.
The plan was to be a search.
A search inside books, at a shop,
Is older than this fair.
The offering of August is not this fair.
The invitation that week asked for books in English,
With a standard number.
A library's old shelf is not that invitation.
The way of the scan is not set down.

On Tuesday, the fourteenth of December,
In {{p|Mountain View}},
The same program was widened to five libraries.
Harvard, in {{p|Cambridge}}.
Stanford.
Michigan, in {{p|Ann Arbor}}.
Oxford, in {{p|Oxford}}.
And the public library in {{p|New York}}.
Page said that even before the company,
They dreamed of making searchable
What librarians lovingly organize.
The release says a user can find a match
In the full text of a book,
While a publisher and an author
Make money from that information.
A match is not the book.

The five did not give the same pile.
Harvard gave a pilot of about forty thousand,
Taken at random,
Both in and out of copyright.
The size of Harvard's whole library is not agreed.
At Michigan the collection was offered.
The count is not agreed.
{{c|William Gosling}} says seven million,
Not the special collections,
And about six years,
Where the library had been doing
About five thousand a year.
Another paper says seven million
And four hundred thousand.
Stanford's count is not agreed.
One estimate says seven million.
Another says nearly all of eight million.

Oxford's share is not agreed.
One account says the books of nineteen hundred
And before.
Another says the nineteenth-century holdings,
About a million, out of about eight million.
Another says the whole library.
The Bodleian dates from sixteen hundred and two.
One paper says more than eight million books,
On shelves of more than a hundred and twenty miles.
The length of the shelves is not the scan.

The number from {{p|New York}} is not given.
One paper says fragile books,
Out of copyright, for scholars.
The library's account says twenty million books
Among more than fifty million items.
The catalog had been searchable
For more than ten years.
The pages had not.
It is the only public library named that Tuesday.

For a book out of copyright,
The reports give the whole text.
For a book still in copyright,
They do not agree what a searcher may see.
One says, at first, only the line that names it.
Another says a short excerpt,
And where the book can be found.
The whole of such a book may be taken in.
The page shown is not the whole.
The way of the scan is not set down.

One report says a small sample from Michigan
Could be searched that Tuesday.
The library's own account says a larger body
Comes later, as the work moves out of a pilot.
The two are not one arrival.

The company did not give the price.
One estimate, not theirs,
Says ten dollars a book, and a decade,
For fifteen million.
The librarian's six years are not that estimate.
None is chosen.
One report says a library receives a copy
Of what was made from its own books.

The name on this Tuesday is Google Print.
A later name is not this year.
The suits of the next year are not this Tuesday.

The absurdity is a catalog searched for years
While the pages of the books were not,
And a company that takes the pages in
So a sentence can be found,
While the libraries do not agree which pages,
And a searcher may be given the whole,
Or a scrap, or only the name.
The striving is to keep the page shown
From standing in for the book,
And the catalog from standing in for the page,
And to hold the public whole
Apart from the book that is still owned.
The searchable catalog was a care.
The limit on the excerpt is a care.
They are not the same care.
The counts are not chosen.
