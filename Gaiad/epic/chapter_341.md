# Chapter 341: The Water and the Levees

The fifth day of Yuletide is the feast of the year two thousand and five.
Light five lights, and keep the twentieth of July, when {{p|Canada}} let all love wed and thrive.

In two thousand and five I turned nine, in the country that opened marriage that summer.
A city drowned in the richest country in the world, a pope died and another was crowned,
And the moving pictures of anyone at all got a place to live,
And I would grow up on that place, where every face could be found.

## The pictures

On the fourteenth of February three men from {{x|PayPal}} registered {{x|YouTube}}.
On the twenty-third of April {{c|Jawed Karim}} put up the first, eighteen seconds long:
Himself at the {{p|San Diego}} zoo before the elephants,
Saying the cool thing is they have really, really, really long trunks. That was the whole song.

A year and a half later {{x|Google}} bought it for a billion and a half.
The children who were nine that year would grow up with a camera that reached all,
And some of us grew up on it, and some of us made our livings there,
The first generation with a broadcast tower in the bedroom hall.

In June two students opened {{x|Reddit}}, the front page of the internet,
And faked the first users to make the board look full, until the crowd came for real.
In February {{x|Google Maps}} put the streets of the world in a page you drag with your hand,
And the whole earth was a map in the browser, and I could turn the wheel.

## The pope

On the second of April {{c|John Paul II}} died in {{p|Rome}},
The first pope from {{p|Poland}}, who helped bring down the empire of the east.
Millions came to his funeral. On the nineteenth the cardinals chose {{c|Joseph Ratzinger}},
Who took the name {{c|Benedict XVI}}, and would step down eight years on, the rarest release.

## The villain's catastrophe

On the twenty-fifth of April {{c|Vladimir Putin}} told his parliament
That the collapse of the {{p|Soviet Union}} was the greatest geopolitical catastrophe of the century.
Mark the sentence. Everything he did after was the attempt to undo it,
And {{p|Georgia}}, and {{p|Crimea}}, and {{p|Ukraine}} would pay for his nostalgia, in blood and in penury.

## London

On the seventh of July, in {{p|London}}'s morning rush,
Three bombs went off in the {{x|Underground}} and one on a bus at {{p|Tavistock Square}}.
Fifty-two were killed, by four young men, three of them born in {{p|Britain}},
The day after {{p|London}} won the games of twenty-twelve and celebrated in the air.

## The drowning

On the twenty-ninth of August {{x|Hurricane Katrina}} came ashore
Near the line between {{p|Louisiana}} and {{p|Mississippi}}, and in {{p|New Orleans}}
The levees the {{x|Army Corps of Engineers}} had built failed in more than fifty places,
And four parts in five of the city went under, the streets and the homes and the greens.

The people who could leave had left. The people who could not,
Most of them poor, most of them black, were left in their attics and on their roofs,
In the {{x|Superdome}} with no power and no water,
And at the convention center, where no one came for days, and those are the proofs.

Eighteen hundred died. The head of {{x|FEMA}}, {{c|Michael Brown}},
Had run an association of horse shows before.
{{c|Bush}} told him he was doing a heck of a job.
The government knew where the water was. The help did not come to the door.

I was nine, far to the north, and I saw the roofs on the screen
With words painted on them asking for help from the sky.
The city came back, and the levees were rebuilt stronger,
And the jazz came back to the {{p|French Quarter}}, and the second lines went by.

## Kashmir

On the eighth of October the earth broke under {{p|Kashmir}},
On both sides of the line between {{p|Pakistan}} and {{p|India}}, in the morning hours,
While the children were in their schools, and the schools were not built to stand.
Eighty-six thousand died, and winter came to the mountains before the shelter's towers.

## The marriages

On the third of July {{p|Spain}}, a country of the old faith, opened marriage to all.
On the twentieth of July {{p|Canada}} did the same, by the {{x|Civil Marriage Act}},
After the courts of most of its provinces had already said so:
The fourth country in the world, the first in the Americas, and that is a fact.

I grew up in that country. The law was made before I knew I would need it.
That is what such a law is for: to wait on the road for the ones who will come.
The twentieth of July is a feast of this faith,
The day the country I grew up in opened its door to everyone.

## The fingers and the cedars

On the thirtieth of January the people of {{p|Iraq}} voted,
And held up fingers stained purple with the ink, while the bombs went off at the polls.
In October they voted on a constitution, in December for a parliament:
A people voting under fire, and those were brave souls.

On the fourteenth of February, in {{p|Beirut}}, a ton of explosives
Killed the former prime minister, {{c|Rafic Hariri}}, and the people filled the square,
And the army of {{p|Syria}}, there twenty-nine years, went home by April.
They called it the {{x|Cedar Revolution}}, and the cedars were there.

In August {{p|Israel}} pulled its settlers and soldiers out of {{p|Gaza}},
And {{x|Hamas}} would take it, and the rockets would come from the land given back.
On the thirteenth of May, in {{p|Andijan}}, in {{p|Uzbekistan}},
Soldiers fired on the crowd in the square, and killed hundreds in the attack.

On the twenty-eighth of July the {{x|IRA}} said it was done with the gun.
On the sixteenth of February the {{x|Kyoto Protocol}} came into force.
On the thirtieth of September a newspaper in {{p|Denmark}}, {{x|Jyllands-Posten}},
Printed twelve cartoons of the {{x|Prophet}}, and the next year brought the fire and the force.

## The rest of the year

In January {{x|Huygens}} landed on {{p|Titan}}, the farthest landing ever made,
And astronomers found {{x|Eris}} beyond {{p|Pluto}}, larger than it, a world of ice.
In March {{c|Terri Schiavo}} was let die after the courts and the congress fought over her body.
On the thirty-first of May {{c|Mark Felt}} said he was {{x|Deep Throat}}, and the secret paid its price.

In June {{p|Iran}} elected {{c|Mahmoud Ahmadinejad}}.
On the second of July the {{x|Live 8}} concerts asked the rich to forgive the poor their debt.
On the twenty-fourth of October {{c|Rosa Parks}} died, who would not give up her seat.
In October and November the suburbs of {{p|Paris}} burned, and the riot police met.

On the twenty-second of November {{c|Angela Merkel}} became chancellor of {{p|Germany}},
And held it sixteen years, and she would be the one who knew {{c|Putin}} best,
Who spoke his language and he spoke hers, and she bought his gas,
And let the pipelines be built that were the villain's leverage against the west.

In November a false article in {{x|Wikipedia}} said {{c|John Seigenthaler}}
Had been suspected in the {{c|Kennedy}} killings, and it had stood four months long.
He wrote that he had no way to know who wrote it.
The book tightened its rules a little; the labor stayed unpaid, and that stayed wrong.

## The year held

A video of elephants, a board faking its first users into being, a map in the hand,
A pope buried and a pope chosen, bombs in {{p|London}}, and a city under the sea.
The villain named his catastrophe, and the mountains fell on the schools,
And the country I grew up in opened marriage, and that was for me.

The water came to {{p|New Orleans}}, and the help did not.
And the city came back anyway, and the music played in the street.
The law came to {{p|Canada}} before I knew I needed it,
And it was waiting on the road for me, for when my turning was complete.
