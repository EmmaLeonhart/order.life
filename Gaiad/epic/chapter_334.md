# Chapter 334: The White Face

Today is {{c|4493|Dolly}}'s feast, a holy day:
July the fifth, in nineteen ninety-six;
An earlier page: pairing, in its way,
A chain's own copy; here, the cell's own mix:

A body asked to start again, from one
Cell that already had become a part;
That Friday, at {{p|Roslin}}, the work was done,
By {{p|Edinburgh}}; the lab dates its start;

The fusion, eighth of February, made;
The fusion and the birth: two mornings; she,
Called, in the paper, {{x|6LL3}}; and laid
A {{x|Finn Dorset}}; her carrier, to be

A {{x|Scottish Blackface}}; had the lamb been hers,
The face were black; the face was white: a sign,
The first; and later, matching marks, confers
The nucleus to its source; and the line:

The nucleus: a culture of the gland
Of a six-year {{x|Finn Dorset}}, pregnant, late;
Mostly the gland's; which one, none understand;
The egg, a {{x|Scottish Blackface}}'s, its own freight

Of nucleus removed; the ewe who bore,
Another; three in all, and three the kind
Of parent: this book counts them three, and more,
Each one a parent; what the egg, behind,

Kept of its own stayed in her; cell held still,
Its cycle stopped; the nucleus then moved;
Two seventy-seven fusions' skill;
Twenty-nine embryos; thirteen ewes proved;

One pregnancy, one lamb alive; the rest
In the same paper, from a fetus, and
From embryo, not her; and, by that test,
In 'ninety-five, {{c|4494|Megan}} and {{c|4495|Morag}}, planned,

Born of embryo cells begun to change;
Not a grown body's cell; and so, the first
Mammal the paper claims, in all its range,
From adult tissue. {{c|4496|Ian Wilmut}} versed

The first name; and {{c|4497|Keith Campbell}} on it; more
Than one hand; with a company, the work
At the same village; names, no womb; and, for
Her name from {{c|4498|Dolly Parton}}: gland; a quirk

Of theirs; the name is not the result; this:
A cell, specialized, brought back to begin,
Far enough that a lamb stood; and, in this,
Within minutes she stood alone; and in

A quiet kept; a Saturday, they told,
'Ninety-seven, spoke, the twenty-second;
A silence for a journal, not state's hold;
And {{x|Nature}}'s, the twenty-seventh, reckoned;

The fear it woke: a person copied; none
Was born that Friday; and a sheep's no leave;
One lamb, many fusions; yet this one
Hard birth is not a never; we believe

The asking good: a person cloned; and kept
With three parents, each one a parent's kind;
The white face in the pen first, as she leapt;
Bore six lambs; no dead end, the line not blind;

The ram, a {{x|Welsh Mountain}}; then, in 'oh-three,
February fourteenth: she was put down;
A sheep's lung illness, no tie proved; and she
In {{p|Edinburgh}}'s museum, in town;

The striving: what the cell was asked, the whole:
Could one part, set to work, begin again
The whole once more? A lamb, the answer's role,
Who lived, and bore; a rule for persons, then,

For us to take; count the one living lamb,
The three in making, each a parent; she,
Not by two parents; and the feast, the dam,
And all her line, kept on this day, and free.
