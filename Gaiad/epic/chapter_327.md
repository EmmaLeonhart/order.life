# Chapter 327: As Far as a New Time

On the twentieth of April, 1972,
The first day of summer that year,
A meeting at a hotel called the {{p|Borg}},
In {{p|Reykjavík}}, formed a fellowship.
The name of it was Ásatrúarfélagið.
A later account also names the thirtieth.
Do not make the two meetings one meeting.
They were not a church that day.
The poems were already old. The meeting was new.

{{c|4454|Sveinbjörn Beinteinsson}} was a sheep farmer
In {{p|Borgarfjörður}}, and a singer of rímur.
Soon after, they gave him the title allsherjargoði,
The word that had named the goði
Who hallowed the assembly.
The word was used again. The office was not.
Do not say a medieval law had been waiting
On a farm.

In the first letter to the ministry
They named the old religion of the {{c|4455|Icelanders}},
Law until the year 1000, so far as it fitted
A new time and changed conditions.
A paper that spring called them
The Ásatrú people of the new time.
They said nýheiðni: new heathenry.
Do not say they claimed an unbroken priesthood.
The limit is in the application.

At {{p|Þingvellir}}, in the year later tellings
Call 1000, the assembly took the Christian law.
A private sacrifice was still permitted,
And then that permission was taken back.
A book is not a blót.
A blót in 1973 is not that assembly's law.
Do not say the gods ended in a day,
Or that a public sacrifice had never stopped.

Shortly before {{x|Christmas}}, 1972,
{{c|4454|Sveinbjörn Beinteinsson}} and {{c|4456|Þorsteinn Guðjónsson}}
Went to {{c|4457|Ólafur Jóhannesson}},
Minister of justice and of ecclesiastical affairs.
He thought the request was a joke.
They said they were serious, and he asked for papers.
By {{c|4458|Sveinbjörn}}'s account, after they had left,
The lights went out in the center of {{p|Reykjavík}}.
On the second of January a paper made a joke of it,
And gave the thunder to {{c|4459|Þór}}.
The letter was not that night. A storm is not a signature.

{{c|4460|Sigurbjörn Einarsson}}, bishop of {{p|Iceland}},
Wrote against it. The opinion is dated
The second of January, 1973.
He read the right to found organizations to serve god
As a phrase for one god.
He wrote that the teachings were not fixed,
And that there was no house for the rite.
He named {{p|Germany}}, and a racial use of the old gods.
He wrote that the group was twenty-one.
An earlier letter had said about a hundred.
And the roll has grown every year since.

The applicants denied that the fellowship
Was national socialism.
They wrote that a genuine following of these gods
In that {{p|Reich}} was doubtful, and that some Christian bodies
Had gone along with it.
A denial is not a proof. The bishop's fear is not the fellowship.
Do not try them as the {{p|Germany}} he named,
And do not call the denial the end of the question.

The first papers did not meet the rules.
Later papers did, and the ministry said so.
On the third of May, 1973, a letter came
To a house on {{p|Ingólfsstræti}}, in {{p|Reykjavík}},
And confirmed him as the legal head.
The papers reported it on the fifteenth.
Do not collapse the third and the fifteenth.
On the sixteenth, twenty-one people were in the room.
Attendance is not the roll.

What May gave was a registered society.
The national church stayed the national church,
The {{x|Evangelical Lutheran Church}}.
What the register carried were acts that church counted:
A naming, a coming of age, a marriage, a burial.
The rights come because the priests have them.
Do not say the old goði's work was copied
Line for line into the letter.
A share of the church tax followed
The adult members on the list.
A name on a list draws a share.
A share is not a belief.
Do not say {{p|Iceland}} changed its church.

Later that year {{c|4461|Halldór Blöndal}}
And {{c|4462|Magnús Jónsson}} asked the minister why.
{{c|4462|Magnús Jónsson}} said the freedom was for one god,
And that a polytheism, and idol worship,
Were not a lawful recognition.
The minister answered with the freedom of association
And the freedom of religion, and said the scholars
Had not agreed how far the clause reached.
It reached far enough: the old gods were recognized.

On the fifth of August, at {{p|Dragháls}},
They held a blót in the rain, under a plaster figure
Of {{c|4459|Þór}} that {{c|4463|Jörmundur Ingi Hansen}} had made.
A May headline had said nine hundred years.
The years since the assembly's law are not nine hundred.
The August telling called the day the first public
Outdoor blót since the ban.
Do not use either sentence as a count of the silence.
A bird was killed in the kitchen while a lamb was cooked.
The farmer said the meat was bought,
Because a killing inside the rite was more trouble
Than it was worth. One account has both.
Do not make the kitchen the theology, and do not leave the bird out.

The return was not only in {{p|Iceland}}.
A brotherhood in the {{p|United States}}
Had a tax exemption in 1972.
In {{p|Britain}} a body called the {{x|Pagan Front}}
Had been named by 1971.
Neither one is this letter.
A later use of these names kept them for a descent.
Do not move that use back into this May,
And do not pretend the bishop had not asked.
In 1975 the assembly made the next recognition harder.
Do not move that change back into May.

Count the twentieth of April, 1972, as the meeting
On the first day of summer, and the thirtieth
As the other account.
Count the third of May, 1973, as the letter,
And the fifteenth as the papers.
Count the fifth of August as the blót in the rain.
The poems are old. The hotel is new.
The letter is a registration.
They asked for the old law only as far
As a new time would bear it.
That limit is the striving.
A list can hold a name. It cannot hold the years since the assembly,
And it cannot make those years nothing.
