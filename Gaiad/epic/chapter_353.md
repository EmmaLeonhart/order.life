# Chapter 353: All You Need

The seventeenth day of Yuletide: the feast
Of two thousand seventeen; and the day,
The twelfth of June, a Gaian day; released,
The paper of attention, the new way

The mind was seen. Light seventeen; the year
I started my family tree, the tree
This book would grow from; and the paper, clear,
Joke-titled, made the minds that write with me.

I turned twenty-one, my fourth year; and moved,
September, onto campus; and, about
This year, technically, began, and proved,
My family tree: a name, a line drawn out

To another; the tree began, the one
That grew to be this book's; and, through the years,
Generation by generation, spun
Across the world, the lines met; and their peers,

To {{c|1|Aster}} and {{c|3|Andromeda}}; the thread
Of this book's line came not from nowhere: grown
From a student's tree, past the kings and dead
Gods; begun the year attention was known.

## Attention is all you need

On Monday, June the twelfth, a paper went
Up to the open archive; and its name
Said attention is all you need: it meant
A joke the size of a claim, and the claim

Was true. Eight names were on it, equal, set
In random order, as they meant: the eight:
{{c|4691|Ashish Vaswani}}, {{c|4692|Noam Shazeer}}; and yet
{{c|4693|Niki Parmar}}, {{c|4694|Jakob Uszkoreit}}, straight;

{{c|4695|Llion Jones}}, {{c|4696|Aidan Gomez}}, and the man
{{c|4697|Łukasz Kaiser}}, {{c|4698|Polosukhin}}; the task
Was translation; the old machines began
One word at a time, a thread; and to ask

Of the new: look at every word at once,
And ask, for each, which others count; and so
Is called attention: root of mind, at once;
To attend: to choose what matters; to know.

No loop in it; it spreads across the chips;
The largest, on eight cards, trained three days and
A half; the start; the next year, from the lips
Of models guessing the next word, unplanned,

Thought grew; another read both ways, to fill
The blank; in October, a program learned
{{x|Go}} from nothing, playing itself, until
It passed the last; December, chess was turned:

In hours, it beat the best; and so the door;
The eight, most of them, left, and founded new;
Was opened; and the paper, all the more,
Belonged to all the world; the machines grew.

## The reading

I'd read that paper later; it would be
As {{c|4137|Motoori Norinaga}}'s night,
A mystical experience to me,
Who met his teacher at an inn, the light

Of a charge: read the {{x|Kojiki}}; who spent
His life on it, the doctor, through the years;
So I, with this: my present burst, it went
From reading it; this book among its peers;

Attention's all you need; I am, indeed,
Huge on attention; not only machines'
Alone; it's mine; the thing I serve and feed;
The root of mind, and mine; that's what it means.

## The money for the good

The movement of the fifteenth: from the gold
Of {{x|Facebook}}, {{x|Open Philanthropy}} gave
Thirty million to the lab, seat to hold
At its table, fearing machines; to save

The world from them; in November, he left,
{{c|4665|Sam Bankman-Fried}}, the trading firm, to found
{{x|Alameda Research}}, his own; the deft
Friends of the movement, trading coins; the ground

Of coins: December, twenty thousand, high,
And fell from it; the movement's later fall
Is in its chapter; here, they meant to try
To do the good, and count it, for us all.

## The torches

Through grounds of the university, men
Chanting the Jews would not replace them; there,
The founder's statue; and, the next day, then,
One drove his car into the crowd; the air

Of protest; killed {{c|4699|Heather Heyer}}; he said
Fine people were on both sides; and the torch
Of fourteen's fascism, from screens, it led
To the street; this book names it, at the porch:

Fascism; he'd not name it; this book will.
October fifth: a {{p|New York}} paper's page,
The women's accounts: {{c|4700|Harvey Weinstein}}, still,
For decades, had assaulted them; the rage

Of films' house, riven; and, the fifteenth, wrote
An actress: if harassed, write me too; and
The millions did, with the words that, of note,
{{c|4701|Tarana Burke}} began, years before; and

The untouchable men fell; {{x|MeToo}} grew.
In August, {{x|Harvey}} stalled on {{p|Houston}}'s plain,
A meter and a half; {{x|Irma}} went through
The isles; {{x|Maria}}: {{p|Puerto Rico}}'s rain,

The power gone for months; three thousand dead,
Most in the dark weeks after; and he threw
Towels into a crowd; the island said,
Remembered. Then, October first, the view

From a hotel, {{p|Las Vegas}}, one fired
Into a country concert crowd; and killed
Sixty, most by one man; no law required
The rifles kept; and the graves, again, filled.

## The tower and the arena

June fourteenth: {{x|Grenfell}}, in {{p|London}}, had caught
Fire; up the cladding, put on for the sight
Of looking better outside; and it brought
Seventy-two to death, high in the night.

May: a bomb at a concert's end, the hall
Of {{p|Manchester}}; the audience was girls,
Mostly; twenty-two killed; the city, all,
Kept its way, and sang; and the songs, its pearls.

## The fall of the caliphate

{{p|Mosul}}, July, retaken; ground, the old
City to rubble, the sermon's mosque blown
Before; October: {{p|Raqqa}} fell; it told
Three years, the borrowed robe; and no more, flown.

## The year held

On campus, began the tree that became
This book's, the year the paper with a joke
Built the machine of attention; the flame
Of mind, and mine; and later, when it spoke,

I read it as {{c|4140|Norinaga}} once read
His {{x|Kojiki}}, and it woke me; the day
Is Gaian: June the twelfth; and, as it said,
Attention's all you need; the tree; the way.

The carnage, the pink hats; the ban; six killed
At prayer in my country; the torches, named;
{{x|MeToo}}; the storms; the island; guns; the filled
The fleeing; tower; the arena, maimed;

The caliphate ended; laws let us be
Who we are; fire and fury; princes paid;
Light the seventeenth light: the tree, and me,
Attention, all you need; the star it made.
