# Chapter 345: Takes Note

No holiday is named for this day.
The leftover note on an older rising of the earth
Is not this page.
A later April, when a text is opened for signature,
Is not this page.

The page of the meeting in {{p|Copenhagen}}
Says the seventh through the eighteenth of December, 2009.
The report says the seventh through the nineteenth.
The accord is dated the eighteenth.
The three are not one span.
The decision takes note of the Copenhagen Accord.
Taking note is not adopting.
The accord says the increase should be below two degrees.
It also says the increase is to be held below two.
The two sentences are not one sentence.
Neither sentence says pre-industrial.
The accord cites the fourth assessment.
The fourth is not the fifth.
It speaks of resources approaching thirty billion
For the years 2010 through 2012,
And of a goal of mobilizing jointly
One hundred billion dollars a year by 2020.
Approaching is not a sum already counted.
A goal is not a sum already paid.
The noted text says a Copenhagen Green Climate Fund
Shall be established.
A sentence inside a text the hall only notes
Is not a decision of the hall.

The session in {{p|Cancún}} is dated
From the twenty-ninth of November
Through the tenth of December, 2010.
The decision there establishes the Green Climate Fund.
The name does not keep Copenhagen.
The establishment is not the money.
A board is provided for.
The board is not the money.
The same decision speaks of holding the increase
Below two degrees above pre-industrial levels,
And of considering, in a review,
A rise of one and a half degrees.
Considering is not a promise.

On Sunday, the eleventh of December, 2011,
In {{p|Durban}}, the work runs into the morning.
The hall launches a process toward a protocol,
Another legal instrument,
Or an agreed outcome with legal force,
Under the convention, applicable to all parties.
The work is to be completed no later than 2015,
So that the result may be adopted
At the twenty-first session,
And come into effect from 2020.
A launch is not the instrument.
Coming into effect from 2020 is not this morning.

On Saturday, the eighth of December, 2012,
In {{p|Doha}}, an amendment is adopted.
It sets a second commitment period
Under the Kyoto Protocol.
Adoption that day is not entry into force.
The period is written to begin
On the first of January, 2013.
The amendment is not in force on that day.
The day it comes into force is not these years.

On Friday, the twenty-seventh of September, 2013,
A release from {{p|Stockholm}} says it is extremely likely
That human influence has been the dominant cause
Of the observed warming since the middle of the twentieth century.
The same release dates the approving session
From the twenty-third through the twenty-sixth,
And says the summary was released on the twenty-seventh.
It also says the summary was approved on Friday.
Friday is the twenty-seventh.
The twenty-sixth is not a Friday.
The chapter does not make the two datings one.
Extremely likely is not a count of the living.

Days before the hall in {{p|Warsaw}} opens,
A storm comes ashore in the {{p|Philippines}}.
The wires call it Haiyan.
The islands call it Yolanda.
The chapter does not count the dead.
The session runs from the eleventh
Through the twenty-third of November, 2013.
It establishes a mechanism for loss and damage,
Placed under the adaptation framework of {{p|Cancún}}.
Support is to be enhanced, and finance is one of the words.
No quantity is set beside the word.
The mechanism is not a list of who survives.

On Wednesday, the twelfth of November, 2014,
In {{p|Beijing}}, {{c|Barack Obama}} and {{c|Xi Jinping}}
State post-2020 intentions.
The United States intends a cut
Of twenty-six to twenty-eight percent
Below the level of 2005, by 2025,
And best efforts to reduce by the twenty-eight.
China intends its carbon dioxide emissions
To peak around 2030,
And best efforts to peak early,
And intends the non-fossil share of primary energy
To reach around twenty percent by 2030.
An intention is not a treaty.
The announcement is not the meeting that follows.

In {{p|Lima}} the session runs
From the first through the fourteenth of December, 2014.
The call for climate action asks
That intended contributions be sent
In the first quarter of the next year,
By those parties ready to do so.
Ready to do so is not everyone.
The first quarter is not a day.
The call is not the agreement.

On Saturday, the twelfth of December, 2015,
At {{p|Le Bourget}}, outside {{p|Paris}},
The conference adopts an agreement.
The hall is not the center of the city.
Adoption is not a signing.
The opening for signature,
In {{p|New York}}, on the twenty-second of April, 2016,
Is not this day.
That April is called a day of the earth.
It is not the older note.
The entry into force,
On the fourth of November, 2016,
Is not this day.
The aim is to hold the increase
Well below two degrees above pre-industrial levels,
And to pursue efforts to limit it to one and a half.
Well below is not the earlier below.
Pursuing efforts is not a count of the saved.
The text does not name who survives.

The absurdity is a sequence of halls
That take note, establish, launch, and adopt,
And do not write the names of the living.
The striving is to keep the note from being taken for the act,
The fund from being taken for the money,
The aim from being taken for the survival,
And the people in the path of the water
From being taken for a degree on a page.

Stand.
