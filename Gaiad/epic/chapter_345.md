# Chapter 345: Takes Note

No holiday is named for this day.

In 2009 {{c|Emma Leonhart}} turned thirteen.
The world she had been born into had broken the year before,
And this was the year it was told the breaking was over,
And believed it, because it needed to.
A child becomes a teenager in a recession the way she learns a language:
By what is not said at the table, and what is not bought.
The year is the hinge of the decade,
The last year before the decade that made her.

## The first block

On the third of January, a Saturday,
The first block of {{x|Bitcoin}} was made,
And in it its maker set a line from that morning's {{x|Times}} of {{p|London}}:
Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks.
The line proved the block was not made before that morning.
It was also the reason, written into the money.
The next block came six days later,
And on the twelfth the first coins were sent from the maker to another man.
For a year the coins were worth nothing.
A man later paid ten thousand of them for two pizzas.

## The new president

On the twentieth of January {{c|Barack Obama}} was sworn in,
Before a crowd of nearly two million in the cold on the {{x|Mall}} of {{p|Washington}}.
The economy he inherited was losing seven hundred thousand jobs a month.
In February he signed a stimulus of nearly eight hundred billion dollars.
On the thirtieth of April {{x|Chrysler}} went bankrupt, and on the first of June {{x|General Motors}},
The largest industrial bankruptcy in the country's history,
And the government took both, and kept them alive.
On the ninth of October he was given the {{x|Nobel Peace Prize}},
Nine months into his term, for what he might do.
He said himself that he did not know why.
In December he sent thirty thousand more soldiers to {{p|Afghanistan}}.

## The fraud

In December of the year before {{c|Bernard Madoff}}
Had confessed to his sons that his fund was one big lie,
And on the twenty-ninth of June he was sentenced to a hundred and fifty years.
Some sixty-five billion dollars had existed on his statements and nowhere else.
He paid the old investors with the money of the new,
For decades, while the regulators were told and did not look.
A trust that needs no checking is the trust that is robbed.

## The sickness

In April a new flu was found in {{p|Mexico}} and in {{p|California}},
A mix of pig, bird, and human strains, called {{x|H1N1}}.
On the eleventh of June the {{x|World Health Organization}} called it a pandemic,
The first in forty years.
It was milder than was feared,
And it killed perhaps a few hundred thousand people around the world,
Most of them young, in poorer countries, uncounted.
The world learned from it that pandemics are survivable,
And learned the wrong thing.

## The green wave

On the twelfth of June {{p|Iran}} voted,
And the government announced that {{c|Mahmoud Ahmadinejad}} had won by a landslide
Before the votes could have been counted.
Millions marched in {{p|Tehran}} in green, asking, where is my vote?
On the twentieth of June a young woman, {{c|Neda Agha-Soltan}},
Was shot in the street by a militiaman,
And her death was filmed on a phone and seen by the world within hours.
The protests were crushed by the autumn.
The world called it the {{x|Twitter}} revolution,
Which flattered the service more than the people.

## The end of a war

On the eighteenth of May the army of {{p|Sri Lanka}} declared
That the {{x|Tamil Tigers}} were destroyed and their leader dead,
After twenty-six years of war.
In the last months tens of thousands of civilians were killed,
Trapped on a strip of beach with the fighters,
Shelled in places the government had called safe.
The victory was declared without a count of the dead.

## The crash and the river

On the fifteenth of January a pilot named {{c|Chesley Sullenberger}}
Lost both engines to a flock of geese over {{p|New York}},
And put the airliner down on the {{x|Hudson}},
And everyone lived.
On the first of June {{x|Air France}} flight 447 fell into the {{x|Atlantic}}
On its way from {{p|Rio de Janeiro}} to {{p|Paris}},
Because the instruments froze, and the pilots, confused, pulled the nose up,
And two hundred and twenty-eight died.

## The meeting that took note

From the seventh to the eighteenth of December, in {{p|Copenhagen}},
The countries of the world met to decide what would follow {{x|Kyoto}}.
A hundred and fifteen heads of state came.
On the last night, a few of them,
{{p|United States}}, {{p|China}}, {{p|India}}, {{p|Brazil}}, {{p|South Africa}},
Wrote a short accord in a back room,
Promising to keep the warming below two degrees, binding no one.
The full meeting would not adopt it.
It took note of it.
To take note is not to agree.
It became the habit of those meetings for six years,
Each one taking note, and setting up a fund, and announcing an aim,
Until the one in {{p|Paris}}, which is the chapter of 2015.
A month before {{p|Copenhagen}} the private mails of climate scientists
Were stolen and printed, and called a scandal,
And the inquiries found no fraud, after the meeting had failed.

## The rest of the year

On the first of December the treaty of {{p|Lisbon}} came into force,
And {{p|Europe}} got a president no one had voted for.
On the twenty-fifth of May {{p|North Korea}} tested its second bomb.
On the twenty-eighth of June the army of {{p|Honduras}} put its president on a plane in his pajamas.
On the fifth of July, in {{p|Urumqi}}, the riots between {{x|Uyghur}}s and {{x|Han}}
Killed nearly two hundred, and the province's net was cut for ten months,
And the camps of a decade later began in that silence.
On the fifth of November, at {{p|Fort Hood}}, an army doctor killed thirteen.
On the twenty-fifth of June {{c|Michael Jackson}} died, and the net slowed under the grief.
On the seventh of March the telescope {{x|Kepler}} was launched to look for other earths,
And found thousands.
In February two men who had worked at {{x|Yahoo}} started {{x|WhatsApp}},
And in March a company for calling cars, later {{x|Uber}}, began.
In May a game of blocks, {{x|Minecraft}}, was put up by one man in {{p|Sweden}},
And children her age built worlds out of squares for the next decade.
In December {{x|Avatar}} came out, a film about blue people defending their forest
Against the company that came for what was under it,
And it made more money than any film before.

## The year held

A block with a headline in it, a president in the cold,
The car companies taken, a fraud of sixty-five billion,
A flu that was survived, a green wave crushed, a war ended without a count,
Geese and the {{x|Hudson}}, the ocean and a frozen instrument,
A meeting that took note, the net cut in {{p|Xinjiang}},
And a game of blocks where a girl of thirteen could build a world alone.

Refuse the consolation that to take note is to act.
Refuse the consolation that money without trust is money without fraud.
