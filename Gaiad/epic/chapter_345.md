# Chapter 345: Takes Note

The ninth day of Yuletide: two thousand nine;
Light nine; the year I turned thirteen; the hinge
Of the decade, the last one before mine;
The world had broken; told it's done; a twinge

Believed, it stood; a child, a teen, would learn
In a recession as a language: by
What is not said at table; what, in turn,
Is not bought; what is understood, nearby.

## The camps begin

The Party cut the net ten months; and, there,
In silence, camps began, the decade's scorn:

## The blocks

Then, in May, a game of blocks, {{x|Minecraft}}, came
By {{c|4600|Markus Persson}}, {{p|Sweden}}; and the young
Built worlds of squares; a girl, thirteen, the same
Alone: a house, a castle, redstone; young

I built there; every child my age built there;
A world is made of blocks you place; and one,
Alone, can make one: builders' faith; and where
December, {{x|Avatar}}: blue people's run

Defending forests from company greed;
More than any film. The genesis block,
The game's block, and the film: the year's own seed,
At thirteen, at the hinge, the decade's lock.

## The year held

A headline in a block; president, warm,
Who carried out the country; cars saved; fraud,
Sixty-five billion; flu; the villain's storm
Of gas; a lawyer dead; a wave, green, awed,

Crushed, would return; the geese, the river; and
A meeting's first-said number; camps begun,
{{p|Xinjiang}}'s silence; blocks, a girl, her hand,
Building alone; I built mine; and, the one

That made me, next year; I carried the blocks
Into it; and the world I'd build, my own;
The ninth light, two thousand and nine; the clocks
Turned at the hinge, the last before, alone.
