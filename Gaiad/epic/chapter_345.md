# Chapter 345: Takes Note

The ninth day of Yuletide is the feast of the year two thousand and nine.
Light nine lights for the year I turned thirteen, the hinge of the decade, the last before mine.

In two thousand and nine I turned thirteen. The world had broken the year before,
And this was the year it was told the breaking was over, and believed it, and stood.
A child becomes a teenager in a recession the way she learns a language:
By what is not said at the table, and what is not bought, and what is understood.

## The first block

On Saturday the third of January the first block of {{x|Bitcoin}} was made,
And in it {{c|4589|Satoshi Nakamoto}} set that morning's headline from {{x|The Times}} of {{p|London}}:
"Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks."
The line proved the date, and it was the reason, written into the money, a sermon.

On the twelfth the first coins went from {{c|4590|Satoshi}} to {{c|4593|Hal Finney}}.
For a year they were worth nothing, and then a man paid ten thousand for two pizzas.
The money the banks could not bail out had begun,
And the genesis block still carries the headline, for the readers and the teachers.

## The new president

On the twentieth of January {{c|4591|Barack Obama}} was sworn in
Before nearly two million in the cold on the {{x|National Mall}},
The first black president, and the crowd wept, and the world watched with them,
And he inherited an economy losing seven hundred thousand jobs a month, in free fall.

In February he signed the stimulus, nearly eight hundred billion,
And he saved the car companies: {{x|Chrysler}} and {{x|General Motors}} went bankrupt and came back,
And the workers of {{p|Detroit}} kept their jobs, and the factories ran,
And the country climbed out of the hole, and the recovery came on track.

On the ninth of October he was given the {{x|Nobel Peace Prize}}
For the hope he gave the world, for the dignity he brought to the office he held.
He was a good man in the hardest job, with grace and with humor,
And the country he led was better for him, and that is what this book has held.

## The fraud

On the twenty-ninth of June {{c|4594|Bernard Madoff}} was sentenced to a hundred and fifty years.
Sixty-five billion dollars had existed on his statements and nowhere else.
He paid the old investors with the new for decades,
While the regulators were told and did not look, and the fraud rang its bells.

## The sickness

In April a new flu was found in {{p|Mexico}} and {{p|California}}, {{x|H1N1}}.
On the eleventh of June the {{x|World Health Organization}} called it a pandemic, the first in forty years.
It was milder than was feared, and the vaccines were made in months,
And the world learned a little, and forgot the rest, which would cost it tears.

## The villain's gas

On the first of January the villain cut the gas to {{p|Ukraine}},
And in the coldest week of the winter the pipes to {{p|Europe}} went empty through it.
{{p|Bulgaria}} froze, and {{p|Slovakia}} shut its factories,
And the villain showed {{p|Europe}} the hand on the valve, and how he would use it.

On the sixteenth of November {{c|4595|Sergei Magnitsky}},
The lawyer who found the officials' theft of two hundred and thirty million from the state,
Died in a Moscow prison, beaten, denied his medicine.
The {{x|Magnitsky Act}} carries his name: the free world's answer, a few years late.

## The green wave

On the twelfth of June {{p|Iran}} voted, and the regime announced
That {{c|4561|Mahmoud Ahmadinejad}} had won by a landslide before the votes could be counted.
Millions marched in {{p|Tehran}} in green, asking: where is my vote?
And on the twentieth {{c|4596|Neda Agha-Soltan}} was shot in the street, and the people mounted

Her name on their banners, and her death was filmed on a phone and seen by the world within hours.
The protests were crushed by the autumn, but they were not forgotten.
The {{x|Green Movement}} was the first of the uprisings of the phones,
The rehearsal of the {{x|Arab Spring}}, and the regime's hold had begun to be rotten.

## Sri Lanka

On the eighteenth of May the army of {{p|Sri Lanka}} declared the {{x|Tamil Tigers}} destroyed,
After twenty-six years of war, and their leader dead.
In the last months tens of thousands of civilians were killed on a strip of beach,
Shelled in places the government had called safe, and the dead were uncounted, unsaid.

## The river and the ocean

On the fifteenth of January {{c|4597|Chesley Sullenberger}} lost both engines to geese over {{p|New York}},
And put the airliner down on the {{x|Hudson}}, and all a hundred and fifty-five lived.
That is the craft of a pilot: the training, the calm, the river,
And the ferries came to the wings, and the city gave what it had to give.

On the first of June {{x|Air France}} flight 447 fell into the {{x|Atlantic}}
On its way from {{p|Rio de Janeiro}} to {{p|Paris}}, because the instruments froze,
And the pilots, confused, pulled the nose up, and two hundred and twenty-eight died.
The airlines changed the training after, and the next crews knew what the stall shows.

## The meeting that took note

From the seventh to the eighteenth of December, in {{p|Copenhagen}},
The countries of the world met to decide what would follow {{x|Kyoto}}.
On the last night {{c|4598|Obama}} walked into a room where {{p|China}}, {{p|India}}, {{p|Brazil}}, and {{p|South Africa}} met,
And wrote an accord with them: keep the warming below two degrees, and so,

For the first time, the great polluters of the rich world and the rising world
Both said a number. The full meeting took note of it, and did not adopt it.
Six years later in {{p|Paris}} the number became a treaty,
And {{c|4598|Obama}}'s room in {{p|Copenhagen}} was where they first got it.

## The camps begin

On the fifth of July, in {{p|Urumqi}}, the riots between {{x|Uyghur}}s and {{x|Han}}
Killed nearly two hundred, and the {{x|Chinese Communist Party}} cut the province's net for ten months.
In that silence the camps of the next decade began:
The million {{x|Uyghur}}s in the camps, the mosques torn down, the children taken at once.

This book condemns it plainly. It is a crime against a people,
Their faith and their language and their families, behind the wire.
The Party that built the camps is guilty of it,
And the cotton and the solar panels of {{p|Xinjiang}} were made by the forced and the dire.

## The rest of the year

On the first of December the {{x|Treaty of Lisbon}} came into force.
On the twenty-fifth of May {{p|North Korea}} tested its second bomb.
On the twenty-eighth of June the army of {{p|Honduras}} put its president on a plane in his pajamas.
On the fifth of November, at {{p|Fort Hood}}, an army doctor killed thirteen, a jihadist's psalm.

On the twenty-fifth of June {{c|4599|Michael Jackson}} died, and the net slowed under the grief.
On the seventh of March {{x|Kepler}} was launched to look for other earths, and found thousands of worlds.
In February two men from {{x|Yahoo}} started {{x|WhatsApp}},
And in March {{x|Uber}} began, and the cars came to the hand that unfurls.

## The blocks

In May a game of blocks, {{x|Minecraft}}, was put up by {{c|4600|Markus Persson}} in {{p|Sweden}},
And children my age built worlds out of squares for the next decade.
In it a girl of thirteen could build a world alone,
A house, a castle, a city, a machine of redstone, from the first block laid.

I built there. Every child of my year built there.
It taught a generation that a world is made of blocks you place,
And that one person alone can make one.
That is the faith of the builders: a world from nothing, block by block, in its space.

In December {{x|Avatar}} came out, the blue people defending their forest
Against the company that came for what was under it, and it made more than any film before.
The genesis block of the money, the block of the game, and the world of the film:
The year of the blocks, at thirteen, at the hinge, at the decade's door.

## The year held

A block with a headline in it, a president in the cold who carried the country out,
The car companies saved, a fraud of sixty-five billion, a flu that was survived,
The villain's hand on the gas valve and a lawyer dead in his prison,
A green wave crushed that would come again, and geese, and a river where all arrived.

A meeting where the first number was said, the camps begun in the silence of {{p|Xinjiang}},
And a game of blocks where a girl of thirteen could build a world alone.
I built mine. The decade that made me began the next year,
And I carried the blocks into it, and the world I would build was my own.
