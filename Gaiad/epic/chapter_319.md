# Chapter 319: None Against

June twenty-sixth, in nineteen forty-five,
In {{p|San Francisco}}, fifty signed the deed;
{{p|Poland}}, not there, in autumn would arrive,
Counted a founder; and the charter's creed:

Promote respect for human rights; and free
Freedoms, without distinction made, of race,
Sex, tongue, or faith; it did not list; to be
A purpose, not a list; and in its place,

October twenty-fourth, in force; the five
Permanent members, others, ratified;
The body's day; and still, not yet alive,
The articles of right. The work, applied,

The commission to draft them; at its head
{{c|4399|Eleanor Roosevelt}}; {{c|4400|John Humphrey}}'s draft,
The first full one; {{c|4401|René Cassin}} then spread
An order on it; text not one hand's craft;

{{c|4402|Peng Chun Chang}} asked the first article hold
Conscience with reason; brought the {{x|Chinese}} ren,
The regard one has for another; told
In {{x|English}}, conscience. {{c|4403|Hansa Mehta}}, then,

Required: human beings; a draft said men;
It does not say men now. December's day,
In 'forty-eight, {{p|Palais de Chaillot}}, when
In {{p|Paris}}, the tenth, before midnight, they,

The {{x|General Assembly}} there said yes:
Two-seventeen, first part; and thirty, set
Of articles; the first: born free, no less,
Equal in dignity and rights; and, met

With reason and with conscience, should toward each
Act in a brotherhood's spirit; and one
Lets one's belief change. The preamble's speech:
Contempt for rights brought barbarous acts, done

That outraged conscience; no camps named, no place;
Fifty-eight members; forty-eight for; none
Against; and eight held back; in each one's case:
Six were the Soviet side;{{fn|The Soviet Union, Ukraine, Belarus, Poland, Czechoslovakia and Yugoslavia.}} and each one

Held back; {{p|Saudi Arabia}}, which spoke
Against the right to change belief; and, too,
{{p|South Africa}}; {{p|Honduras}}, {{p|Yemen}}, broke
No vote, absent; not every member, through.

{{c|4404|Andrei Vyshinsky}} spoke before; his note:
A right had meaning in a state; and wait
A year, he asked; but forty-five, the vote,
To six, refused; it bore no treaty's weight:

No one signed it; none bound by signing; made,
Adopted; and the binding text came on
Later, written. The charter named, displayed,
Respect; the declaration, two years on.

Hold June, fifty hands, {{p|Poland}} not yet there;
October, force, no list; December's day,
Before midnight; for, forty-eight; and there
None against; eight withheld their yes or nay;

The first article: human beings; there,
The word {{c|4405|Chang}} asked for stands; a page now sets
A person with each other one; but where
Adopted, keeps none living; but it lets

The right be said where states can hear; the dead
Are not its articles; yet not, thereby,
Left out of naming's work. Count what was said:
June, and October, and December's sky.
