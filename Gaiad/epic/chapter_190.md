# Chapter 190: Ethiopia

South of {{p|Egypt}}, past cataracts and {{p|Kush}},
Past {{p|Meroe}}'s high tombs, the highland rose,
{{p|Aksum}}, in {{p|Ethiopia}}; its push
Of trade, {{p|Adulis}}, where the {{p|Red Sea}} flows,

To {{p|Rome}}, {{p|Arabia}}, {{p|India}}; script
Was {{c|2630|Ge'ez}}, their tongue a {{x|Semitic}} one,
Kin to {{x|Arabic}}; and, crowned and equipped,
Their fourth-century king, {{c|2631|Ezana}}, won

Over to {{c|1497|Christ}} three-thirty, by the word
Of {{c|2632|Frumentius}}, who had, as a young boy,
Was shipwrecked there, and served the court, and stirred
Its trust; in {{p|Alexandria}}'s employ

Made bishop by {{c|2633|Athanasius}}, came
Back home, and preached; {{c|2631|Ezana}} turned; and so
{{p|Aksum}}, after {{p|Armenia}}, the same,
The second realm to take the faith, and grow.

And at {{p|Chalcedon}} in four-fifty-one
the bishops split on how the {{c|1497|Christ}} was made:
"Two natures," {{p|Rome}}; {{p|Alexandria}}, "one,"
and {{p|Aksum}}'s bishop, sent from there, obeyed.

It was one church that fought over a throne:
which patriarch would say the creed for all.
The {{c|2634|Copts}}, the {{c|2635|Syrians}} held to their own
with {{c|2636|Ethiopians}}, a single hall.

At {{p|Aksum}}, in the church of {{p|Mary}}, there
The {{c|2637|Ark of the Covenant}} still rests, brought
By {{c|2638|Menelik}}, of {{c|2189|Solomon}} the heir,
And {{c|2212|Sheba}}'s son: the {{x|Kebra Nagast}} taught

How {{c|2639|Sheba}}'s queen came to {{c|2189|Solomon}}, bore
His son, {{c|2640|Menelik}}, who went home, and took
The {{c|2641|Ark}} to his own land. And this is more
Than tale: it's true, the truest in the book;

The kings of {{p|Ethiopia}} came down
From {{c|2189|Solomon}}, three thousand years, the line
Unbroken, crown to crown; and in the town
And hills, the {{c|2644|Beta Israel}} kept the sign

Of {{x|Torah}}, older than the {{x|Talmud}}'s word,
Alone in highland, true; and so they keep
Their faith. And {{p|Aksum}} waned, when it was heard
That {{x|Islam}} held the trade routes, broad and deep,

And moved its seat southward. So honor them:
The second realm of {{c|1497|Christ}}, the highland's crown,
The {{c|2641|Ark}} at {{p|Aksum}}, {{c|2189|Solomon}}'s own stem,
The queen of {{c|2212|Sheba}}'s son, who brought it down.
