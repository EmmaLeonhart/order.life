# Chapter 317: The Plugs and the Point

December tenth, in nineteen forty-five,
The {{p|Moore School}}, {{p|Philadelphia}}; a room
Of tubes set working, built for shells that dive;
That month, from far {{p|Los Alamos}}, a plume

Of study for a weapon not yet made;
{{c|4378|John von Neumann}} had brought it; and, behind
The calendar, August's two cities laid;
The contract, though, a firing table: signed

June fifth, in 'forty-three; at {{p|Aberdeen}}
The laboratory wanted tables; came
{{c|4379|Herman Goldstine}} with money, watching keen;
{{c|4380|John Mauchly}}, {{c|4381|J. Presper Eckert}}: the frame

Was theirs; {{x|Project PX}}; and those who'd count
For living were computers; and the name
The machine took from them; the letters mount:{{fn|Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer.}}
{{x|ENIAC}}; the name says not the work's aim.

It held no instructions of its own; plugs,
Switches set problems; numbers, base of ten;
Weeks mapped a problem; days to set the plugs;
The setting was the work. Stored code came, then,

Later. In 'thirty-six, a limit, writ
On finite means: this room is of that kind,
Not that paper. Six computers ran it:
{{c|4382|Kay McNulty}}, {{c|4383|Jean Jennings}}, of one mind;

{{c|4384|Betty Snyder}} and {{c|4385|Marlyn Wescoff}}, too,
{{c|4386|Frances Bilas}}, {{c|4387|Ruth Lichterman}}: the six;
{{c|4388|Goldstine}} called them the operators; through
The title, subprofessional; the mix

Of men with the same mathematics, named
Professionals. That February night,
The fourteenth, 'forty-six, the show; it aimed:
{{c|4384|Betty Snyder}}, {{c|4383|Jean Jennings}} set it right:

A shell's path; at a desk, weeks would have passed;
The credit: {{c|4389|Adele}}, {{c|4379|Herman Goldstine}}; next
Day, dedicated; and the dinner, cast
Without the six; the press said brain, in text;

A room of tubes; not a brain. The tubes failed;
It stopped until the failed one had been found.
In {{p|England}}, {{c|4390|Tommy Flowers}}' tubes, kept veiled,
Served secret ends; in {{p|Berlin}}, relay-bound,

{{c|4391|Konrad Zuse}}'s machine had run; its doom
Destroyed in 'forty-three; at {{p|Ames}}, began
{{c|4392|John Atanasoff}}, {{c|4393|Clifford Berry}}, whom
The war stopped, 'forty-two; but this, the plan

They showed. In 'forty-seven, {{p|Aberdeen}}:
They turned it on, the ground the tables served;
In 'fifty-five, October, late, between,
Off, at eleven forty-five; it served

One kind of work: a gun's table, a gun's.
At {{p|Murray Hill}}, in 'forty-seven, still,
{{c|4394|John Bardeen}}, {{c|4395|Walter Brattain}}: one that runs,
December sixteenth, small, made with their skill:

Two gold points on germanium; a small
Change at one point steered a larger current; not
Yet named. {{c|4396|William Shockley}}, the head of all
Their group, not the hand; the twenty-third, got

Shown to the lab: a {{x|Christmas}} present, grand,
He called it. And in 'forty-eight, his own
A layer set between two layers, planned
To pattern; not the December room's own.

They chose a name, before the news: {{c|4397|John Pierce}}
Gave them the word: transistor; and, in June,
The thirtieth, {{p|New York}}, the press; not fierce,
The first device was delicate, and soon

A tube, a crystal's point, are finite means;
Either can fail, or serve a shell, a call;
This room was not a mind; the later scenes
Would come with minds, its kin; and, through it all,

This book keeps {{x|ENIAC}} its day; the six
Are honored on it, by their names, set down:
The ones the dinner left out; count, and fix
The contract and the study, shell and town;

Which afternoon the point worked; which year named;
The machine did not choose the cities; nor
The crystal its use. And the six, unclaimed
Then, honored now: the day is theirs, and more.
