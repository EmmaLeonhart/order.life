# Chapter 328: As of 1979

From the eighteenth to twenty-second, late
December, 'seventy-eight, in {{p|Beijing}},
The {{x|Central Committee}}, third session; straight
Eleventh's; {{c|4440|Hua Guofeng}}, chair, in the ring,

Not taken. {{c|4464|Deng Xiaoping}}, vice-chairman, there,
Five days before, the thirteenth, spoke, to find
The mind unbound; seek facts' truth; and declare,
And unity ahead; the speech, not signed.

The communique, twenty-second, said:
The movement to expose and criticize
{{c|4435|Lin Biao}}, the four, in the main, had sped
To victory; the stress of work would rise

Toward modernization, 'seventy-nine;
The party stayed; no vote the country made;
The chairman's title stayed; and the design
Of later tellings: {{c|4464|Deng}} the leader, made

From then. The fifteenth, {{p|Washington}}; next day,
{{p|Beijing}}: that, with the new year, they'd begin
Relations; in 'seventy-nine, a stay:
{{c|4464|Deng Xiaoping}}, vice premier, was welcomed in

At {{p|Washington}} by {{c|4465|Jimmy Carter}}; signed,
The thirty-first, on science, culture, deals;
The visit, to the fourth; and left behind
{{p|Seattle}}, on the fifth; and turning wheels

Of work toward modernization; and there
The chair stayed. {{p|Tehran}}, January's span,
{{c|4466|Mohammad Reza Pahlavi}} went; where
No abdication signed; and, as the plan,

{{c|4467|Shapour Bakhtiar}}, prime minister, stayed;
On February first {{c|4468|Khomeini}} came,
By half past nine; {{x|Air France}}'s charter, made
From {{p|Paris}}; fifteen years of exile; came

Millions to meet him; at the airport, spoke;
Went to {{p|Behesht-e Zahra}}, where the dead
Of revolution lay; eleventh, broke
The government; then March, a vote, instead:

The thirtieth and thirty-first: the poll,
A referendum, the republic named
Islamic; and a name is not the whole
Of every person's will; two winters, framed

Not one event; the session closed no grave;
A campaign ended is no filled one; old,
The faith is older than the vote; that gave
A form of state, which writing does not hold;

{{p|Beijing}}: seek facts, and modernize the work;
{{p|Tehran}}: a people's change, the form they live;
Count December twenty-second, the work,
And February first: two maps, which live.
