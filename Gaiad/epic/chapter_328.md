# Chapter 328: As of 1979

From the eighteenth to the twenty-second of December, 1978,
The {{x|Central Committee}} sat in {{p|Beijing}}.
It was the third session of the eleventh committee.
{{c|4440|Hua Guofeng}} presided.
He was the chairman.
The session did not remove him.
{{c|4464|Deng Xiaoping}} was a vice-chairman.
On the thirteenth, five days before the session opened,
At the close of the working conference, he spoke.
The speech asks for the mind to be unbound,
For truth to be sought from facts,
And for unity in looking to the future.
The speech is not the communique.

The communique was adopted on the twenty-second.
It said the large movement to expose and criticize
{{c|4435|Lin Biao}} and the gang of four
Had in the main been completed,
And it said victoriously.
The word is the communique's.
It is not a count of any grave.
It said the stress of the party's work
Would shift to socialist modernization,
As of 1979.
A shift of the work is not a resignation of the party.
The session was not a vote of the country.
Do not say the chairman's title passed that week.
A later telling calls {{c|4464|Deng Xiaoping}} the leader from this session.
The titles in the communique are the titles of that week.
A later change of chairman is not this December.

On the fifteenth of December, in {{p|Washington}},
And on the sixteenth, in {{p|Beijing}},
The two states announced
That relations would begin on the first of January.
Do not collapse the two dates of the announcement.
On the twenty-ninth of January, 1979,
{{c|4464|Deng Xiaoping}} was received in {{p|Washington}},
As vice premier.
{{c|4465|Jimmy Carter}} welcomed him.
On the thirty-first they signed agreements
On science, and on culture.
A communique dated the official visit through the fourth of February.
He left {{p|Seattle}} on the morning of the fifth.
The visit is not the day the relations began.
An agreement on a science is not a constitution.
Do not say one sentence opened a market.
Do not say the work was only a new name.
The work was turned toward modernization.
The chairman stayed.

On the sixteenth of January, 1979,
{{c|4466|Mohammad Reza Pahlavi}} left {{p|Tehran}}.
No abdication was signed.
{{c|4467|Shapour Bakhtiar}} remained the prime minister.
On the first of February {{c|4468|Ruhollah Khomeini}} landed
At the airport in the city,
At half past nine in the morning, by that city's clock.
The aircraft was a chartered plane of {{x|Air France}}.
It had left {{p|Paris}}.
He had been in exile fifteen years.
Millions came to meet him.
He spoke at the airport.
He went to the cemetery of {{p|Behesht-e Zahra}},
Where people killed in the revolution were buried.
On the eleventh of February the government fell.
A landing is not yet a state.

On the thirtieth and the thirty-first of March
A referendum was held on an Islamic republic.
The percentage that is printed is not used.
A ballot is not the airport.
The name the vote took is a name.
It is not a report that every person agreed.
Do not say the return was the only decision open.
Do not say the king left because a speech was given in {{p|Beijing}}.
The two winters are not one event.

Do not say the session restored a grave.
The communique said a campaign had in the main ended.
An ended campaign is not a filled grave.
Do not say the republic in {{p|Tehran}} is a verdict on a faith.
The faith is older than the vote.
The vote is a form of a state.
A form is not kept by being written.
The striving named in {{p|Beijing}} is that the work
Be modernization, and that a fact be sought.
The striving named in {{p|Tehran}} is that a people
Change the form they live under.
Neither year accomplished the other's sentence.

Hold the session that shifted the work and kept the chairman,
The king who left, the cleric who returned,
And the vote that named a republic.
Refuse the consolation that an opening is a revolution,
And the consolation that a revolution is an opening.
Count the communique on the twenty-second of December.
Count the landing on the first of February.
The map of the work and the map of the state
Are not the same map.
