# Chapter 311: The Appointed Hour

June twenty-eighth, nineteen-fourteen; a car;
{{c|4311|Gavrilo Princip}}, {{p|Sarajevo}}, killed
{{c|4312|Franz Ferdinand}}, the heir, and, in the car,
{{c|4313|Sophie, Duchess of Hohenberg}}, both stilled.

A pistol is no war; the war was set
By cabinets, a month of signing, haste:
On {{p|Serbia}}, an ultimatum; yet
The twenty-eighth declared; no day to waste;

On {{p|Russia}}, August first, {{p|Germany}}; then
The third, on {{p|France}}; the fourth, it {{p|Belgium}} crossed;
{{p|Britain}}'s note ran out at eleven, when
Unanswered: war; empire, at that cost.

On August's twenty-third, {{p|Japan}} declared
On {{p|Germany}}; the rescript, in the name
Of {{c|4314|Yoshihito}}, said he'd hoped, and cared
To keep the {{p|East}}'s peace; mourned his mother; came

An ultimatum, sent mid-month, unheard;
True to its pact with {{p|Britain}}, twelve years, went
To do its part, its promise kept; the word:
{{p|Qingdao}}: the {{x|German}} base, where raiders went,

On {{p|Shandong}}'s coast, to sea; a force took hold,
The {{x|Japanese}}, with {{x|British}} at its side;
November's seventh, the garrison, told,
Gave up; this book's two anchors stood allied,

In the same war, on one side, as they'd sworn.
In {{p|Europe}}, killing did not pause; graves laid
Where lines lay, by the next fight overborne,
Marks gone. {{c|4315|Fabian Ware}}, forty-five, stayed,

Too old for the army, in {{p|France}}, that fall,
A {{x|British Red Cross}} mobile unit's head;
He wrote the graves it found down, one and all;
In 'fifteen, the army took it; it spread:

The {{x|Graves Registration Commission}}; then,
'Sixteen, wider: a directorate; came{{fn|In 1916 it became the Directorate of Grave Registration and Enquiries.}}
The dead were filed while being made, as men
Were falling. Then, May twenty-first, the name,

In 'seventeen, a royal charter: and
{{x|Imperial War Graves Commission}}, bound
To graves of empire's dead; their care, planned
To last; the missing's names; and on the ground

The {{c|3373|Prince of Wales}} presided; {{c|4316|Ware}}, vice-chair;
{{c|4317|Frederic Kenyon}}, {{x|British Museum}}'s head,
Asked, in November, for a rule, laid there
Equality: the same stone for each dead,

Private and officer; no private stone
As rule; he signed, January, 'eighteen;
Published, November; architects, made known:
{{c|4318|Edwin Lutyens}}, {{c|4319|Herbert Baker}}, and seen

With {{c|4320|Reginald Blomfield}}; the stones unseen,
The rule was written. {{c|4321|Rudyard Kipling}} gave
The words for nameless, cut on stone between:
Known unto God. His son was in no grave:

{{c|4322|John Kipling}}, missing since September, slain
At {{p|Loos}}, 'fifteen. Empire's rule, a choice;
In nineteen-twenty, families, though, in vain,
Asked {{x|Parliament}} to bring them home; their voice

Was heard; but the commission held the ground,
One form, permanent. And the stopping, too,
A choice, and timed. At {{p|Compiègne}}, around
The forest's railway carriage, as dawn grew,

November's eleventh, in 'eighteen, signed
The armistice: {{c|4323|Ferdinand Foch}} who led
{{c|4324|Allies}}; {{c|4325|Matthias Erzberger}}, assigned
To lead the {{c|2669|Germans}}; and the terms had said:

Land, air, would cease six hours on, by clock;
The government told {{x|Parliament}}: at five
Signed; at eleven, the war ceased. The lock
Of orders ran on both sides; and, alive

Till then, a line that had a clock, no peace.
The hour was appointed. {{c|4326|Princip}} shot;
{{c|4327|Sophie}} died with the heir; then, piece by piece,
Others signed declarations; the end, bought

By an hour too, as debts are paid. Begun
Years earlier, the register: the ground
Kept not what it was given; {{c|4316|Ware}}'s file, one
Refused the next attack's erasing round;

Count the named; cut one stone for private, rank;
Keep the missing as missing: a name too.
And keep the day both ways: grief, for the blank
Millions in the ground; joy: guns quiet, too,

At eleven; the bells, the crowds, the thanks.
Eleventh hour, eleventh day: as one,
A mourning and a feast at once; the ranks
Known unto God: a sentence where the one

Body lay in the field. The day keeps, too,
The hour appointed, and the names it gave
Back to no one; the living, who came through,
Danced and lived, ancestors now, glad and brave.
