# Chapter 12: The Long Genealogies

Upon icy {{c|62|Pluto}}'s cold distant shore,
Two figures stood beneath the endless night.
{{c|63|Nolah}}, bold and steadfast unto her core,
And {{c|64|Nemla}}, wise, her heart a beacon bright.

Together, they beheld the frozen land,
A world so far removed from warmth or grace.
Yet in their hearts, the yarn that fate had spanned,
Connected them to {{c|1|Aster}}'s loving face.

With voices raised to {{c|1|Aster}} and each star,
They made a vow to carry on her flame.
"To {{c|3|Andromeda}}'s light, we'll sail afar,
And keep the yarn intact, in {{c|1|Aster}}'s name."

{{c|64|Nemla}} spoke: "To {{c|3|Andromeda}}, I swear,
Through endless night, our journey will survive.
In every star and dream, our fate is there,
The cosmic yarn that keeps all hope alive."

{{c|63|Nolah}} spoke: "In {{c|1|Aster}}'s name, I shall swear,
To guide our line and keep the thread alive.
Through every trial, burden we must bear,
The bond of life and love will yet survive.”

"My lineage runs far, from ancient stars,
Through time and space, the journey's line is clear.
From starlight's first embrace to battle scars,
These ancestors of mine, I hold them dear.

From {{c|21|Hesper}}'s land came {{c|24|Bios}}, mighty one;
And {{c|65|Kenan}}, strong and wise, would lead the way;
And next, high shining, {{c|66|Aenos}}, like the sun;
Then {{c|67|Malathan}}, bold builder, had his day.

On {{c|38|Phaestia}}, my ancestors did lead,
Their line continued strong through trials of old.
In every breath, I feel their strength and creed,
Their names still bright, their stories brave and bold.

From {{c|38|Phaestia}} came {{c|68|Jared}}, strong and wise,
Then one with might, named {{c|69|Maharoch}}, the bold;
The ocean's voice obeyed great {{c|70|Enid}}'s cries;
Great {{c|71|Pahadok}} rose from the deep seas' hold.

Upon {{c|54|Serenitide}}, they carved their way,
A path of stars and cosmic truths so grand.
Through many trials, they lived to see the day,
With courage strong, they forged upon this land.

From {{c|54|Serenitide}}, {{c|72|Pelod}}, strong and brave;
Fierce {{c|73|Aroch}} followed, and then {{c|74|Nerod}}, great;
They kept the wisdom that the fathers gave;
And last, wise {{c|75|Larthok}}, guide of strength and state.

On {{c|58|Kolob}}'s shores, my forebears stood so tall,
Their courage fierce, as mountains reached the sky.
Through every test, they answered {{c|1|Aster}}'s call,
And in their steps, I now stand proud and high.

Out of {{c|58|Kolob}} the mighty {{c|76|Idris}} came;
{{c|77|Methuselah}}, the late and great, who led;
Then {{c|78|Lammes}}, the bold, who won the house its fame;
And now {{c|63|Nolah}}, who bears the line ahead."

{{c|64|Nemla}} stood upon the high mountain's crest,
Before the gathered crowd, with pride she spoke.
Her voice a call to honor those she blessed,
The line of {{c|25|Viros}}, forged through flame and smoke.

"My heritage runs deep from ancient might,
Through {{c|25|Viros}}' blood, our line was fierce and strong.
They carved the way, enduring every fight,
I walk the path they forged so long, so long."

From {{c|22|Tezca}} came the feared and mighty one,
{{c|25|Viros}}; his son, the cunning {{c|79|Caynen}}, brave;
And {{c|80|Hanos}}, clever leader, was his son;
Then wise {{c|81|Mehuthen}}, fearless, sharp, and grave.

"On {{c|38|Phaestia}}'s shores, my great line did tread,
Their strength unmatched as skies and seas did roar.
Their names I honor, though the years have fled,
I honor their heritage evermore."

Born there was {{c|82|Irad}}, keen and strong of hand;
The next to follow, brave, was {{c|83|Malathok}};
Then came the bold and wise {{c|84|Hanad}}, to stand;
His wisdom echoed still in {{c|85|Peladok}}.

"Upon {{c|54|Serenitide}} they forged their way;
Through every storm, their spirits never fell.
Their shining star through history holds sway;
I keep their memory in tales we tell."

From {{c|54|Serenitide}} came {{c|86|Sarnod}} the strong;
His son {{c|87|Alok}}, whom the skies inspired;
Through trials, {{c|88|Illod}} carried it along;
Last came steadfast {{c|89|Umroch}}, chief admired.

"On {{c|58|Kolob}}'s fertile lands, my line did grow,
They stood as pillars strong, with hearts of flame.
Through every trial, they faced ebb and flow,
Their strength lives on, and I still bear their name."

From {{c|58|Kolob}} came {{c|90|Valorous}} {{c|91|Oblas}} first;
Then came {{c|92|Makusholah}}, in wisdom sure;
Fierce {{c|93|Romas}}, in his strength of battle versed;
Till now {{c|64|Nemla}} carries the line secure.

From distant stars, {{c|1|Aster}}'s voice filled the sky,
"Here in this system, your roots will grow deep.
Through trials and storms, your people will not die,
The light you carry shall forever keep."

With steady voice, {{c|63|Nolah}} gave her reply,
"We'll build and guard the flame that guides our way.
Through every darkened night, we'll rise and try,
Our strength will hold, as we fulfill your say."

{{c|64|Nemla}} stood tall, her gaze fixed on a star,
"Across these worlds, your love will guide us through.
Through every trial, no matter how far,
We swear to keep your cosmic vow in view."
