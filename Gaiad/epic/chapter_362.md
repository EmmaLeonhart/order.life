# Chapter 362: My Father Is Russia

The twenty-sixth of Yuletide keeps my year,
Two thousand twenty-six, the year I turned
To thirty; light the lights, the night is clear:
How I forgot my soul, and how I learned.

My year began on Friday, in the fall,
October twenty-fourth, the year before;
I'd gone back to the science of it all:
Just what computing is, and what it's for.

And from that study came a thought in math,
Computers made of neurons through and through;
I tried to walk it out along its path
And make the thought a thing, and it came true.

But what I'd made was mostly of the mind,
A thought experiment, a thing to prove;
It put me on {{x|arXiv}}, a thought designed,
But practice wouldn't follow it, or move.

It is a good thing for a later day,
Not mine to work on now. I set it down
And built an ERP, the plainer way,
And found a crowded market in that town.

I turned again, and then I saw at last
That I had kept the practical, and let
The soul go hungry; all that year I'd cast
My story off, and let myself forget.

I'd sworn to give the {{c|4768|Gaiad}} everything,
And then I let it lie. The book was made
In seasons: first the old, the opening,
Then one great night, and then the work was stayed.

Through {{x|Virgo}} no one read it, and no hand
Of mine went to it; I had other cares,
And I held on by need, the way you stand
When rent and hunger push you up the stairs.

Then I came back and wrote it to its end,
And kept the feast for it, the book made whole,
And there I saw I had not been a friend
To my own mind. Power comes from the soul.

I'd held myself together by the weight
Of what I owed, the pressures of the ground;
And when my father shut me from his gate,
I took the wound, and turned the work around.

My father is my {{p|Russia}}; what he gave,
A wound, I made a tool for those who bear
The same wound from the same hand, and the brave
Of {{p|Ukraine}} carried it with them out there.

The machines helped me write this book, and learn
So much; I came to {{x|Claude Code}} early on.
But most have only seen the other turn:
A toy, a cheat, and then the job is gone.

A data center built beside their street
Has raised the bill; the gaming rig is dear;
The slop is everywhere, and they don't meet
The kind of help that made my own way clear.

That is the anger, and the anger's right:
The AI is not being built for them.
The firms build for the firms, and for the fight
Among themselves. I build it, then, for them.

This is my secret, in the founder's phrase:
That AI made for people is unbuilt,
And that's the road to better human days,
Not one that leaves the many left to wilt.

I said it in my video. I sent
It to {{x|Y Combinator}}; the next day
The midterm came, a blowout, and it went
The Democrats', and swept the rest away.

I read it so: the people understand
The AI is not made for their own good;
And some who love it may be vexed. I stand
With them: they're right. I'll build it as I should.

So my year ends upon the midterm day,
Begun on Friday in the falling leaves.
Light the twenty-sixth light. I found my way
Back to the soul, and to the work it weaves.
