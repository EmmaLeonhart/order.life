# Chapter 303: The Short Plant Returns

In the abbey garden at {{p|Brno}}, {{c|Gregor Mendel}} counted peas.
He had come from {{p|Hynčice}} as {{c|Johann}}, a farmer's son,
And taken the name Gregor with the habit. On his knees
In the rows of the Augustinians, year after year, he was one

Man with a brush and a tally. Seven differences, kept apart:
The seed yellow or green, the seed round or wrinkled, the stem
Tall or short, the flower purple or white, the pod's colour, the chart
Of where the blossom sits, the pod full or pinched. He did not hem

A theory in words and then go looking. He crossed them by hand.
A tall with a short gave him tall children. He let those breed,
And the short came back, about one in four, and the stand
Of the tall ones was two kinds of tall: one that would indeed

Breed true, and one that carried a shortness it did not show.
He called the trait that shows itself dominating, and the trait
That waits, recessive. A thing can leave the visible, and go
Under for a generation, and return, whole, not an approximate.

He said it in {{p|Brno}} on the eighth of February, 1865,
And again on the eighth of March, to the naturalists' society.
The next year they printed it: *Experiments on Plant Hybrids*.
He sent the paper out. The century did not take the variety

Of what he had counted. He wrote to {{c|Carl Nägeli}}, and Nägeli
Pointed him to hawkweed, and hawkweed sets its seed alone,
Without the cross. The ratios would not come. The tally
Of the peas had been the truth, and the next plant overthrew the tone

Of an easy repetition. In 1868 the house made him abbot.
The counting stopped. There was a monastery to run, and a fight
With the tax, and the garden still outside, and the work not
Picked up. He died in 1884. Sixteen years later the light

Came from other gardens: men counting other plants found
His paper, and found they had repeated him. The peas had waited
As the recessive waits. That is 1866. Hold the ground
Of it: inheritance can hide, and what hides is not vacated.

Three years after the paper, in {{p|London}}, a second book.
{{c|Francis Galton}}, born the same year as the friar, cousin
To {{c|Charles Darwin}} through {{c|Erasmus Darwin}}, undertook
To show that genius runs in families. He had been

Through the directories of reputation: judges, commanders,
Men of science, the names a nation keeps because they are known.
The eminent had eminent kin, past what chance would hand us.
So, he said, ability is inherited, and a people is grown

By which of its stocks it lets continue. *Hereditary Genius*,
Macmillan, 1869. He did not yet say eugenics.
That word is his, in 1883, from the Greek for well-born,
A name for arranging births. The earlier book already has the premises:

Let the eminent marry young. Discourage the rest.
The fame of a house becomes a right over the unborn.
{{c|Charles Darwin}}, at {{p|Downe}}, who had printed the whole tree, blessed
The book with admiration. The man of the one descent turned

Warmly toward the man who had begun to prune it.
Hold both facts. The counting, in both gardens, was real.
Mendel saw a hidden factor survive intact, and knew it
By the ratio. Galton saw a distribution, and the appeal

He made of it was a policy. One of them described
How a trait goes on. The other made a mission of which
Traits, which families, which nations, ought to be the tribe
That continues. From a list of the famous he drew a switch

For other people's children. People had already made
A joke of apes out of the book of the tree. This was quieter,
And it was worse: not a cartoon of a body, a grade
Of the living, with the future as the prize for the higher.

This calendar stands with the peas, and then it goes further
Than the peas. The short plant is not a defect that returned
By accident. It is the shape of a record. What you defer,
What no directory entered, what the visible generation spurned,

Is not gone. It is recessive to the page, and it comes back
Entire in some child who will never be eminent, and in some
Who will, and the eminence is not the test. The lack
In Galton's list is the list. It can only see the sum

Of reputations. An ancestor day is the other eye:
Everyone who lived, in the line of the living, or in the living
World beside them. Not the branch that learned how to apply
For continuance. Chapter 302 still stands. The giving

Of the future to the already famous is not descent.
Descent is the hidden returning whole. Count that.
Do not appoint a survival. The abbey garden went
On its knees with a brush. The other book knelt to a table

Of the renowned, and called the table a care for the stock.
Refuse the care. It is a hand on a lock that is not yours.
Take the ratio. One in four, the hidden, entire. The clock
Of a life is not a prize list. Keep the ones the directories ignore.

They are in the seed. They return. Let them.

Stand.
