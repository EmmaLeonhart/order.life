# Chapter 303: The Short Plant Returns

In {{p|Brno}}'s garden, {{c|4241|Gregor Mendel}}, there,
And counted peas; from far {{p|Hynčice}} had come,
As {{c|4242|Johann}}, farmer's son; {{c|4243|Gregor}}, the wear
The habit; {{c|3753|Augustinians}}'; and some

Of years with brush and tally: seven traits
Kept each apart: the seed, yellow or green;
Round, wrinkled; stem, tall, short; the flower's states,
Purple or white; the pod's colour; between

The stem, where blossoms sit; the pod, full, pinched;
He crossed by hand, no theory first: and tall
With short gave tall; he let them breed; it clinched:
The short came back, one in four; and of all

The tall, two kinds: one true, and one that, in
Its seed, held shortness that it did not show;
He named it: dominant, recessive; kin
Can go from sight a generation, flow

Beneath, as rivers under ground, and come
Back whole, not mixed. He said it, plain and grave,
In February and in March; and some
The *{{x|Experiments on Plant Hybrids}}* gave

In print; he wrote {{c|4244|Carl Nägeli}}; and then
{{c|4245|Nägeli}} sent him to the hawkweed's run,
In setting seed; no ratios; and when
In sixty-eight made abbot; rows were done;

He fought a tax upon the house; and died
In eighty-four; and, sixteen years on, men
In other gardens, counting, found it, wide,
His peers; the peas had waited, as, again,

The hidden waits: descent's own sacred sign,
Inheritance can hide, and what hides stays;
The hidden, whole, returning, in the line,
Kept in the seed through all its unseen days;

Three years on, in {{p|London}}, a second book:
{{c|4246|Francis Galton}}, a cousin, born the same
Year, {{c|4232|Darwin}}'s kin through {{c|4098|Erasmus}}; he took
To show that genius runs in houses; fame

Directories: judges, commanders, known
Men of science, the names a nation keeps;
The eminent had eminent kin; grown
By which stocks last, a people's future reaps;

*{{x|Hereditary Genius}}*, sixty-nine;
The name, in eighty-three: eugenics, well
Born, from the {{x|Greek}}; and {{c|4232|Charles Darwin}}, in line,
At {{p|Downe}}, with {{c|4240|Emma Darwin}} reading, fell

To fifty pages, wrote he'd never read
So new a thing; and {{c|4248|Galton}} counted too;
And what he saw was there: the great lines led
The story: famous houses' heirs, as true;

This book keeps those: through kings, the named, renowned;
But {{c|4248|Galton}}'s list saw only fame; and what
{{c|4247|Mendel}} had counted, gone from sight, comes round,
Returning whole, in the unknown, the shut

And underground of each house; both are kept:
The eminent and the unentered; crown
And short plant; famous lines, the story swept;
The hidden, all the rest; the up, the down;

An ancestor day sees both, high and low,
The line, the living world; and the test, too,
Of descent: not a prize list, nor, below,
The ignored; the whole seed; one in four, true,

The hidden whole; the great, the small; and, scored
Together, abbey garden, knees, and brush,
The honoured's book, one tree; in seed, restored,
They come back; keep them all, in the seed's hush.
