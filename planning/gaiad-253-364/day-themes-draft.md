# Day-by-day themes, Virgo to Ophiuchus (draft, 2026-10-12)

A theme for every day of the four modern months, drafted with Emma in chat on the night of
2026-10-11/12. **A complete draft on purpose**, in her words: "just this thing being out there as a
thing ... makes it so that it's, in a sense, citable ... a thing that I can use next year or somebody
could read through and say, hey, I don't think that this works very well ... But it does kind of have
to be complete." This overrides the folder README's "not a chronologically grid-filled plan" for this
file only.

**Status, her words:** Virgo, Libra and Scorpius: "I am supportive of every single one of these
things." Ophiuchus: "the 21st century needs a bit of a redo", because the first version had blanks
and leaned on her personal arc, which "wasn't as well documented in there as I think you thought it
was". The Ophiuchus below is that redo and has **not** been read by her yet.

The division is hers, 2026-10-10 (`../gaiad-inochi-themes.md`): Virgo 1500s and 1600s, Libra 1700s
and 1800s, Scorpius 1900s, Ophiuchus 2000s. Leo already covers the early-modern Americas. Hard
anchors are kept: Virgo 1 (ch 253) is the Chronicle of the Popes; Ophiuchus 26 to 28 (ch 362 to 364)
are the time skip, the cryonic revival and Milkomeda.
